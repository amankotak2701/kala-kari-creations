Meet the founders

Kala Kari Creations began in our home in Ahmedabad, with a mother and son who shared one dream.

We are Aman Hiren Kotak and Sonal Kotak, a son and his mother. Two months ago, we turned years of kitchen-table conversations into something real and started Kala Kari Creations from our home in Ahmedabad.

Two journeys, one dream

Aman studied Business Administration and specialised in Marketing. From early on he knew he wanted to build a brand of his own, not just work for one.

Sonal studied Commerce and spent years running different businesses alongside Aman's father, Hiren Kotak. Later she gave her time to the family as a homemaker, but the entrepreneur in her never went quiet.

Between those two journeys, a shared dream took shape: to start a business of our own, build it with our own hands, and grow it together.

How we work, together

Kala Kari is a true mother-and-son partnership, and we do things the personal way:

  • We meet our artisans ourselves. We both travel to buy every piece in person. We sit with the artisans, talk to them about their craft and choose each design by hand.
  • We plan side by side. Every business decision, from what to stock to how we grow, is something we work out together.
  • We share the work. We've divided our responsibilities so each of us leads where we're strongest, and our easy rapport keeps it all joyful.
  • We love meeting you. You'll find us at live exhibitions, showing our collection in person, and on our social media, where we share every new piece.

Where we're going

What started in our home now has a bigger goal: to reach women and girls across India, wherever you live, and bring handcrafted Chikankari, Jaipuri hand-block and Kutchi pieces to your doorstep through this website.

Every piece you choose supports the artisans we work with, and the small family business behind it. Thank you for being part of our story.

Tradition, Beautifully Worn.

Kala Kari means the art of the hand. It is the name we chose because everything we make begins and ends with one: the hand that sketches a motif, carves a wooden block, guides a needle through fine mul, or presses colour onto cloth.

Three crafts, one house

Every Kala Kari piece begins in one of three craft homes of India:

  • Lucknow: Chikankari (चिकनकारी). Delicate shadow-work and floral needlework, stitched by hand on soft, breathable fabric.
  • Jaipur: Hand-block printing (ठप्पा छपाई). Hand-carved wooden blocks pressed onto cotton, one impression at a time.
  • Kutch: Kutchi craft (कच्छी कारीगरी). Ajrakh, patchwork and mirror work in bold, earthy colour from the Rann of Kutch.

How we work

We work closely with artisans, in small batches, on natural fabrics like cotton, mul and silk. There is no mass production and no rushing. That is why no two pieces are ever exactly alike: a slightly shifted block, a stitch that leans its own way. These are the signatures of the maker, and we celebrate them.

Why we do it

India's textile crafts are carried by people, not machines. Every time you choose a handcrafted piece, you help keep a skill alive and a family of artisans at work. We want these crafts to be worn every day, not just saved for special occasions, so we design pieces that feel easy, comfortable and modern while staying true to their roots.

From the artisan's courtyard to your wardrobe: thank you for being part of the story.
