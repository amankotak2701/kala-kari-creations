# AI model photoshoot prompts — tunics & shrug

How to use: open an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo (e.g. Google Gemini, ChatGPT,
or a fashion "virtual try-on" app). Upload the product photo, paste the prompt, generate 4–6
options, and keep only the ones where the garment matches the real piece exactly.

Common rules (already included in each prompt):
- Keep the garment 100% identical: colour, motifs, placement, neckline, sleeves, buttons, length.
- No other brand names or text anywhere in the scene.
- Portrait 4:5 (e.g. 1600×2000 px), full-length or knee-up, garment sharp and centred.

Check before uploading to Shopify: motif count/placement, button count, sleeve length and cuff,
neckline shape, colour shade. If anything differs, regenerate — customers buy what they see.
