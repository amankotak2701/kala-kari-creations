## Shipping policy

Kala Kari Creations ships across India from Ahmedabad, Gujarat, through our shipping partner Shiprocket and its network of courier companies.

**Dispatch time** — Every piece is handcrafted and checked before it leaves us. Orders are dispatched within 1–3 business days of being placed (Monday to Saturday, excluding public holidays).

**Estimated delivery time after dispatch**
- Ahmedabad and the rest of Gujarat: 2–4 business days
- Metro cities (Mumbai, Delhi NCR, Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Pune): 3–5 business days
- Rest of India: 4–7 business days
- North-East states, Jammu & Kashmir, Ladakh, Andaman & Nicobar and remote pin codes: 7–10 business days

Most orders reach you within 3–10 business days of ordering. These are estimates from our courier partners, not guarantees; actual delivery can vary by pin code and courier.

**Payment** — All orders are prepaid. We do not offer Cash on Delivery (COD).

**Tracking** — As soon as your order is dispatched, we email you the courier name and tracking link. Any applicable shipping charges are shown at checkout before you pay.

**Delivery address** — Please make sure your shipping address, pin code and phone number are complete and correct. We are not responsible for delays or non-delivery caused by an incorrect or incomplete address.

**Delays** — Courier delays, weather, festivals, sale seasons or other events outside our control can occasionally affect delivery times. If your order seems delayed, contact us and we will follow up with the courier for you.
