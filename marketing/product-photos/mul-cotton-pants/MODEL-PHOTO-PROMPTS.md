# Mul cotton pants: model photoshoot prompts

There are 9 prompts, 3 per colour (white, maroon, black). Use them in an AI image tool that
accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini (the tool your reel photos appear to come from) or
ChatGPT.

**How to use:**
1. Open a new chat and upload the studio photo of that colour from this folder
   (e.g. `mul-cotton-pants-white-studio.jpg`).
2. Paste the **Base instruction** and then **one** shot prompt below.
3. Check the result: the pants should look like your real product (same colour, straight wide
   leg, elastic waist, ankle length, soft crinkled mul texture). If anything differs, reply
   "keep the pants exactly like the reference photo" and regenerate.
4. Ask for portrait 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Base instruction (paste before every shot prompt)

> Create a photorealistic professional fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted
> clothing brand Kala Kari Creations. The model is wearing the pants shown in the reference
> image. Reproduce the pants exactly: same colour, soft lightweight mul cotton with a natural
> gentle crinkle, an elastic gathered waistband, a relaxed straight wide-leg fit, ankle length.
> Do not add pockets, prints, pleats, slits or embroidery to the pants. No clips, hangers or
> tags. The model is a young Indian woman with a natural, warm look, light makeup and minimal
> silver jewellery. The image should look like a high-end shoot: shot on a full-frame camera with
> an 85mm lens, soft natural light, true-to-life colour, sharp focus on the garment. No text,
> no logos, no watermark.
