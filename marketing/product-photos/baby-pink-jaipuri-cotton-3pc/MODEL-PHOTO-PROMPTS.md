# Baby pink Jaipuri cotton 3-piece set: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a baby-pink Jaipuri cotton gathered A-line (nayra-style) kurta with:
- a hand-embroidered yoke of mirrored butterfly-and-lotus motifs in peach, coral, magenta, lilac
  and sage;

It comes with matching pants and a pink mulmul dupatta with scattered star bootis.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the photo of the model holding the dupatta open against the white
   curtain.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:** a soft **baby / candy pink** (about #ED999F), a warm light pink. It isn't hot
     pink, peach or lilac. All three pieces are the same pink.
   - **Neck:** a round neck with a short V-notch. No buttons.
   - **Yoke:** a rectangular yoke from the shoulders to just under the bust, framed on each side by
     a fine darker-pink piping line that continues down the skirt as a seam line.
   - **Yoke embroidery:** a **mirror-symmetric** design (the left half mirrors the right):
     - large butterfly-and-lotus motifs with fanned, veined petals/wings in **peach, coral and
       magenta**, with a touch of **sage green** and **lilac**;
     - fine lilac leafy sprigs between them;
     - a scatter of small magenta dots around the neck notch;
     - a few tiny yellow star flowers at the edges.
     It's thread work in long-and-short and running stitches, outlined in darker pink.
   - **Skirt:** gathered at the empire line into a soft, flowing A-line to mid-calf, scattered with
     **tiny six-petal star bootis**, alternating **yellow-gold** and **magenta**, spaced well apart.
   - **Sleeves:** three-quarter, each with an embroidered band near the cuff of the same
     butterfly-lotus motifs and yellow flowers.
   - **Pants:** pink, straight, ankle length.
   - **Dupatta:** soft pink mulmul with the same tiny yellow and magenta star bootis scattered
     across it, and small tassels at the corners. No border.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the set exactly like the reference photo: soft baby pink,
   a notched neck, a symmetric yoke of peach, coral and magenta butterfly-lotus embroidery with lilac
   sprigs and magenta dots, piping down the yoke sides, a gathered skirt with tiny yellow and
   magenta star bootis, embroidered cuffs, pink pants, and a pink dupatta with star bootis."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a white curtain backdrop with a jute rug, so these use other dreamy,
aesthetic settings. Soft whites, creams and greens keep the pink fresh and true.

**1 · White haveli courtyard with roses (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a sunlit white haveli courtyard with carved
> arches, a small marble fountain and pots of pink and white roses. The dupatta is draped over one
> shoulder, and one hand lightly holds out the skirt to show its flare. The embroidered yoke, the
> piping lines, the star bootis and the pants are clearly visible. Soft, warm late-afternoon light.

**2 · Twirl in a garden (movement)**
> The model spins on a lawn in a garden with flowering trees, the gathered skirt flaring into a
> circle that shows the scattered star bootis, the dupatta floating behind her, and a joyful laugh.
> Slight motion in the fabric, but her face and the yoke stay sharp. Soft, golden light. Full length.

**3 · Dupatta held open (your reference pose)**
> Full-length front view against a warm cream lime-plaster wall. The model holds the dupatta open
> out to one side with her arm stretched and the skirt held out with the other hand, so the dupatta's
> star bootis and the skirt's flare are visible. Soft, even daylight.

**4 · Cherry-blossom-style flowering tree**
> The model stands beneath a tree in full pink blossom, with petals drifting around her, looking up
> with a dreamy smile. Soft, diffused spring light. Full length visible.

**5 · Pastel café with cane chairs**
> The model sits at a small marble table in a pastel café with cane chairs, a pink-and-white
> striped awning outside and a slice of cake in front of her, laughing. The yoke embroidery faces the
> camera. Bright, airy daylight. Captures her from head to ankle.

**6 · Cream limewash wall with dried flowers**
> Full-length front view against a warm cream limewash wall, with a tall ceramic vase of dried
> pampas grass and a cane stool. She tucks her hair behind one ear with a gentle smile. Soft,
> diffused daylight. Clean, Pinterest-style aesthetic.

**7 · Garden swing**
> The model sits on a wooden swing hung from a tree in a lush garden with white flowers, smiling,
> the skirt spread over the seat and the dupatta draped over her arms. Soft, dappled afternoon light.
> Captures her from head to ankle.

**8 · Diwali evening with diyas (festive)**
> Full-length front view at dusk on a terrace lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy lights,
> with a pink-and-white flower rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both hands at waist
> height. The warm glow keeps the pink soft and the embroidery visible.

**9 · Detail close-up, embroidered yoke**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The notched neckline, the magenta dots around it, and the symmetric
> butterfly-and-lotus embroidery in peach, coral, magenta, sage and lilac with its darker-pink
> outlines are in crisp focus, with the piping line at the yoke edge. Soft side light shows the
> thread texture. Shallow depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, cuff and star bootis**
> A close-up of the model's arm resting at her side. It shows the embroidered cuff band with its
> butterfly motif and yellow flowers, and the gathered skirt behind it with its tiny yellow and
> magenta star bootis. Soft daylight, true colour.

**11 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact 3-piece set on a soft ivory linen cloth. The kurta is laid flat
> with the skirt fanned out, showing the yoke, the piping lines, the bootis and the cuffs, with the
> pants folded to one side and the dupatta folded in soft pleats to the other with its bootis and
> tassels showing. Styled with pink roses and small gold studs. Even, soft daylight. Catalogue
> quality.

**12 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera under a flowering
> tree, smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as clean, softly blurred blossoms and sky with no
> objects, so text can be added later. Soft light, true baby pink, yoke clearly visible.
