# Denim embroidered co-ord set: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a matching indigo denim co-ord set. Tunic: mandarin collar, three fabric knot buttons, an offset
L-shaped panel seam, a large white paisley-and-leaf embroidery down the front and embroidered sleeve borders.
Pants: matching denim, elastic waist, straight wide leg, ankle length, with rows of white topstitching at the hem.

## Before you start
1. Upload the photos in one Gemini chat: the two hanger shots of the tunic and the three sofa shots that show the
   pants. Add: "Ignore the sofa, hanger and tags."
2. Paste the Base instruction, then one shot prompt per message. Ask for 4:5 (posts) or 9:16 (Reels/Stories).
3. If something is off, reply: "Keep the co-ord set exactly like the reference photos: same indigo denim for
   both pieces, mandarin collar, three knot buttons, L-shaped seam, white paisley embroidery, embroidered cuffs,
   and matching straight pants with white topstitched hem rows."
4. Turn on Instagram's AI label if asked.

## Base instruction
> Create a photorealistic professional fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala Kari
> Creations. The model wears the women's two-piece denim co-ord set shown in the reference photos. Ignore the sofa,
> hanger and tags. Reproduce both pieces exactly, in the same fabric and colour:
> - **Fabric and colour:** soft, lightweight mid-indigo denim with a natural faded wash, identical for top and pants.
> - **Tunic neck:** a mandarin (band) collar with a short front placket closed by three round fabric knot buttons.
> - **Tunic front seam:** below the placket, a stitched seam turns left and runs straight down, making an offset
>   L-shaped panel.
> - **Tunic embroidery:** next to that seam, a large vertical motif of white and pale-blue thread embroidery with a
>   thin brown outline: a paisley at the top, curling scrolls and leaves in the middle and rounded petals near the
>   hem, with a fine mesh (jaali) fill.
> - **Tunic sleeves and fit:** three-quarter sleeves ending in a border of white looped scroll embroidery with brown
>   accents. Straight, gently A-line, hip length, worn untucked.
> - **Pants:** matching denim pants with a fully elasticated gathered waist, a relaxed straight wide leg falling to
>   the ankle, and three rows of white topstitching around each hem.
> Model: a graceful Indian woman in her mid-20s, natural makeup, small oxidised silver jhumkas, hair in soft waves.
> Show the full matching set. Real fabric texture, true-to-life colour, sharp focus on the outfit. No logos, text or
> watermark.

## Shot 1: Hero, full length
> Full-length front view showing the whole co-ord set from collar to the hem rows of the pants, one hand in her
> pocket, a soft smile. A sunlit whitewashed courtyard with an arched wooden door, terracotta pots and a
> bougainvillea vine. Golden-hour light, tan kolhapuri sandals.

## Shot 2: Walking, street style
> Full-length walking shot on a quiet tree-lined street with pastel heritage buildings, holding a jute tote, white
> sneakers. The wide-leg pants moving with the step. Candid, breezy, modern-ethnic look.

## Shot 3: Café, casual chic
> Seated at a marble café table with a cold coffee, legs crossed so the pant hem stitching shows, cane chairs and
> green plants by a large window. Collar, buttons and embroidery clearly visible. Warm natural light.

## Shot 4: Close-up, top details
> Close-up from the collar to the waist, one hand touching the knot buttons. Sharp detail on the mandarin collar,
> knot buttons, L-shaped seam and the mesh-filled paisley embroidery. Soft blurred beige wall.

## Shot 5: Detail, pants hem
> Low-angle shot from the knees down as she steps forward on a terracotta-tiled floor, showing the straight wide leg
> and the three rows of white topstitching at the hem, with tan sandals. Soft daylight.

## Shot 6 (optional): Travel look
> Full-length on a sunlit railway-heritage platform or airport-style lounge with a small trolley bag, sunglasses
> pushed up on her head. Comfortable, stylish travel co-ord look.
