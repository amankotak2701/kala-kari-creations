# Ivory deer appliqué co-ord set: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** an ivory cotton-linen short kurta with mustard deer appliqué, a hand-embroidered
wildflower border, a stitched band collar and placket, and stitch-line cuffs, with matching ivory
straight pants (supplier design no. 6166).

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the beach photo. Crop off the blue "D.no.6166" label at the top
   left first, or the AI may copy the text.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set (the small details are where AI tools most often go
   wrong):
   - **Deer:** three mustard deer.
     - A stag with antlers on the upper chest, to the left as you face the model.
     - A second deer near the hem, just right of centre.
     - A small one at the right side seam.
     - None are on the back, sleeves or pants.
   - **Flowers:** a light, airy wildflower border along the hem only, in pink, pale blue and white
     with green leaves on thin stems. It is not a dense or all-over pattern.
   - **Neck:** a band collar and a long placket edged with fine stitch lines, no buttons showing.
   - **Cuffs:** three-quarter sleeves with rows of fine pink and grey stitch lines near the cuff.
   - **Colour:** natural ivory with a slubby linen texture, not bright white.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photo: same three
   mustard deer in the same places, same wildflower border at the hem only, same stitched collar,
   placket and cuffs, same ivory colour."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a beach setting, so these use other soft, aesthetic settings that
suit the calm ivory and the deer-and-wildflower story.

**1 · Wildflower meadow at golden hour (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a meadow of soft wild grasses and small white
> and pale-pink wildflowers at golden hour, her hands relaxed at her sides so the chest stag, the
> hem deer and the flower border are clearly visible. Warm backlight glows through her hair, with
> a soft, hazy, dreamy mood and a gentle hill in the far background. True ivory colour.

**2 · Sunlit café with arched window**
> The model sits at a small round marble table by a large arched window in a minimal café with
> cane chairs, linen curtains and a vase of dried flowers, holding a ceramic cup of coffee with
> both hands and smiling softly. The kurta hem and its flower border rest on her lap, facing the
> camera. Bright, airy morning light. Captures her from head to ankle.

**3 · Limewash wall with plant shadows**
> Full-length front view of the model standing against a warm, beige limewash wall, with soft
> leafy shadows of a nearby plant falling on the wall beside her, not on the kurta. A tall
> ceramic vase of dried pampas grass and a cane stool stand at one side. She tucks her hair
> behind one ear with a calm half-smile. Soft, diffused daylight. Clean, Pinterest-style aesthetic.

**4 · Forest trail, walking (the deer story)**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking slowly along a sun-dappled forest trail lined with
> tall trees and ferns, mid-step, looking down with a gentle smile. Soft rays of morning light
> filter through the leaves. The calm forest echoes the deer and wildflower embroidery. Full
> length visible, true ivory colour.

**5 · Boho bedroom, morning light**
> The model sits cross-legged on a bed made with crumpled natural linen sheets and a woven throw,
> with a macramé wall hanging and trailing plants behind her, writing in a small journal and
> looking up at the camera with a soft smile. The kurta's flower border and hem deer are visible
> on her lap. Warm, soft morning window light. Captures her from head to ankle.

**6 · Flower market stall**
> The model stands at a small flower stall full of buckets of white daisies, pink roses and blue
> hydrangeas, holding a loose bunch of white and pink wildflowers against her shoulder and
> laughing. The flowers echo the embroidery, and the kurta's chest stag and flower border stay
> fully visible. Bright, natural daylight. Full length visible.

**7 · Hill-station verandah with chai**
> The model leans on the wooden railing of an old colonial-style hill-station verandah, with misty
> green tea gardens and hills behind her, holding a kulhad of chai and looking out with a peaceful
> smile. Soft, cool morning mist and gentle light. Shows her from head to toe in profile-to-front
> three-quarter view.

**8 · Pottery and linen studio (artisan mood)**
> The model sits on a wooden bench in a bright, whitewashed craft studio with shelves of handmade
> ceramics and folded linen fabrics, gently tracing the embroidered stag on her chest with her
> fingers and smiling down at it. Soft, warm window light. Captures her from head to ankle.

**9 · Detail close-up, embroidery**
> A close-up crop from the hips to just above the hem. The model's hand rests lightly at her side,
> showing the stitch-line cuff band. The hem deer and the full wildflower border (pink flower,
> pale-blue flower, white flower, green leaves) are in crisp focus, so you can see the appliqué
> edges, the brown spots, the French knots and the slub texture of the linen. Soft side light,
> shallow depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, collar and stag**
> A close-up from chin to waist, with the model's head slightly turned. The stitched band collar,
> the fine grey-blue lines along the placket and the mustard stag appliqué on the chest are crisp
> and detailed. Soft, natural window light and a softly blurred neutral background.

**11 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact co-ord set on a natural oatmeal linen cloth. The kurta is
> laid flat and smoothed, showing all three deer and the full flower border, with the ivory pants
> folded neatly beside it. Styled with a few sprigs of dried wildflowers, a woven straw bag,
> tan flat sandals and a thin silver bracelet at the edges. Even, soft daylight. No wrinkles, no
> tags. Catalogue quality.

**12 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera through a
> golden wild-grass field, smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as clean, soft, hazy sky with
> no objects, so text can be added later. Warm golden-hour light, true ivory colour, deer and
> flower embroidery clearly visible.
