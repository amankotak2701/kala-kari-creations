# Brown pintuck tunic with tulip embroidery: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a chocolate-brown cotton slub short kurti with a mandarin collar, a V-placket edged in white
zigzag stitching, seven round embroidered buttons, vertical pintucks with tiny sequin/mirror dots across the front,
a large white tulip embroidery on the back, and pintucked sleeve hems with white running stitch.

## Before you start
1. Upload both photos (front and back) in the same Gemini chat. Add: "Ignore the hanger, the size tag and the
   packing tag."
2. Paste the Base instruction, then one shot prompt per message. Ask for 4:5 (posts) or 9:16 (Reels/Stories).
3. If something is off, reply: "Keep the tunic exactly like the reference photos: same chocolate brown, mandarin
   collar, zigzag V-placket, seven round buttons, front pintucks with sequin dots, tulip embroidery on the back,
   pintucked sleeve hems."
4. Turn on Instagram's AI label if asked.

## Base instruction
> Create a photorealistic professional fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala
> Kari Creations. The model wears the women's tunic shown in the reference photos. Ignore the hanger and all tags.
> Reproduce the tunic exactly:
> - **Fabric and colour:** soft cotton slub with a subtle woven texture, in a warm chocolate brown.
> - **Neck:** a mandarin (band) collar opening into a narrow V-placket edged with white zigzag hand stitching.
> - **Buttons:** seven small round fabric buttons with a white embroidered knot design, running down the centre
>   front to mid-chest.
> - **Front:** fine vertical pintucks on both sides of the placket from the shoulders to the waist, scattered with
>   tiny silver sequin and mirror dots in small clusters.
> - **Back:** a large hand-embroidered motif in the upper back: three tulips on stems with two leaves, outlined in
>   white running stitch with a light kantha-style fill.
> - **Sleeves:** three-quarter sleeves finished with three horizontal pintucks and a row of white running stitch.
> - **Fit and length:** straight, relaxed fit, hip length, with small side slits.
> Styling: paired with off-white or cream straight cotton pants. Model: a graceful Indian woman in her mid-20s,
> natural makeup, oxidised silver jhumkas, a thin silver bracelet, hair in soft waves. Real fabric texture,
> true-to-life colour, sharp focus on the tunic. No logos, text or watermark.
