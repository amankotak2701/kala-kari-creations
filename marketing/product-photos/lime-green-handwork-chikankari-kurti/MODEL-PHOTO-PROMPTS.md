# Lime green handwork chikankari kurti: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a soft lime/pistachio-green straight long kurti, hand-embroidered in ivory-white
Lucknowi chikankari. It has a notched neck with two dense vertical embroidered panels on each side
of a plain placket, all-over small bootis, embroidered necklines and cuffs, and a wide geometric
chikankari border at the hem.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the studio photo.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real kurti. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:** a soft lime / pistachio green with a warm yellow undertone (about #BFB058). Not
     neon, not mint, not olive or mustard. The thread is **ivory-white**.
   - **Fabric:** lightly sheer, soft and fluid, so the shadow-work (bakhiya) stitches look
     slightly translucent.
   - **Neck:** a round neckline with a narrow V-notch, edged all around with a band of white
     fishbone/leaf stitches.
   - **Front panels:** a narrow plain green placket runs down the centre from the notch to the
     bust line. On **each side** of it is a **long vertical embroidered panel**:
     - small flowers and ovals in jaali and murri in the middle;
     - framed on both edges by a vine of tiny leaves.
   - **Body:** small bootis in staggered rows all over the front, alternating:
     - a small pinecone/grape-cluster booti made of tiny murri knots;
     - a small fan-leaf booti.
     Plenty of green shows between them.
   - **Sleeves:** three-quarter to full sleeves with the same bootis, ending in a white
     embroidered cuff band.
   - **Hem border:** a wide border, from top to bottom:
     1. a row of slanted (diagonal) stitched bars;
     2. a row of four-petal flowers;
     3. a thin line;
     4. two more rows of four-petal flowers.
   - **Shape:** straight, calf-to-ankle length, with side slits.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the kurti exactly like the reference photo: soft lime
   green with ivory-white chikankari, a notched neck with a leaf-stitch edge, two vertical
   embroidered panels on each side of a plain placket, alternating knot and leaf bootis,
   embroidered cuffs, and the wide hem border of slanted bars and rows of four-petal flowers."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference is a plain studio shot, so these add fresh, aesthetic settings. Soft whites,
creams and greens keep the lime looking fresh and true.

**1 · Lucknow Nawabi arches (hero, the chikankari story)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a sunlit Nawabi-era arcade with cusped cream
> arches and fine stucco work. Her hands are lightly clasped at her waist with a serene
> expression. The neckline, both vertical panels, the bootis, the cuffs and the full hem border
> are clearly visible. Warm, soft afternoon light, true lime colour.

**2 · Haldi / Mehendi morning (festive)**
> The model sits on a floor cushion under a canopy of yellow marigold strings and white jasmine
> garlands at a haldi morning, laughing, with a brass bowl of marigold petals beside her. The
> yoke panels and bootis face the camera. Bright, cheerful morning light. Captures her from head
> to ankle.

**3 · White jasmine garden**
> The model walks slowly along a stone path in a garden of white jasmine and frangipani, gently
> touching a flower, mid-step, with a soft smile. Soft morning light. Full length visible.

**4 · Sunlit café with cane chairs**
> The model sits at a small marble table in a bright café with cane chairs, white walls and
> potted palms, holding a cup of lemongrass tea and smiling. Bright, airy daylight. Captures her
> from head to ankle.

**5 · Cream limewash wall with dried flowers**
> Full-length front view against a warm cream limewash wall, with a tall ceramic vase of dried
> pampas grass and a wooden stool. She looks slightly off-camera with a calm half-smile, hands
> clasped at her waist. Soft, diffused daylight. Clean, editorial aesthetic.

**6 · Tea-garden hillside**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking along a narrow path through lush green tea gardens on
> a misty hillside, mid-step, looking back with a smile. Soft, cool morning light. Full length
> visible.

**7 · Diwali evening with diyas**
> Full-length front view at dusk in a courtyard lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy lights,
> with a white-and-marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya at waist height. The
> warm light keeps the lime fresh and the white chikankari glowing.

**8 · Lotus pond steps**
> The model sits on stone steps by a lotus pond in the early morning, with pink lotuses and lily
> pads behind her, holding a lotus flower. The hem border spreads across the step. Soft, misty
> light. Captures her from head to toe.

**9 · Detail close-up, neckline and panels**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The notched neckline with its leaf-stitch edge, the plain
> placket and the two vertical panels of jaali and murri flowers framed by leaf vines are in crisp
> focus, with the oxidised choker just in frame. Soft side light shows the raised and shadow-work
> stitches. Shallow depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, bootis and hem border**
> A close-up of the lower kurti. The alternating knot-cluster and leaf bootis above, and the hem
> border of slanted knotted bars and rows of four-petal flowers below, are crisp and detailed.
> Soft daylight, true colours.

**11 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact kurti on a soft ivory linen cloth, laid flat and smoothed so
> the neckline, both panels, the bootis, the sleeves and the full hem border are visible. Styled
> with white jasmine, a few yellow marigolds, an oxidised silver choker and a small embroidery
> hoop with white thread at the edges. Even, soft daylight. Catalogue quality.

**12 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera through cream
> heritage arches, serene and smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as a clean, softly blurred
> cream arch and wall with no objects, so text can be added later. Soft light, true lime green,
> crisp white chikankari.
