# Chanderi patchwork block-print shirt: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a women's chanderi shirt pieced from panels of different block prints in red, hot
pink and black. A gold-coin-trimmed V border runs across the chest, under a pink shirt collar.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini or ChatGPT.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photos:** upload **both** photos in the same chat.
   - The photo on the **blue cloth** shows the colours most clearly, so treat it as the main
     reference.
   - The bedsheet photo shows the details. Crop it to just the shirt first, so the bedsheet
     print and the "Hiren Kotak" date stamp aren't copied.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate message (or a new chat)
   so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real shirt: a red left sleeve and a pink right sleeve, vertical
   print panels, a gold-coin V border and a pink collar. If anything is off, reply with:
   "Keep the shirt exactly like the reference photos: same panels, same sleeve colours, same V
   border with gold coins."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

**1 · Hero, full-length front**
> Full-length front view. The model stands relaxed with one hand resting on her hip and a warm
> smile, with the whole shirt and the gold-coin V border clearly visible. She wears the shirt
> untucked over plain black straight wide-leg cotton pants and tan leather juttis. Setting: a
> whitewashed haveli courtyard with a carved wooden door and potted palms. Bright, soft
> daylight.

**2 · Walking, three-quarter view**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking towards the camera, mid-stride, with the sleeves and
> hem moving slightly and the gold coins catching the light. She wears the shirt with plain white
> straight wide-leg cotton pants and embroidered mojaris. Setting: a narrow lane of old
> pastel-coloured Jaipur-style houses. Golden-hour light.

**3 · Seated, festive lifestyle**
> The model sits on a carved wooden jharokha seat with brass diyas and marigold garlands beside
> her, turned slightly to camera and laughing. The shirt is worn with black pants and a stack of
> silver bangles. Warm, festive evening light with a soft glow. The full shirt is visible.

**4 · Detail close-up**
> A close-up from chin to waist, with the model standing straight and one hand lightly holding
> the collar. The frame shows the pink collar, the V border with its dangling gold coins and the
> panels of different block prints. Soft side light brings out the chanderi sheen and print
> detail. Shallow depth of field.

**5 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact shirt, laid flat and smoothed with the sleeves spread
> slightly and the collar neatly arranged, on an off-white handloom cotton cloth. Styled with
> oxidised silver jhumkas, a few marigold flowers and a pair of embroidered juttis at the edge.
> Even, soft daylight. No wrinkles, no tags. Catalogue quality.

Tip: shots 1 and 2 pair the shirt with plain black and white wide-leg pants, which look like
your mul cotton pants. In the caption you can mention they're sold separately and make a
complete look.
