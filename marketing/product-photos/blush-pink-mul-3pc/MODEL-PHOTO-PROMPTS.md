# Blush pink pure mul 3-piece dress: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a pure mul cotton 3-piece set in soft blush pink with Jaipuri hand-block-style carnation buttas: an
empire-waist anarkali-style kurta with a framed yoke, mirror and sequin work and wide printed borders; plain blush
straight pants; and a matching printed mul dupatta.

## Before you start
1. Upload all the photos in one Gemini chat (the store hanger photo shows the yoke, colours and borders best). Add:
   "Use the photos only for the outfit; do not copy the person, the room, the hanger, the phone or any tags."
2. Paste the Base instruction, then one shot prompt per message. Ask for 4:5 (posts) or 9:16 (Reels/Stories).
3. If something is off, reply: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photos: same blush pink mul, rose-pink
   and grey carnation buttas, framed yoke with mirror borders, gathered flared kurta with wide hem border, plain
   blush pants and matching printed dupatta."
4. Turn on Instagram's AI label if asked.

## Base instruction
> Create a photorealistic professional fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala Kari
> Creations. The model wears the women's 3-piece pure mul cotton suit shown in the reference photos. Use the photos
> only for the outfit; do not copy the person, the room, the hanger, the phone or any tags. Reproduce it exactly:
> - **Fabric and colour:** feather-light, slightly sheer pure mul cotton in a soft blush / powder pink (about
>   #F2CCD1). Soft and airy, with natural soft creases.
> - **Print:** Jaipuri hand-block-style buttas: a bunch of three rose-pink carnation/marigold flowers on slim stems
>   with charcoal-grey leaves, spaced evenly across the kurta, sleeves and dupatta.
> - **Neckline and yoke:** a round neck finished with a tiny pearl-bead piping. A rectangular framed yoke: two
>   vertical printed border strips from the shoulders down to the waist and a horizontal band at the empire waist,
>   in deeper rose pink with a grey floral vine, dotted with small round mirrors / sequins. One large carnation
>   butta with small mirror accents sits in the centre of the yoke.
> - **Kurta body:** gathered at the empire waist into a full, flowing, flared anarkali-style skirt, calf to ankle
>   length, with a wide hem border band in rose pink and grey (rows of small floral vines and stripes).
> - **Sleeves:** three-quarter sleeves with a carnation butta and a narrow matching printed border at the cuff.
> - **Back:** a plain back yoke with one carnation butta and gathers below the waist seam.
> - **Pants:** plain blush pink straight pants in the same mul.
> - **Dupatta:** matching blush mul dupatta with carnation buttas, a rose-pink-and-grey printed border along the
>   edges and a band of grey zigzag (chevron) print at the ends, draped over one shoulder.
> Model: a graceful Indian woman in her mid-20s, soft natural makeup, a small bindi, oxidised silver or delicate
> gold jhumkas, thin bangles, hair in a loose side braid. Real fabric texture, true-to-life colour, sharp focus on
> the outfit. No logos, text or watermark.
