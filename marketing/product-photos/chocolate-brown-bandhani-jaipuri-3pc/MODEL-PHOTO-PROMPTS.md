Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the photo of the model holding the dupatta open against the white
   curtain.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:** a warm **chocolate / coffee brown** (about #563119) with **cream/ivory** print and
     knots. It isn't black, maroon or tan.
   - **Neck:** a short band collar with a narrow V-slit at the centre.
   - **Yoke:** a rectangular yoke from the shoulders to mid-chest, **smocked/pintucked** into a
     textured wavy honeycomb pattern and dotted all over with small **cream French knots**. It's
     edged on both sides by a vertical line of cream knots.
   - **Latkans:** along the yoke's lower edge, a row of **five wooden-bead latkans**: round dark
     wooden beads with a light disc, each hanging from a small cream bead.
   - **Chest motif:** one large **cream bandhani mandala** (a flower made of tiny dots, with
     dotted petals around a dotted centre) printed just below the latkans.
   - **Panel lines:** the flared skirt has vertical panel seams, each traced with a line of cream
     running stitches/knots from the yoke down to the hem border.
   - **Hem border:** a wide band of cream bandhani **ogee/teardrop** shapes (nested dotted teardrops
     with a dotted centre) along the hem.
   - **Sleeves:** three-quarter, with a small band of cream knot embroidery at the cuff.
   - **Shape:** a flared, panelled A-line kurta to mid-calf. Plain brown straight pants.
   - **Dupatta:** brown mulmul with large **cream bandhani mandalas** spaced across it, and a border
     of **cream square blocks** in rows along the edges.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the set exactly like the reference photo: chocolate brown
   with cream detail, a band collar, a smocked yoke dotted with cream knots, five wooden-bead
   latkans, a bandhani mandala on the chest, knotted panel lines, a bandhani ogee hem border, plain
   brown pants and a brown dupatta with bandhani mandalas and a cream square-block border."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a white curtain backdrop with a jute rug, so these use other warm,
earthy settings. Cream walls, terracotta and greenery suit the chocolate and cream palette.

**1 · Earthy mud-house courtyard (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a sunlit courtyard of a traditional cream-plastered
> mud house with a carved wooden door, terracotta pots and a wooden charpai. The dupatta is draped over
> one shoulder and one hand lightly holds out the skirt, so the smocked yoke, the latkans, the chest
> mandala, the panel lines and the ogee hem border are clearly visible. Warm late-afternoon light.

**2 · Dupatta held open (your reference pose)**
> Full-length front view against a warm cream lime-plaster wall. The model holds the dupatta open out
> to one side with her arm stretched, so its bandhani mandalas and square-block border show, with the
> kurta fully visible. Soft, even daylight.

**3 · Twirl (movement)**
> The model spins in a sunlit sandstone courtyard, the flared skirt opening into a circle that shows
> the full ogee hem border and the knotted panel lines, the latkans swinging, and a joyful laugh.
> Slight motion in the fabric, but her face and the yoke stay sharp. Full length.

**4 · Coffee house with wood and plants**
> The model sits at a reclaimed-wood table in a cosy coffee house with exposed brick, hanging plants
> and warm pendant lights, holding a cappuccino and laughing. The yoke and the chest mandala face the
> camera. The coffee tones match the outfit. Warm, soft light. Captures her from head to ankle.

**5 · Autumn park path**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking along a park path covered in fallen golden and brown leaves,
> mid-step, glancing back with a smile. Warm golden-hour light filtering through trees. Full length
> visible.

**6 · Terracotta pottery studio**
> The model sits on a low wooden stool in a rustic pottery studio surrounded by shelves of terracotta
> pots, holding a small clay vase and smiling down at it. The skirt spreads around the stool showing
> the hem border. Warm window light. Captures her from head to toe.

**7 · Desert camp at golden hour**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in front of a cream canvas tent with lanterns and woven
> rugs in a desert camp at sunset, the dupatta lifting in the breeze. Warm, glowing light.

**8 · Diwali evening with diyas (festive)**
> Full-length front view at dusk in a courtyard lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy lights, with
> a marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both hands at waist height. The warm
> glow makes the brown rich and the cream bandhani glow.

**9 · Detail close-up, yoke and latkans**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The band collar with its V-slit, the smocked honeycomb yoke dotted with
> cream French knots, the knotted edge lines, the five wooden-bead latkans and the top of the bandhani
> mandala are in crisp focus. Soft side light shows the smocking texture. Shallow depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, hem border**
> A close-up of the lower skirt. The wide band of cream bandhani ogee teardrops and the knotted panel
> lines meeting it are crisp. Soft daylight, true colours.

**11 · Detail close-up, dupatta**
> A close-up of the model's hand holding the dupatta. A large bandhani mandala and the cream square-block
> border are crisp on the brown mulmul. Soft side light.

**12 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact 3-piece set on a natural jute rug. The kurta is spread out with the
> skirt fanned, showing the yoke, the latkans, the mandala, the panel lines and the hem border, with the
> pants folded to one side and the dupatta folded in soft pleats to the other showing a mandala and its
> border. Styled with a few dried flowers, a gold watch and silver sandals. Even, soft daylight.
> Catalogue quality.

**13 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera along an autumn path of
> golden leaves, smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as clean, softly blurred trees and sky with no
> objects, so text can be added later. Warm light, true chocolate brown, details clearly visible.
