# Dusty pink mul chanderi 3-piece dress: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a dusty rose-pink mul chanderi 3-piece: an A-line kurta with a hand-embroidered floral yoke (pink and
white flowers, green leaves, pearls and sequins) and a pearl placket line, box pleats below the yoke, three-quarter
sleeves with pleated cuffs; matching pants; and a sheer dusty-pink dupatta with small pink embroidered buttis and a
scalloped pearl-edged border.

## Before you start
1. Upload all the photos in one Gemini chat. Add: "Use the photos only for the outfit; do not copy the person, the
   necklace, the room or any tags."
2. Paste the Base instruction, then one shot prompt per message. Ask for 4:5 (posts) or 9:16 (Reels/Stories).
3. If something is off, reply: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photos: same dusty rose-pink mul chanderi,
   floral embroidered yoke with pearls and the pearl centre line, box pleats, pleated cuffs, matching pants and the
   sheer pink dupatta with small buttis and a scalloped pearl border."
4. Turn on Instagram's AI label if asked.

## Base instruction
> Create a photorealistic, elegant fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala Kari

> - **Fabric and colour:** soft, lightweight mul chanderi with a fine slub texture and a gentle silky sheen, in a dusty

> - **Neckline and yoke:** a round neck. A rectangular yoke covered in hand embroidery: pink and rose thread flowers,
>   white daisies, small green leaves and sprigs, scattered pearls, beads and tiny gold sequins. A vertical line of
>   pearls and gold beads runs down the centre of the yoke like a placket.
> - **Kurta body:** below the yoke, neat box pleats flare into a flowing A-line kurta, calf length, with a soft swing.
> - **Sleeves:** three-quarter sleeves with soft pleats near the cuff and a folded cuff band.
> - **Pants:** matching dusty-pink straight pants.
> - **Dupatta:** sheer, lightweight dusty-pink mul chanderi dupatta with small scattered dark-pink thread-embroidered
>   flower buttis, finished with a scalloped (wavy) border edged with tiny pearls/beads. Draped over one shoulder.
> Model: a graceful Indian woman in her late 20s, soft dewy makeup, a small bindi, delicate pearl or kundan earrings,
> thin gold bangles, hair in soft waves or a low bun with a few roses. Real fabric texture and sheen, true-to-life
> colour, sharp focus on the yoke embroidery. No logos, text or watermark.
