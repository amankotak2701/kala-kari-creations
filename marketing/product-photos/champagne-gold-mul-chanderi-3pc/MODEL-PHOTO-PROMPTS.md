# Champagne-gold mul chanderi 3-piece dress: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a champagne / pale-gold mul chanderi 3-piece: a straight kurta with a gold zari, sequin, pearl and white
thread hand-embroidered neckline, scattered gold buttis and scalloped embroidered sleeve cuffs; matching pants; and a
dupatta with small gold buttis and a scalloped embroidered cutwork border.

## Before you start
1. Upload all the photos in one Gemini chat. Add: "Use the photos only for the outfit; do not copy the person, the
   necklace, the room or any tags."
2. Paste the Base instruction, then one shot prompt per message. Ask for 4:5 (posts) or 9:16 (Reels/Stories).
3. If something is off, reply: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photos: same champagne-gold mul chanderi,
   gold zari and pearl embroidered neckline with white flowers, scattered gold buttis, scalloped embroidered sleeve
   cuffs, matching pants and the dupatta with a scalloped gold embroidered border."
4. Turn on Instagram's AI label if asked.

> - **Pants:** matching champagne straight pants.
> - **Dupatta:** sheer champagne mul chanderi dupatta with small scattered gold embroidered buttis and a wide scalloped
