# Mauve pink bandhani Jaipuri cotton 3-piece set: photoshoot prompts

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the photo of the model holding the dupatta open against the white
   curtain.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:** a soft **mauve / rose-orchid pink** (about #CD7695) with **white/light-pink**
     print. It isn't hot pink, purple or lilac. All three pieces are the same mauve.
   - **Neck:** a round neck with a short V-notch, edged with a few **small white beads/sequins**
     around the neckline.
   - **Kurta layout (front):**
     - a **plain mauve centre panel** with three or four large **bandhani mandala flowers** (made of
       tiny dots, with petals around a dotted centre), each with a **tiny round mirror at its
       centre**;
     - on each side of the centre panel, a narrow **vertical geometric border** (a column of dotted
       medallions and diamonds between dotted lines);
     - outside those borders, **side panels of small white polka dots** with smaller mandalas.
   - **Sleeves:** three-quarter, with a bandhani border band at the cuff.
   - **Shape:** a straight kurta, just below the knee, with side slits.
   - **Pants:** mauve palazzo/wide-leg pants with a **bandhani border** at the hem: a band of
     dotted flowers between dotted lines.
   - **Dupatta:** a mauve mulmul field scattered with **large bandhani mandalas**. At the ends, a
     dotted floral border and then a **wide polka-dot panel** with large mandalas.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the set exactly like the reference photo: mauve pink with
   white bandhani print, a beaded notched neck, a plain centre panel with mirror-centred mandalas,
   two vertical geometric borders, polka-dot side panels, bandhani cuffs, palazzo pants with a
   bandhani hem border, and a mandala dupatta with a polka-dot end panel."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a white curtain backdrop with a jute rug, so these use other aesthetic
settings. Soft whites, warm stone and greenery suit the mauve.

**1 · White haveli courtyard with roses (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a sunlit white haveli courtyard with carved arches,
> a small marble fountain and pots of pink roses. The dupatta is draped over one shoulder and her
> hands are relaxed, so the beaded neck, the mandala centre panel, the geometric borders, the
> polka-dot side panels and the palazzo hem border are clearly visible. Soft, warm afternoon light.

**2 · Dupatta held open (your reference pose)**
> Full-length front view against a warm cream lime-plaster wall. The model holds the dupatta open out
> to one side with her arm stretched, so its mandala field and polka-dot end panel show. Soft, even
> daylight.

**3 · Pink-city lane, Jaipur**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking through an old Jaipur lane with pink sandstone façades and
> small arched windows, mid-step, glancing back with a smile, the palazzos swinging. Warm golden-hour
> light. Full length visible.

**4 · Bandhani tie-dye workshop (the craft story)**
> The model stands in a bright Rajasthani tie-dye workshop with lengths of bandhani fabric hanging
> behind her, holding a length of freshly tied cloth and smiling. Soft natural light from high windows.
> Full length visible.

**5 · Café with cane chairs**
> The model sits at a small marble table in a bright café with cane chairs and potted palms, holding a
> cup of rose tea and laughing. The centre panel mandalas face the camera. Airy daylight. Captures her
> from head to ankle.

**6 · Garden swing among bougainvillea**
> The model sits on a wooden swing in a garden with cascading magenta bougainvillea, smiling, the
> dupatta over her arms and the palazzo border showing. Soft, dappled light. Captures her from head to
> ankle.

**7 · Lotus pond steps**
> The model sits on stone steps by a lotus pond in the early morning, holding a pink lotus, with the
> dupatta spread across the step. Soft, misty light. Captures her from head to toe.

**8 · Diwali evening with diyas (festive)**
> Full-length front view at dusk in a courtyard lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy lights, with
> a rose-and-marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both hands at waist height. The
> candlelight makes the tiny mirrors in the mandalas glint.

**9 · Detail close-up, neckline and mandala**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The notched neck with its small white beads and a large bandhani mandala
> with its tiny mirror centre on the plain centre panel, with a geometric border at the edge, are in
> crisp focus. Soft side light. Shallow depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, borders and side panel**
> A close-up of the kurta's side. The vertical geometric border, the polka-dot side panel with its
> smaller mandala and the cuff band are crisp. Soft daylight, true colours.

**11 · Detail close-up, palazzo hem and dupatta**
> A low close-up of the model's ankles. The palazzo hem border of dotted flowers and the dupatta's
> polka-dot end panel falling beside it are crisp. Soft daylight on a warm stone floor.

**12 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact 3-piece set on a soft ivory linen cloth. The kurta is laid flat
> showing all its panels and mandalas, with the palazzos folded to one side showing the hem border, and
> the dupatta folded in soft pleats to the other showing its mandalas and polka-dot panel. Styled with
> pink roses and thin gold bangles. Even, soft daylight. Catalogue quality.

**13 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera through a pink
> sandstone archway, smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as a clean, softly blurred wall and sky
> with no objects, so text can be added later. Soft light, true mauve, prints clearly visible.
