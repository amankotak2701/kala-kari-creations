# Lemon yellow Jaipuri cotton 3-piece set: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a soft lemon-yellow Jaipuri cotton gathered A-line kurta with tone-on-tone thread
embroidery:
- a yoke of a diamond lattice and florals, with a pintucked centre placket;
- vertical embroidered vine panels down the skirt;
- embroidered cuffs.

It comes with matching pants and a lemon mulmul dupatta with tiny four-dot bootis.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the photo of the model holding the dupatta open against the white
   curtain.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:** a soft **lemon / butter yellow** (about #E8D365), light and fresh. It isn't
     mustard, neon or orange. All three pieces are the same yellow.
   - **Embroidery:** all **tone-on-tone**, in a slightly paler yellow-ivory thread, so it shows as
     subtle texture. No contrast colours, mirrors or sequins.
   - **Neck:** a round neck with a narrow V-notch, and below it a **narrow placket of fine vertical
     pintucks** running down to the empire line.
   - **Yoke:** a rectangular yoke on each side of the placket, from the shoulders to just under the
     bust:
     - small floral sprays in the upper part;
     - a **diamond (lattice) grid** of fine stitches filling the lower part.
     It's framed on the outside by a narrow vertical vine border.
   - **Skirt:** gathered at the empire line into a soft A-line to mid-calf, with **vertical
     embroidered vine panels** (leafy sprigs and small flowers) running down from the yoke, and a
     few scattered small flowers.
   - **Sleeves:** three-quarter, with a narrow embroidered vine running down the sleeve and an
     embroidered cuff band.
   - **Pants:** yellow, straight, ankle length.
   - **Dupatta:** a soft lemon mulmul with **tiny four-dot (cross) bootis** in pale yellow,
     evenly spaced in rows. No border.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the set exactly like the reference photo: soft lemon
   yellow with tone-on-tone embroidery only, a notched neck with a pintucked placket, a yoke of
   florals and a diamond lattice, vertical vine panels on the gathered skirt, embroidered cuffs,
   yellow pants and a lemon dupatta with tiny four-dot bootis."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a white curtain backdrop with a jute rug, so these use other sunny,
aesthetic settings. Whites, greens and soft side light keep the yellow fresh and show the
embroidery.

**1 · Haldi morning (festive hero)**
> The model sits on a floor cushion under a canopy of yellow marigold strings and white jasmine
> garlands at a haldi ceremony, laughing, with a brass bowl of turmeric paste and marigold petals
> beside her. The dupatta is draped over her shoulder. Bright, cheerful morning light. Captures her
> from head to ankle.

**2 · Whitewashed courtyard with marigolds**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a sunlit whitewashed courtyard with a carved
> wooden door and brass urlis of floating marigolds. The dupatta is draped over one shoulder and her
> hands are relaxed, so the yoke, the pintucked placket and the skirt panels are clearly visible.
> Soft, warm side light.

**3 · Sunflower field**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking along a path through a field of sunflowers at golden hour,
> one hand brushing the petals, smiling over her shoulder. Warm backlight. Full length visible.

**4 · Dupatta held open (your reference pose)**
> Full-length front view against a warm white lime-plaster wall. The model holds the dupatta open out
> to one side with her arm stretched, so its rows of tiny four-dot bootis show. Soft side light shows
> the embroidery on the kurta. Soft, even daylight.

**5 · Sunlit café with cane chairs**
> The model sits at a small marble table in a bright café with cane chairs and potted palms, holding
> a glass of lemonade and laughing. The yoke embroidery faces the camera. Airy daylight. Captures
> her from head to ankle.

**6 · Garden swing among white flowers**
> The model sits on a wooden swing hung from a tree in a lush garden with white jasmine, smiling, the
> skirt spread over the seat. Soft, dappled light. Captures her from head to ankle.

**7 · Lotus pond steps**
> The model sits on stone steps by a lotus pond in the early morning, holding a pink lotus, with the
> dupatta spread across the step. Soft, misty light. Captures her from head to toe.

**8 · Diwali evening with diyas**
> Full-length front view at dusk on a terrace lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy lights, with
> a marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both hands at waist height. The warm
> glow makes the yellow golden and the embroidery visible.

**9 · Detail close-up, yoke and pintucks**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The notched neckline, the fine vertical pintucks of the placket, the
> small floral sprays and the diamond-lattice embroidery of the yoke, and the vine border are in crisp
> focus as tone-on-tone texture. Low, raking side light. Shallow depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, skirt panels and cuff**
> A close-up of the model's hand resting on the gathered skirt. It shows the vertical embroidered vine
> panels, the cuff band and the vine on the sleeve as tone-on-tone texture on the yellow cotton. Soft
> side light, true colour.

**11 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact 3-piece set on a soft white linen cloth. The kurta is laid flat
> with the skirt fanned out, showing the yoke, the placket, the vine panels and the cuffs, with the
> pants folded to one side and the dupatta folded in soft pleats to the other with its tiny bootis
> showing. Styled with yellow marigolds, white jasmine and thin gold bangles. Soft side light.
> Catalogue quality.

**12 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera through a sunflower
> field, smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as clean, softly blurred sky with no objects, so
> text can be added later. Warm light, true lemon yellow, yoke clearly visible.
