## Shot prompts

**1 · Hero, full-length front**
> Full-length front view. The model stands relaxed with a soft smile, one hand lightly in
> front of her. The whole co-ord set is visible from neckline to ankle, with all 12 embroidered
> squares clearly readable. She wears tan leather juttis and small silver jhumkas. Setting: a
> sunlit cream-walled haveli courtyard with a carved wooden doorway and potted palms.
> Warm late-afternoon light.

**2 · Walking, three-quarter view**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking slowly towards the camera, mid-step, so the wide-leg
> pants swing and the kurta's side slit opens slightly. She glances off-camera with a natural
> smile. Setting: a garden path lined with marigold and bougainvillea bushes. Soft golden-hour
> backlight. Full length visible.

**3 · Seated lifestyle**
> The model sits on a wooden bench on a terracotta-floored verandah, angled slightly, holding
> a clay chai kulhad and laughing. The embroidered grid on the kurta faces the camera. Behind
> her: hanging plants, a woven jute rug, brass diyas. Warm, cosy morning light. Captures her
> from head to ankle.

**4 · Embroidery close-up**
> A close-up crop from shoulder to mid-thigh, with the model standing, one hand resting at her
> side. The frame is filled with the embroidered grid so the thread texture of the flowers,
> the green leaves and the hand-stitched cream lines are crisp and detailed. Soft window light
> from the side shows the stitch texture. Shallow depth of field.

**5 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact co-ord set: the kurta laid flat and neatly smoothed with the
> pants folded beside it, on a crisp off-white handloom cotton cloth. Styled with a few fresh
> marigold flowers, a brass bowl and a pair of tan juttis at the edge. Even, soft daylight.
> No wrinkles, no tags, no hanger. Catalogue quality.
