# Chanderi 3-piece suit with dupatta: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a long cream chanderi kurta with black block-print stripes and a printed yoke, plus
plain beige pants and a printed dupatta.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini or ChatGPT.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photos:** upload **both** photos in the same chat.
   - The photo with all three pieces (kurta, pants, dupatta) is the main reference.
   - The kurta-only photo shows the full length and the hem border.
   - Crop both photos to just the clothes first, so the bedsheet print, your feet and the
     "Hiren Kotak" date stamp aren't copied.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set:
   - a cream kurta with thin black stripes,
   - a yoke split into a red diamond print and a black floral print, with a mustard band below,
   - diamond-print bands on the cuffs and hem,
   - plain beige pants,
   - a diamond-print dupatta with black floral borders.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photos: same
   yoke, same borders, same dupatta print."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

**1 · Hero, full-length front**
> Full-length front view, showing the model from head to toe. The dupatta is draped neatly over
> her left shoulder and falls down her back, so the kurta yoke, cuffs and hem border are fully
> visible. She stands gracefully with a soft smile, wearing tan leather juttis. Setting: a sunlit
> haveli courtyard with cream walls, a carved wooden door and potted palms. Warm
> late-afternoon light.

**2 · Walking with flowing dupatta**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking along an arched haveli corridor. The dupatta is
> draped across both shoulders and its loose end lifts gently in the breeze, showing the black
> floral border and the mustard paisley panel. Golden-hour light streams in through the arches.
> Full length visible.

**3 · Seated, festive lifestyle**
> The model sits on a low carved wooden diwan with brass diyas, a brass urli of marigolds and
> rose petals beside her, smiling and looking slightly off-camera. The dupatta is draped over one
> shoulder and spread across her lap so its print is visible. Warm festive evening light with a
> soft glow. Captures her from head to ankle.

**4 · Detail close-up, yoke and dupatta**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The model gently holds the edge of the dupatta near her
> shoulder so its black floral border sits beside the kurta's printed yoke. The red diamond print,
> the black floral half, the mustard paisley band and one diamond-print sleeve cuff are all in
> crisp focus. Soft side light shows the chanderi sheen. Shallow depth of field.

**5 · Styled flat-lay of all 3 pieces (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact 3-piece set on an off-white handloom cotton cloth. The kurta
> is laid flat and smoothed in the centre. The beige pants are folded neatly to its left. The
> dupatta is folded in soft pleats to its right, with the mustard paisley end and black floral
> border showing. Styled with oxidised silver jhumkas, a few marigold flowers and a pair of tan
> juttis at the edge. Even, soft daylight. No wrinkles, no tags, no hanger. Catalogue quality.
