## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the photo of the model holding the dupatta open against the white
   curtain.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:** a soft **pista / sage-lime green** (about #929C5C), muted and slightly yellow.
     It isn't mint, bright parrot green or olive. All three pieces are the same green.
   - **Neck:** a round neck with a short V-notch, and a narrow placket below it with **three
     small round fabric-covered buttons** in pale pink/ivory.
   - **Yoke:** a rectangular yoke, from the shoulders to mid-chest, with a slightly pointed bottom
     edge. It's outlined with a narrow textured green border of stitch lines. Inside it:
     - **six-to-eight large flowers** embroidered in pink and blush thread, each with **a small round
       mirror at the centre**;
     - smaller pink buds;
     - leafy vines in **white and pale-green thread**.
     It's soft and pastel, not heavy.
   - **Tassels:** along the bottom edge of the yoke hang **four green thread tassels** with **small
     pearl/white bead latkans** between them.
   - **Side slits:** the side slits are edged with a narrow pink-and-white embroidered vine.
   - **Sleeves:** three-quarter, plain green, with a thin pink-gold piping at the cuff.
   - **Shape:** a relaxed A-line kurta reaching mid-calf, with matching straight green pants.
   - **Dupatta:** a soft pista mulmul with **tiny embroidered flowers** (blush and gold-brown),
     scattered and spaced apart. No border.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the set exactly like the reference photo: soft pista
   green, a notched neck with three fabric buttons, a yoke of pink mirror-centred flowers and
   white-green vines, four green tassels with pearl latkans, embroidered side slits, plain

4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a white curtain backdrop with a jute rug, so these use other fresh,
aesthetic settings. Soft creams, pinks and greenery suit the pista and blush palette.

**1 · Jaipur haveli courtyard with bougainvillea (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a sunlit haveli courtyard with soft cream walls,
> a carved jharokha window and cascading pink bougainvillea that echoes the pink embroidery. The
> dupatta is draped over one shoulder and her hands are relaxed, so the embroidered yoke, the buttons,
> the tassels, the side slits and the pants are clearly visible. Warm late-afternoon light.

**2 · Dupatta held open (your reference pose)**
> Full-length front view against a warm cream lime-plaster wall. The model holds the dupatta open
> out to one side with her arm stretched, so its soft drape and the tiny scattered flowers show,
> with the yoke and tassels clearly visible. Soft, even daylight.

**3 · Rose garden morning**
> The model walks slowly along a garden path lined with blush-pink roses and soft greenery, gently
> touching a rose, mid-step, with a warm smile. Fresh morning light. Full length visible.

**4 · Sunlit café with cane chairs**
> The model sits at a small marble table in a bright café with cane chairs, white walls and potted
> palms, holding a cup of chai and laughing. The yoke embroidery and tassels face the camera. Airy
> daylight. Captures her from head to ankle.

**5 · Cream limewash wall with dried flowers**
> Full-length front view against a warm cream limewash wall, with a tall ceramic vase of dried
> pampas grass and a wooden stool. She tucks her hair behind one ear with a gentle smile. Soft,
> diffused daylight. Clean, Pinterest-style aesthetic.

**6 · Garden swing**
> The model sits on a wooden swing hung from a tree in a lush garden with white flowers, smiling
> dreamily, the dupatta draped over her arms. Soft, dappled afternoon light. Captures her from head
> to ankle.

**7 · Lotus pond steps**
> The model sits on stone steps by a lotus pond in the early morning, with pink lotuses and lily
> pads behind her, holding a lotus. The dupatta spreads across the step. Soft, misty light.
> Captures her from head to toe.

**8 · Diwali evening with diyas (festive)**
> Full-length front view at dusk on a terrace lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy lights,
> with a pink-and-white flower rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both hands at
> waist height. The candlelight makes the little mirrors in the flowers glint, and the green stays
> fresh.

**9 · Detail close-up, embroidered yoke**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The notched neckline, the three fabric buttons, the pink flowers
> with their mirror centres, the white-and-green leafy vines, the textured border and the row of
> tassels with pearl latkans are in crisp focus. Soft side light makes the mirrors glint. Shallow
> depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, side slit and dupatta**
> A close-up of the model's side and hand. The embroidered pink-and-white vine along the side slit,
> the thin piping on the sleeve cuff and a fold of the dupatta with its tiny embroidered flowers are
> crisp. Soft daylight, true colour.

**11 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact 3-piece set on a soft ivory linen cloth. The kurta is laid flat
> and smoothed, showing the yoke, buttons, tassels and side slits, with the pants folded to one side
> and the dupatta folded in soft pleats to the other with its tiny flowers showing. Styled with
> blush-pink roses, thin gold bangles and silver sandals at the edges. Even, soft daylight.
> Catalogue quality.

**12 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera along a garden path
> lined with pink roses, smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as clean, softly blurred greenery
> and sky with no objects, so text can be added later. Soft light, true pista green, yoke clearly
> visible.
