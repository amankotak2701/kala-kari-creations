# Steel-grey silk kurti: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a steel blue-grey silk straight kurti with a lustrous sheen. It has a round neck
and five fine vertical lines of hand embroidery down the yoke, each studded with small
sparkling stone-and-bead flowers. The sleeves are three-quarter length, and there are side
slits. It's worn with black leggings.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the photo of the model with the palm plants and the white
   backdrop. Crop it to just the model first, so the creased backdrop and the artificial grass
   aren't copied.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real kurti. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:** a cool steel blue-grey (about #65758A), with lighter silvery highlights where
     the light hits and deeper slate in the folds. Not navy, not light grey, not lavender.
   - **Sheen:** smooth silk with a liquid, satin-like lustre and soft fluid folds. Not matte
     cotton, not shiny plastic.
   - **Embroidery:** **five** thin vertical lines of fine silver-white thread running down from
     the neckline over the chest, ending at about the bust line. The centre line and the two
     lines next to it are longest. Each line has small flower motifs made of tiny crystal
     stones and beads spaced along it, about four per line. Delicate and minimal, not heavy work.
   - **Neck:** a plain round neckline with a fine self piping, no collar, no buttons.
   - **Shape:** straight, ending just above the knee, with side slits. The three-quarter sleeves
     have a plain hem. There's no print and no border.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the kurti exactly like the reference photo: steel
   blue-grey silk with a soft lustre, a plain round neck, five fine vertical silver thread lines
   on the yoke with tiny stone-and-bead flowers, plain three-quarter sleeves, knee length, no
   print or border."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Base instruction (paste before every shot prompt)

> metallic.
>

> no slit.

> centre line and the two beside it reach to about the bust line, and the two outer lines are
> slightly shorter. Along each line, spaced evenly, sit about four tiny flower motifs, each made
> of a small sparkling crystal stone in the centre with a few tiny silver beads around it. There

>
> **Styling:** black ankle-length leggings and tan leather flat sandals.
>

> colour or make the fabric matte. No tags. No text or numbers anywhere.

> The model is a young Indian woman with a warm, shy smile, soft makeup, long, sleek, straight
> dark hair worn open, small diamond studs and a slim silver watch. The photo should look like a

>
> **Make exactly ONE single photo**: one image, one scene, one pose. Not a collage, grid,

## Shot prompts

Soft, elegant settings with warm or neutral light suit the cool silver-grey silk and make its
sheen glow.

**1 · Luxe hotel lobby (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in an elegant hotel lobby with cream marble
> floors, a grand curved staircase with brass railings and a large vase of white orchids. Her
> hands are lightly clasped at her waist, with a soft smile. The silk's lustre, the five
> embroidered lines and the stone flowers are clearly visible. Warm, soft ambient light.

**2 · Beige limewash wall with dried flowers**
> Full-length front view against a warm beige limewash wall, with a tall ceramic vase of dried
> pampas grass and a cane stool beside her. She tucks her hair behind one ear and looks down with
> a shy smile, as in the reference pose. Soft, diffused daylight with a gentle window shadow on
> the wall, not on the kurti. Clean, Pinterest-style aesthetic.

**3 · Café by a large window**
> The model sits at a small round marble table by a tall window in a chic café with velvet chairs
> and soft pendant lights, holding a cappuccino and laughing. Window light catches the silk sheen
> and the tiny stones. Captures her from head to ankle.

**4 · Rooftop at golden hour**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking across a rooftop terrace with potted olive trees and
> string lights, a softly blurred city skyline at golden hour behind her, mid-step and smiling.
> The warm light makes the silver-grey silk glow with soft highlights. Full length visible.

**5 · Diwali evening with diyas (festive)**
> Full-length front view at dusk on a balcony strung with warm fairy lights, with lit brass diyas
> along the railing and a white-and-marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit diya in both
> hands at chest height and smiles softly. The candlelight makes the stone flowers sparkle and the
> silk shimmer, while the colour stays true.

**6 · Office-to-evening, modern workspace**
> The model stands at a light wooden desk in a bright, modern workspace with large windows and a
> plant, holding a coffee mug and a notebook, looking confident. Smart and polished. Soft
> daylight. Full length visible.

**7 · Garden with white flowers**
> The model stands on a stone path in a lush garden full of white roses and jasmine, gently
> touching a flower with a soft smile. Soft, dappled afternoon light shows the fluid drape. Full
> length visible.

**8 · Art gallery, editorial**
> Full-length view of the model in a minimal white art gallery, in front of a large abstract
> painting in soft greys and blush pink, looking slightly off-camera with a calm, poised
> expression. Soft gallery lighting. High-fashion mood.

**9 · Detail close-up, embroidery**
> A close-up from chin to mid-chest. The round piped neckline and the five fine silver thread
> lines with their tiny crystal-and-bead flowers are in crisp focus, so the stones catch a little
> sparkle. The silk's smooth lustre shows in soft light. Shallow depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, silk drape**
> A close-up of the model's hand resting at her waist, showing the three-quarter sleeve hem and
> the soft, liquid folds of the steel-grey silk with silvery highlights and slate shadows. Soft
> side light, true colour.

**11 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact kurti on a soft ivory silk-satin cloth, laid flat and smoothed
> so the neckline and all five embroidered lines with their stone flowers are fully visible.
> Styled with a few white orchids, small diamond studs, a slim silver watch and tan sandals at the
> edges. Soft daylight that shows the sheen. Catalogue quality.

**12 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera down an elegant
> cream-marble corridor with soft arches, smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as a clean,
> softly blurred cream wall with no objects, so text can be added later. Soft warm light, true
> steel-grey colour, embroidery visible.
