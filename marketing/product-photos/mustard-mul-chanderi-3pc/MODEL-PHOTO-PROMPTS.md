# Mustard mul chanderi 3-piece dress: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a mustard / turmeric-gold mul chanderi 3-piece: an A-line kurta with a notched V neck, a round
hand-embroidered medallion on the chest with beaded tassels, pintucked sleeves with an ivory cuff band and an ivory
hem band; matching pants; and a mustard dupatta with ivory borders, a pearl-and-gold lattice lace and an ivory end
panel with round embroidered medallions.

## Before you start
1. Upload all the photos in one Gemini chat (the store hanger photo shows the full kurta and hem band best). Add:
   "Use the photos only for the outfit; do not copy the person, the room, the hanger or any tags."
2. Paste the Base instruction, then one shot prompt per message. Ask for 4:5 (posts) or 9:16 (Reels/Stories).
3. If something is off, reply: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photos: same mustard mul chanderi, notched
   V neck, round embroidered medallion with beaded tassels, ivory sleeve and hem bands, and the mustard dupatta with
   pearl lace and the ivory end with round embroidered medallions."
4. Turn on Instagram's AI label if asked.

## Base instruction
> Create a photorealistic, elegant festive fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala
> Kari Creations. The model wears the women's 3-piece mul chanderi suit shown in the reference photos. Use the photos
> only for the outfit; do not copy the person, the room, the hanger or any tags. Reproduce it exactly:
> - **Fabric and colour:** soft, lightweight mul chanderi with a subtle silky sheen, in a rich mustard / turmeric
>   gold (deep golden yellow with an olive undertone, not lemon yellow, not orange).
> - **Neckline:** a round neck with a small notched V at the centre front.
> - **Chest medallion:** a large circular hand-embroidered motif on the chest: gold zari, sequins, beads and small
>   stones forming a dense base, with raised thread flowers in pink, red, sky blue, peach and green leaves. A fringe
>   of long beaded tassels (pearls and gold beads) hangs from the lower half of the circle.
> - **Kurta body:** fitted bodice with princess seams flaring into a long A-line kurta to the calf, with a wide plain
>   ivory/off-white band along the hem.
> - **Sleeves:** three-quarter bell-ish sleeves with two fine pintucks above a narrow ivory cuff band.
> - **Pants:** matching mustard straight pants.
> - **Dupatta:** mustard mul chanderi with a narrow ivory border along the long edges. Near each end, a gold
>   criss-cross lattice lace with small hanging pearls, then an ivory silk end panel with two large round embroidered
>   medallions matching the kurta (gold zari, sequins and pastel thread flowers). Draped over one shoulder so the
>   ivory medallion end falls in front.
> Model: a graceful Indian woman in her late 20s, soft glam festive makeup, a small bindi, gold-and-pearl jhumkas,
> thin gold bangles, hair in a low bun with fresh jasmine or open soft waves. Real fabric texture and sheen,
> true-to-life colour, sharp focus on the embroidery. No logos, text or watermark.
