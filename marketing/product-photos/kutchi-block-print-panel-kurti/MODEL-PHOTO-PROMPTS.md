# Kutchi hand block-print panel kurti: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a long, flared A-line cotton kurti in Kutchi hand block print (Ajrakh and dabu
style). It has:
- an indigo Ajrakh yoke and sleeves with madder-red medallions;
- a skirt pieced from vertical panels of eight different block prints in indigo, madder red,
  cream, aubergine and rust.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the boutique photo.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real kurti. AI tools most often get the panels wrong: the order,
   repeated prints, or panels that turn horizontal. Check these:
   - **Colours:** a natural-dye palette of **indigo blue**, **madder red**, **cream**, **deep
     aubergine** and **rust-orange**, with white outlines. Matte cotton, not neon.
   - **Yoke and sleeves:** indigo Ajrakh with a grid of large madder-red and indigo medallions
     (ogee/teardrop shapes and eight-point stars) outlined in fine white dots. It runs from the
     round neck to an empire line just under the bust. The sleeves are three-quarter, in the
     same print.
   - **Skirt panels:** from the empire line, **vertical** gathered panels run to the hem. From
     the left side to the right side as you face the model:
     1. indigo with scattered white floral sprays (dabu);
     2. cream with small black flower dots;
     3. madder red with black and red floral vines;
     4. indigo with cream geometric motifs: triangles, rings and bracket shapes;
     5. a **wide centre panel** in deep aubergine, in alternating blocks of white flower lattice
        and lavender paisley scrolls;
     6. indigo with white stripes and small square tiles;
     7. a **rust-orange and white diamond lattice** with indigo flowers inside the diamonds;
     8. indigo with large white circular motifs and dots.
   - **Shape:** a full, flared A-line, calf length. No pants are visible.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the kurti exactly like the reference photo: an indigo
   Ajrakh yoke and sleeves with red medallions, and a skirt of eight vertical block-print panels in
   the same order: indigo floral, cream dots, red floral, indigo geometric, a wide aubergine centre,
   indigo stripes, a rust diamond, indigo circles. Do not repeat or reorder the panels."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a boutique interior, so these use settings that tell the Kutchi
story. Warm whites and earth tones make the indigo and madder red glow.

**1 · Kutch bhunga courtyard (hero, the Kutchi story)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in the courtyard of a traditional round Kutch
> bhunga mud house, with whitewashed walls decorated with mirror-work and clay relief, a wooden
> charpai and a brass pot. Her hands are relaxed in front of her, so the Ajrakh yoke and all eight
> skirt panels are clearly visible. Warm late-afternoon light.

**2 · Rann of Kutch white desert at golden hour**
> Full-length front view of the model walking across the white salt desert of the Rann of Kutch
> at golden hour, under a soft pink-orange sky. A breeze lifts the flared skirt so the panels fan
> out. Low, warm side light, long soft shadow. Calm, cinematic mood.

**3 · Ajrakh printing workshop (the craft story)**
> The model stands at a long wooden printing table in a Kutch Ajrakh workshop, holding a carved
> wooden block. Behind her are trays of indigo and madder dye and long lengths of freshly printed
> Ajrakh fabric drying on bamboo lines. Soft natural light from high windows. Full length visible.

**4 · Twirl in a whitewashed courtyard (movement)**
> The model spins in a sunlit whitewashed courtyard, the A-line skirt flaring into a full circle
> so every vertical panel shows, her hair moving and a joyful laugh. Slight motion in the fabric,
> but her face and the yoke stay sharp. Bright, warm light. Full length.

**5 · Indigo dye yard**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking between bamboo lines hung with long lengths of indigo
> and madder-red fabric in an artisan's dye yard, mid-step, glancing back with a smile. Clay dye
> vats sit in the background. Dappled morning sunlight. Full length visible.

**6 · Café with cane chairs and plants**
> The model sits at a small round wooden table in a bright café with cane chairs, white walls and
> potted palms, holding a cup of chai and laughing. The skirt panels fall around her. Airy daylight.
> Captures her from head to ankle.

**7 · Terracotta wall with a blue wooden door**
> Full-length front view against a warm terracotta lime-plaster wall beside an old carved
> indigo-blue wooden door and a clay pot of dried grass. She tucks her hair behind one ear with a
> gentle smile. Soft, warm daylight.

**8 · Diwali evening with diyas (festive)**
> Full-length front view at dusk in a courtyard lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy lights,
> with a marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both hands at waist height. The
> candlelight warms the madder red and rust, and the indigo stays rich.

**9 · Detail close-up, Ajrakh yoke**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The round neckline and the indigo Ajrakh yoke with its madder-red
> ogee medallions, eight-point stars and fine white dot outlines are in crisp focus, with the start
> of the skirt panels at the empire seam. Soft side light shows the hand-block texture.

**10 · Detail close-up, skirt panels**
> A close-up of the skirt from waist to knee. The vertical panels side by side (indigo floral, cream
> dots, red floral, indigo geometric, the aubergine centre with its flower lattice and paisley
> blocks, indigo stripes, rust diamonds, indigo circles) are in crisp focus, with the model's hands
> resting in front. Soft daylight, true colours.

**11 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact kurti on a natural off-white handloom cloth, with the skirt fanned
> out so all eight panels and the Ajrakh yoke and sleeves are fully visible. Styled with carved
> wooden printing blocks, a small brass bowl of indigo powder, a gold pendant and slate-blue sandals
> at the edges. Even, soft daylight. Catalogue quality.

**12 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera down a sandy Kutch
> village lane lined with whitewashed mud walls, smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as clean,
> soft, warm sky with no objects, so text can be added later. Warm light, true indigo and madder,
> panels clearly visible.
