## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the photo of the model holding the dupatta open against the white
   curtain.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colours:**
     - deep **wine/burgundy** (about #511522) for the hem band, cuffs, pants and dupatta;
     - **blush-beige** (about #E3C2A8) for the stripe ground;
     - **olive green** and soft blush-pink in the motifs.
     The colours aren't bright red, purple or brown.
   - **Kurta body:** fine, even **vertical stripes** of wine on blush-beige, hand block-printed
     with slightly wavy lines, from the shoulders to the hem band. The sleeves are striped too.
   - **Chest motif:** one **keyhole-shaped medallion** just below the round neckline, with a round
     top and a smaller drop below joined by a short neck. It's printed in wine, blush and olive
     dots, and set with **five small round mirrors** in a cross on the round part and two or three
     small dark mirrors or beads in the drop. It's outlined with a fine stitched edge.
   - **Shape:** a fitted bodice with an empire seam, then a gathered, **flared anarkali** skirt with
     panels, to the ankle.
   - **Hem:** a narrow border of a blush-and-wine paisley vine with a dotted edge, then a **wide
     solid-wine band** with a row of large **medallions**. Each medallion has a big round top and
     a smaller oval drop, filled with blush-pink flowers on an olive ground, like a pendant. A fine
     wine lace runs along the hem edge.
   - **Cuffs:** three-quarter sleeves with the narrow paisley-vine border, then a wine cuff band
     with a medallion.
   - **Dupatta:**
     - a light wine mulmul;
     - vertical chains of the same round-and-oval medallions in olive and blush;
     - one long edge has a border of wine and olive stripes with a blush dotted paisley vine.
   - **Pants:** plain wine, straight.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the set exactly like the reference photo: a flared
   anarkali with fine wine-on-blush stripes, a mirror-work keyhole medallion at the chest, a wide
   wine hem band with olive-and-blush medallions, wine cuffs and pants, and the wine mulmul
   dupatta with medallion chains and an olive-striped border."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a white curtain backdrop with a jute rug, so these use other
aesthetic settings. Pink City (Jaipur) tones, warm stone and greenery suit the wine and olive
palette.

**1 · Jaipur pink-city haveli courtyard (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a sunlit haveli courtyard with soft pink-terracotta
> walls, carved jharokha windows and a small marble fountain. The dupatta is draped over her left
> shoulder and falls down her side, and her right hand lightly holds the skirt. The mirror
> medallion, the stripes, the full hem band of medallions and the dupatta's border are clearly
> visible. Warm late-afternoon light.

**2 · Dupatta held open (shows the full print)**
> Full-length front view against a warm cream lime-plaster wall. The model holds the dupatta open
> out to one side with her arm stretched, as in the reference pose, so the medallion chains and the
> olive-striped border are fully visible. The kurta's chest medallion and hem band show clearly.
> Soft, even daylight.

**3 · Twirl in a courtyard (movement)**
> The model spins in a sunlit sandstone courtyard, the anarkali skirt flaring into a full circle that
> shows the whole wine hem band with its row of medallions, the dupatta floating behind her, and a
> joyful laugh. Slight motion in the fabric, but her face and the chest medallion stay sharp. Full
> length.

**4 · Hawa Mahal-style lane at golden hour**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking through an old Jaipur lane with pink sandstone façades and
> small arched windows, mid-step, glancing back with a smile. The skirt swings to show the hem band.
> Warm golden-hour light. Full length visible.

**5 · Block-printing workshop (the craft story)**
> The model stands at a long wooden printing table in a Sanganer/Bagru artisan workshop, holding a
> carved wooden medallion block in both hands. Lengths of block-printed fabric dry on lines behind
> her. Soft natural light from high windows. Full length visible.

**6 · Diwali evening with diyas (festive)**
> Full-length front view at dusk in a courtyard lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy lights,
> with a marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both hands at waist height. The
> candlelight makes the mirrors on the chest medallion sparkle, and the wine stays rich.

**7 · Garden swing among bougainvillea**
> The model sits on a wooden swing in a garden with cascading magenta bougainvillea and green
> hedges, the anarkali skirt spread around her and the dupatta over one shoulder, smiling. Soft,
> dappled light. Captures her from head to ankle.

**8 · Café with cane chairs**
> The model sits at a small marble table in a bright café with cane chairs and potted palms,
> holding a cup of masala chai and laughing. The striped bodice and the chest medallion face the
> camera. Airy daylight. Captures her from head to ankle.

**9 · Detail close-up, mirror medallion**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The round neckline and the keyhole medallion with its five round
> mirrors, its dotted block print, its stitched outline and its lower drop are in crisp focus on the
> fine wine stripes. Soft side light makes the mirrors glint. Shallow depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, hem band**
> A close-up of the lower skirt. The narrow paisley-vine border, the wide wine band with its row of
> olive-and-blush pendant medallions and the fine lace edge are crisp, with the stripes above. Soft
> daylight, true colours.

**11 · Detail close-up, dupatta and cuff**
> A close-up of the model's hand holding the dupatta. It shows the medallion chains on the wine
> mulmul, the olive-and-wine striped border with its dotted paisley vine, and the kurta cuff with
> its paisley border and medallion band. Soft side light.

**12 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact 3-piece set on a natural jute rug. The anarkali is spread out
> with its skirt fanned to show the hem band, and the chest medallion with its mirrors is visible.
> The wine pants are folded to one side, and the dupatta is folded in soft pleats to the other side,
> showing its medallions and border. Styled with a carved wooden printing block, small gold hoops
> and a few marigolds. Even, soft daylight. Catalogue quality.

**13 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera through a pink
> sandstone archway, smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as a clean, softly blurred pink wall
> and sky with no objects, so text can be added later. Warm light, true wine and blush colours, hem
> band clearly visible.
