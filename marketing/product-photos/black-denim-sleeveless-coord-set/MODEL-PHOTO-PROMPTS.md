# Black denim sleeveless co-ord set: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a black soft-denim sleeveless shirt with a collar, two flap patch pockets, metal
buttons, a self-fabric tie belt and a curved hem, with matching wide-leg pants. Every seam is
finished with grey-white contrast topstitching.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the fruit-cart photo. The cart has another brand's name
   ("NAWAZISH") painted on it, so crop the photo to just the model first, or add "Ignore the
   cart and its sign, do not show any text" to your message.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:** washed jet-black denim with a very slight cool tone (like a black rinse). The
     pants are a touch deeper black than the top. Not navy, not grey, not faded.
   - **Topstitching:** fine grey-white double lines on the collar, placket, pocket flaps and
     pockets, belt edges, hem and pant hems. Thin, not thick stripes.
   - **Pockets:** two chest patch pockets with pointed-corner flaps, each with a button and a
     vertical double topstitch line down the middle of the pocket.
   - **Buttons:** antique silver/gunmetal metal buttons down the front placket and on the pocket
     flaps.
   - **Belt:** a matching topstitched tie belt at the waist, knotted at the front with long
     hanging ends.
   - **Shape:** sleeveless with clean armholes, hip length, with a curved shirt-tail hem.
   - **Pants:** high-waisted, wide straight leg, ankle length, with a single topstitch line
     near the hem.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photo: black
   denim with thin grey-white topstitching, collar, two flap chest pockets with a centre
   stitch line, metal buttons, front tie belt, sleeveless curved-hem shirt and wide-leg pants."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a seaside fruit cart, so these use other bright, playful settings.
Light and colourful backgrounds make the black and the white stitch lines pop.

**1 · Pastel café front (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in front of a pastel-pink café façade with a
> striped awning, a small bistro table and potted palms. One hand is in her pant pocket and the
> other holds an iced coffee, with a big laugh. The collar, the pockets, the buttons, the tie
> belt and the wide-leg pants are all clearly visible. Bright, sunny daylight.

**2 · Goa Latin Quarter, colourful houses**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking along a narrow lane of bright yellow, blue and red
> Portuguese-style houses in Fontainhas, Goa, mid-stride, glancing back with a smile. The tie-belt
> ends and the wide pants swing with the step. Warm afternoon light. Full length visible.

**3 · Beach boardwalk at sunset**
> Full-length view of the model leaning on a weathered white wooden railing on a beach boardwalk
> at golden hour, with the sea and a soft pink-orange sky behind her. A light breeze lifts her
> curls and the belt ends. Warm backlight outlines the outfit while the topstitching stays
> visible.

**4 · Ice-cream parlour, playful**
> The model sits on a mint-green stool at the counter of a retro ice-cream parlour with
> black-and-white checkered floors and pastel walls, holding a waffle cone and laughing. The
> pockets, buttons and belt face the camera. Bright, cheerful light. Captures her from head to
> ankle.

**5 · Bougainvillea wall, street style**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in front of a white wall covered in hot-pink
> bougainvillea, one hand adjusting her red bandana and her weight on one hip, with a confident
> smile. Bright, soft daylight. Fresh, Instagram street-style energy.

**6 · Rooftop terrace with string lights**
> Three-quarter view of the model on a city rooftop terrace with potted olive trees, a rattan
> sofa and string lights at dusk, holding a mocktail glass and laughing. A softly blurred skyline

**7 · Flea-market stall, vintage vibe**
> The model browses a colourful vintage flea-market stall of hanging scarves, sunglasses and

> daylight. A fun, candid mood. Captures her from head to toe.

**8 · Minimal studio, editorial**
> Full-length front view against a seamless warm-cream studio backdrop with one large potted
> palm. The model stands in a strong, relaxed pose with one hand on her hip and the other in her
> pocket, and a confident expression. Soft, even studio light that shows every topstitch line
> and the denim texture. Clean, high-fashion look.

**9 · Detail close-up, pockets and buttons**
> A close-up from collarbone to waist. The pointed collar, the topstitched placket with metal
> buttons, both flap pockets with their centre stitch lines and the knotted tie belt are in crisp
> focus, with the red bandana just in frame. The denim weave is visible. Soft side light.

**10 · Detail close-up, belt and hem**
> A close-up from waist to upper thigh, with the model's hand resting in her pant pocket. The
> tie-belt knot with its topstitched edges and hanging ends, the curved shirt-tail hem with
> topstitching, and the wide-leg pants below are in crisp focus. Soft daylight, true black.

**11 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact co-ord set on a warm sand-coloured linen cloth. The shirt is
> laid flat and buttoned, with the belt tied loosely at the waist and the pockets and stitching
> fully visible. The wide-leg pants are folded neatly beside it, showing the hem stitch line.
> Styled with a red bandana, round sunglasses, silver slides and a small woven bag at the edges.
> Even, soft daylight. No wrinkles, no tags. Catalogue quality.

**12 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera down a bright,
> colourful street with pastel shopfronts, smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as clean,
> softly blurred blue sky with no objects, so text can be added later. Bright daylight, rich
> black, crisp topstitching.
