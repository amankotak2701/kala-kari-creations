# Olive green tassel-yoke co-ord set: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a deep olive-green cotton short kurta with a boho embroidered yoke panel. The
yoke has:
- a beige base with a large maroon paisley, maroon-and-navy floral sides, sequins, a
  brick-red border, coin charms and tassels.

The kurta also has embroidered sleeve panels and banded border trims at the cuffs and hem. It
comes with matching wide pants that have a double border at the hem.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the white-dune photo.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:**
     - the base is a deep olive/moss green (about #313D25), not emerald, bottle green or khaki;
     - the yoke is beige, brick-maroon and navy, with a touch of gold sequins.
   - **Yoke panel:** a long rectangle from the shoulders to mid-chest, with a notched round neck.
     - **Top:** a brick-red band with cream paisley/bird shapes on each side of the notch.
     - **Centre:** a beige field with a large maroon teardrop paisley, its inner layers in red
       and cream.
     - **Sides:** dark navy and maroon floral sprays.
     - **Lower part:** small maroon flower clusters.
     - **Frame:** the whole panel is framed by a brick-red border with cream dots and small
       sequins, finished with a cream zigzag edge.
   - **Tassels and coins:** along the bottom edge hang a row of small round silver coin charms
     and **six tassels** in alternating maroon and beige. Two more tassels hang from the bottom
     corners of the side borders, so there are eight in total.
   - **Sleeves:** three-quarter sleeves, each with a long, narrow maroon embroidered panel down
     the outer arm. The cuffs have a wide banded border in cream, maroon and red with tiny
     flowers.
   - **Hem:** the same banded border in cream, maroon and red runs along the kurta hem. The
     kurta is hip length, with side slits.
   - **Pants:** wide-leg, olive green, with **two stacked bands** of the same border at each hem.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photo: same
   deep olive green, same beige-and-maroon paisley yoke with navy floral sides, sequins, coin
   charms and eight maroon and beige tassels, same sleeve panels and banded borders at the
   cuffs, hem and double pant hems."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows white dunes with driftwood and a jute rug, so these use other warm,
boho settings. Earthy and golden tones make the olive and maroon glow.

**1 · Rajasthani desert camp (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in front of a cream canvas tent decorated with
> hanging block-printed fabrics, brass lanterns and floor cushions in a desert camp at golden
> hour. Her hands are relaxed at her sides, so the yoke, all eight tassels and coins, the sleeve
> panels and every border are clearly visible. Warm, glowing light.

**2 · Pushkar bazaar lane**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking through a colourful Pushkar bazaar lane hung with
> embroidered bags, mirror-work fabrics and brass bells, mid-step, the tassels swinging, glancing
> at the camera with a soft smile. Warm afternoon light. Full length visible.

**3 · Boho café with rattan and plants**
> The model sits on a low floor cushion at a rattan table in a boho café with macramé hangings,
> trailing plants and terracotta pots, holding a cup of masala chai and smiling. The yoke and the
> tassels face the camera, and the pants' double borders show at her ankles. Warm, airy light.
> Captures her from head to ankle.

**4 · Terracotta wall with a wooden door**
> Full-length front view against a warm terracotta-orange lime-plaster wall with an old carved
> blue wooden door and a clay pot of dried grass. She tucks her hair behind one ear with a calm
> expression. The olive and terracotta contrast beautifully. Soft, warm daylight.

**5 · Mustard-field walk**
> The model walks along a narrow path through a blooming yellow mustard field at golden hour,
> running her fingers over the flowers, smiling down. The olive green stands out against the
> yellow. Warm backlight. Full length visible.

**6 · Lakeside ghat at sunset**
> The model sits on old sandstone ghat steps by a calm lake at sunset, with silhouettes of
> temples behind her, turned slightly towards the camera. The pants' double borders fan out on
> the step. Warm golden backlight. Captures her from head to toe.

**7 · Diwali evening with diyas (festive)**
> Full-length front view at dusk in a courtyard lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy lights,
> with a marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both hands at waist height,
> smiling softly. The sequins and the coin charms catch the candlelight.

**8 · Garba / Navratri mood (movement)**
> The model mid-twirl in a courtyard decorated with hanging marigold garlands and paper lanterns,
> arms raised gracefully as if dancing garba, the tassels and coins swinging out and the wide
> pants flaring. Slight motion in the fabric, but her face and the yoke stay sharp. Warm evening
> light. Full length.

**9 · Detail close-up, yoke and tassels**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The full yoke panel is in crisp focus: the brick-red neckline
> band, the maroon paisley on beige, the navy floral sides, the sequin-dotted border, the zigzag
> edge, the row of silver coins and the maroon and beige tassels. Soft side light shows the
> thread texture. Shallow depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, sleeve panel and cuff**
> A close-up of the model's arm and side. The narrow maroon embroidered sleeve panel, the wide
> banded cuff border and the kurta's hem border beside it are in crisp focus, with one corner
> tassel hanging. Soft, warm daylight.

**11 · Detail close-up, pant hems**
> A low close-up of the model's ankles and feet in jute slides. The two stacked bands of cream,
> maroon and red border on each wide pant hem are crisp and detailed. Soft daylight on a warm
> sandstone floor.

**12 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact co-ord set on a natural jute rug. The kurta is laid flat and
> smoothed, with the yoke, all eight tassels and coins, the sleeve panels and the hem border
> fully visible. The pants are folded beside it so the double hem border shows. Styled with dried
> pampas grass, smooth river stones, gold earrings and jute slides at the edges. Even, soft
> daylight. Catalogue quality.

**13 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera across golden
> sand dunes at sunset, the tassels swinging gently. Keep the top third of the frame as clean,
> soft, warm sky with no objects, so text can be added later. Warm light, true olive green, yoke
> clearly visible.
