# Black block-print chevron kurta set: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a black cotton straight kurta with a maroon pin-striped V-neck panel, maroon dotted
buta motifs, a white zigzag hem border and temple-triangle bands at the hem and cuffs, with plain
black straight pants (supplier design no. 6364).

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the courtyard photo. Crop off the green "D.no.6364" label at the
   bottom left first, or the AI may copy the text.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set (these are the details AI tools most often get wrong):
   - **Neck:** a short band collar with a V-slit framed by a maroon panel with fine vertical
     pinstripes. No buttons, no embroidery.
   - **Butas:** three teardrop shapes made of maroon-red dots, one at the centre front and one at
     each side, each with two maroon chevron lines under it.
   - **Hem:** four rows of double white zigzags on black, then a grey band with black triangles,
     then a maroon band with black triangles, and a thin white line at the very edge.
   - **Cuffs:** three-quarter sleeves with the same white zigzag, grey triangle and maroon
     triangle bands.
   - **Colour:** a deep matte black base with maroon (not bright red) and off-white accents.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photo: same
   maroon pinstriped V-neck panel, same three dotted butas, same white zigzag rows and
   grey-and-maroon triangle bands at the hem and cuffs. Do not change the pattern or colours."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a haveli courtyard with bougainvillea, so these use other
aesthetic settings. Light, warm backgrounds make the black and the white zigzags stand out.

**1 · Whitewashed Jaipur lane (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a narrow whitewashed old-city lane with a
> pastel pink door, a terracotta pot of bougainvillea and soft sunlight on the walls. Her hands
> are relaxed at her sides, so the V-neck panel, the three dotted butas and the full zigzag hem
> are clearly visible against the light background. Warm late-afternoon light, crisp detail.

**2 · Minimal limewash studio, editorial**
> Full-length front view against a warm cream limewash wall with a single arched niche, a cane
> stool and a tall ceramic vase of dried palm leaves. The model stands with one foot slightly
> forward and a confident, soft smile. Soft, diffused daylight with a gentle window-frame shadow
> on the wall beside her, not on the kurta. Clean, high-fashion aesthetic.

**3 · Café with cane chairs**
> The model sits at a small marble table in a sunlit café with cane chairs, white walls and
> potted plants, holding a cup of coffee and laughing naturally. The kurta's butas and zigzag hem
> fall across her lap, facing the camera. Bright, airy daylight. Captures her from head to ankle.

**4 · Mustard-field walk (colour contrast)**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking along a narrow path through a blooming yellow mustard
> field at golden hour, mid-step, looking over her shoulder at the camera with a smile. The black
> kurta and white zigzags pop against the sea of yellow flowers. Warm, glowing backlight. Full
> length visible.

**5 · Sandstone stepwell steps**
> The model sits on the warm golden sandstone steps of an ancient stepwell, with the geometric
> steps repeating behind her, turned slightly towards the camera with a calm smile. The zigzag
> hem border echoes the stepwell's patterns. Soft, warm afternoon light. Captures her from head
> to toe.

**6 · Indigo-and-white block-printing workshop**
> The model stands at a long wooden printing table in a bright artisan workshop, with carved
> wooden printing blocks, trays of dye and lengths of block-printed fabric drying in the
> background. She holds a carved wooden block in her hands and smiles down at it. Soft natural
> light from high windows. Full length visible.

**7 · Diwali evening with diyas (festive)**
> Full-length front view at dusk in a courtyard lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy
> lights, with a marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both hands at waist
> height, smiling softly. The warm candlelight brings out the maroon butas and the borders, and
> the black stays rich and detailed.

**8 · Bookshop corner**
> The model leans against tall wooden bookshelves in a cosy, sunlit old bookshop, reading an open
> book and glancing up at the camera with a soft smile. Warm, golden window light. A calm,
> intellectual mood. Captures her from head to ankle.

**9 · Detail close-up, neckline**
> A close-up from chin to mid-chest. The band collar, the V-slit and the maroon pinstriped V
> panel are in crisp focus, showing the fine stripes and the handloom cotton texture. Small gold
> jhumkas are just in frame. Soft side light, shallow depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, butas and hem**
> A close-up crop from the hips to the hem. The model's hand rests lightly at her side with a
> gold bangle. The dotted maroon butas with their chevrons, the four rows of white zigzags, and
> the grey and maroon triangle bands with the thin white edge are in crisp focus, so the
> hand-block texture shows. Soft daylight.

**11 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact kurta set on a natural off-white handloom cotton cloth. The
> kurta is laid flat and smoothed, showing the V-neck panel, all three butas and the full hem
> border, with the black pants folded neatly beside it. Styled with carved wooden printing
> blocks, gold jhumkas, a few marigolds and gold juttis at the edges. Even, soft daylight. No
> wrinkles, no tags. Catalogue quality.

**12 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera through a
> sunlit whitewashed archway, smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as a clean, softly
> blurred white wall with no objects, so text can be added later. Warm light, rich black,
> borders clearly visible.
