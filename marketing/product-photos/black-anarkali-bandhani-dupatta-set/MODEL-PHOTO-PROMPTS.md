# Black anarkali set with bandhani-print dupatta: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a black cotton empire-waist anarkali kurta with:
- a V-neck framed by an antique-gold printed border, and a deep plum sequinned inset;
- a gathered flared skirt and a tasselled tie;
- matching gold-border bands at the cuffs, hem and pant hems.

It comes with a black dupatta printed in a bandhani-style dot pattern, with a large paisley-tree
motif and wide border bands.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the archway photo.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colours:**
     - a soft matte black base;
     - borders in **antique gold/beige with maroon-brown** (a muted, printed look, not shiny
       gold zari);
     - the neck inset is a **deep plum-aubergine** (about #3F2833).
   - **Neckline:** a deep V outlined with a gold-and-maroon border with oval (ring) motifs. Inside
     the V, a squared plum inset has a small self-pattern and tiny scattered silver sequins.
   - **Empire band:** under the bust, two rows of the same border run across the front, and the
     V borders meet them. A thin black tie hangs from the centre, ending in a small black tassel.
   - **Skirt:** heavily gathered, flowing to mid-calf, with a wide hem border of two rows of the
     oval-ring band.
   - **Sleeves:** three-quarter, with a two-row border band at each cuff.
   - **Pants:** black, straight, with a two-row border band at each hem.
   - **Dupatta:**
     - a black field covered in a cream bandhani dot pattern arranged in diagonal lattice
       lines and diamonds;
     - a large rust-and-cream paisley/flower-tree (buta) motif near each end;
     - wide end borders and long-edge borders of cream, maroon and black block-printed
       bands.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photo: soft black
   anarkali, a gold-and-maroon oval-ring border on the V-neck, the empire band, cuffs, skirt hem
   and pant hems, a plum sequinned inset, a tasselled tie, and the black bandhani-dot dupatta
   with paisley-tree motifs and wide printed borders."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a sandstone archway courtyard with tiles, so these use other
aesthetic settings. Warm stone, soft whites and candlelight make the black and gold glow.

**1 · Palace corridor with soft light (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a long palace corridor with cusped white
> marble arches and warm sunlight streaming in. The dupatta is draped over her right shoulder and
> arm. The V-neck border, the plum inset, the empire band, the full gathered skirt and the
> dupatta's bandhani field and paisley motif are clearly visible. Soft, warm afternoon light.

**2 · Twirl in a courtyard (movement)**
> The model spins in a sunlit white courtyard with a marble fountain, the anarkali skirt flaring
> into a full circle that shows the wide hem border, and the dupatta floating behind her. She
> laughs joyfully. Slight motion in the fabric, but her face and the neckline stay sharp. Full
> length.

**3 · Dupatta spread open**
> Full-length front view against a warm off-white lime-plaster wall. The model holds the dupatta
> open behind her with both arms at shoulder height, like wings, so the whole bandhani dot
> lattice, both paisley-tree motifs and the wide printed borders are visible. The kurta's neckline
> and empire band show in the centre. Soft, even daylight.

**4 · Diwali evening, rangoli and diyas (festive hero)**
> Full-length front view at dusk in a courtyard decorated for Diwali. The model kneels beside a
> marigold-and-rose rangoli, placing a lit clay diya on it, and looks up at the camera with a warm
> smile. Rows of lit diyas and fairy lights glow behind her, and the dupatta drapes over her
> shoulder onto the floor. The warm candlelight brings out the gold borders and the plum sequins.

**5 · Stepwell steps at golden hour**
> The model sits gracefully on the golden sandstone steps of an ancient stepwell, her anarkali
> skirt fanned out across the step and the dupatta draped beside her. The repeating geometric
> steps rise behind her. Warm golden-hour light. Captures her from head to toe.

**6 · Lake palace balcony at sunset**
> Three-quarter view of the model leaning on a carved white marble balcony railing overlooking a
> calm lake at sunset, the dupatta falling over the railing and a soft breeze lifting its edge.
> Warm golden backlight with a gentle glow on her face. Full length visible.

**7 · Haveli doorway with brass lamps**
> The model stands in a carved wooden haveli doorway framed by hanging brass lamps and a marigold
> toran, one hand resting on the door frame and smiling softly. Warm, glowing evening light. Full
> length visible.

**8 · Wedding-guest mood, mehendi evening**
> The model sits on a low floor cushion under a canopy of marigold strings and fairy lights at a
> mehendi evening, laughing as she holds out her hennaed hand. The dupatta is draped over her
> shoulder and the skirt spreads around her. Warm, festive light. Captures her from head to ankle.

**9 · Detail close-up, neckline**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The deep V-neck with its oval-ring gold-and-maroon border, the
> plum inset with its self-pattern and tiny silver sequins, the two-row empire band and the black
> tie with its tassel are all in crisp focus. Soft side light shows the mulmul texture. Shallow
> depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, dupatta motif and border**
> A close-up of the dupatta held in the model's hand. The bandhani dot lattice, one large rust
> and cream paisley-tree motif and the wide stacked printed border bands are crisp and detailed.
> Soft, warm daylight.

**11 · Detail close-up, hem, cuff and pant hem**
> A close-up of the model's lower half as she steps forward. The wide two-row skirt hem border,
> the pant hem border and the edge of a gold-and-maroon jutti are in crisp focus, with her hand
> and cuff border just in frame. Soft daylight, true colours.

**12 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact 3-piece set on a warm ivory handloom cloth. The anarkali is
> spread out in the centre with its skirt fanned to show the hem border, and the neckline, inset
> and empire band are visible. The pants are folded to one side, and the dupatta is folded in soft
> pleats to the other side with a paisley motif and the borders showing. Styled with gold
> jhumkas, a brass diya and a few marigolds. Even, soft daylight. Catalogue quality.

**13 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera through a
> sunlit marble archway, the skirt swaying and the dupatta over one shoulder, smiling. Keep the
> top third of the frame as a clean, softly blurred warm arch and sky with no objects, so text
> can be added later. Warm light, rich black, crisp borders.
