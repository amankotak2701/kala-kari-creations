# Mustard Jaipuri cotton medallion 3-piece set: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a deep mustard / turmeric-ochre Jaipuri cotton straight kurta with:
- a V-neck yoke and long front side panels in a pale-yellow ikat-style floral block print;
- a plain mustard centre panel with two round mirror-medallion appliqués and tassels;
- printed flared sleeves.

It comes with matching pants and a mustard mulmul dupatta with spaced floral buti prints.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** crop your photo to just the model first. That removes the Kala Kari logo
   card, the Instagram tag, the gold leaf border and the paper butterflies, so the AI doesn't copy
   them. Then upload it.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:** a rich **mustard / turmeric-ochre** (about #A34D04 in shade, warm golden ochre in
     light), with the print in **pale butter-yellow** (about #FFE286). It isn't orange, brown or
     lemon yellow. All three pieces are the same mustard.
   - **Print:** an **ikat-style floral block print**. Rows of upright flower-and-leaf bootis and
     leafy sprays in pale yellow, with soft, feathery (ikat-blurred) edges.
   - **Neckline:** a V-neck. The upper bodice on both sides of the V is a **printed yoke** that
     ends in a straight line under the bust. The V is filled with a plain mustard inset dotted with
     **tiny gold sequins**, and the neckline is edged with a fine scalloped/picot trim.
   - **Front panels:** below the yoke, the **centre front is plain mustard**. On each side is a
     long **printed side panel** running to the hem, edged with a fine scalloped trim.
   - **Medallions:** down the plain centre hang **two round appliqué medallions**, one under the
     yoke point and one lower. Each is a printed fabric disc with a fine stitched edge and a **round
     mirror** at the centre, with a **pearl/bead drop and a small mustard tassel** hanging below.
   - **Sleeves:** printed, three-quarter length, slightly flared.
   - **Shape:** a straight kurta to the calf, with side slits. Matching mustard straight pants.
   - **Dupatta:** a mustard mulmul with **large pale-yellow floral butis** spaced apart and a
     printed border.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the set exactly like the reference photo: deep mustard
   with a pale-yellow ikat floral print on the yoke, side panels and sleeves, a sequinned V inset
   with picot trim, a plain centre panel with two round mirror medallions with bead-and-tassel
   drops, matching pants, and a mustard dupatta with floral butis."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference was shot against a grey curtain, so these use warm, aesthetic settings. Whitewash,
sandstone and marigolds make the mustard glow.

**1 · Whitewashed haveli courtyard with marigolds (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a sunlit whitewashed haveli courtyard with a carved
> blue wooden door and brass urlis of floating marigolds. One hand rests on her hip, as in the
> reference pose, and the dupatta drapes over the other shoulder. The printed yoke, the sequinned V,
> both mirror medallions with their tassels, the side panels and the pants are clearly visible. Warm
> late-afternoon light.

**2 · Haldi / mehendi ceremony (festive)**
> The model sits on a floor cushion under a canopy of marigold strings at a mehendi ceremony, smiling as
> she shows her hennaed hands, with brass bowls of marigold petals around her. The medallions and the
> printed yoke face the camera. Bright, cheerful light. Captures her from head to ankle.

**3 · Jaisalmer golden lane**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking through a golden sandstone lane in Jaisalmer with carved
> jharokha balconies, mid-step, glancing back with a smile. The warm stone echoes the mustard. Golden
> light. Full length visible.

**4 · Diwali evening with diyas**
> Full-length front view at dusk in a courtyard lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy lights, with
> a marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both hands at waist height. The
> candlelight makes the medallion mirrors and the sequins sparkle.

**5 · Mustard-field walk**
> The model walks along a narrow path through a blooming yellow mustard field at golden hour, the
> dupatta lifting in the breeze, smiling. Warm backlight. Full length visible.

**6 · Café with cane chairs**
> The model sits at a small marble table in a bright café with cane chairs and potted palms, holding a
> cup of masala chai and laughing. Airy daylight. Captures her from head to ankle.

**7 · Haveli jharokha window**
> The model sits on a cushioned stone ledge inside a carved jharokha window, looking out with a calm
> smile. Soft golden light streams through a jaali screen. The dupatta falls over the ledge. Captures
> her from head to toe.

**8 · Dupatta held open**
> Full-length front view against a warm cream lime-plaster wall. The model holds the dupatta open out to
> one side with her arm stretched, so its floral butis show, with the kurta fully visible. Soft, even
> daylight.

**9 · Detail close-up, neckline and medallions**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The V-neck with its picot trim, the sequinned mustard inset, the
> printed yoke and the two round mirror medallions with their pearl drops and tassels are in crisp
> focus. Soft side light makes the mirrors glint. Shallow depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, print and sleeve**
> A close-up of the model's hennaed hand on her hip. It shows the flared printed sleeve and the side
> panel's pale-yellow ikat floral bootis with their feathery edges and the fine scalloped trim. Soft
> warm light, true colour.

**11 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact 3-piece set on a natural off-white handloom cloth. The kurta is
> laid flat showing the yoke, the V inset, both medallions and the side panels, with the pants folded
> to one side and the dupatta folded in soft pleats to the other showing its butis. Styled with
> marigolds, a brass diya and gold juttis. Even, soft daylight. Catalogue quality.

**12 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera down a golden
> sandstone lane, smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as a clean, softly blurred wall and sky
> with no objects, so text can be added later. Warm light, true mustard, medallions clearly visible.
