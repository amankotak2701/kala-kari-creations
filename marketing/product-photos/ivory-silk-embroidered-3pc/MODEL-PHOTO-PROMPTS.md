# Ivory silk embroidered 3-piece dress: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a festive 3-piece set in soft ivory "coral silk" (lightweight textured silk blend with a soft sheen):
straight A-line kurta with a rich hand-embroidered yoke (silver zari, sequins, a deep rani-pink inset and pastel
thread florals), scalloped embroidered sleeve cuffs, scattered floral buttis; matching ivory pants; and a sheer
ivory dupatta with sequin buttis and a scalloped zari edge.

## Before you start
1. Upload all the photos in one Gemini chat (the store hanger photo shows the true colour and full kurta best).
   Add: "Use the photos only for the outfit; do not copy the person, the room, the hanger or any tags."
2. Paste the Base instruction, then one shot prompt per message. Ask for 4:5 (posts) or 9:16 (Reels/Stories).
3. If something is off, reply: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photos: same ivory silk, same
   embroidered yoke with the deep pink inset, zari and pastel flowers, same scalloped sleeve cuffs, same scattered
   buttis, matching pants and sheer ivory dupatta with sequin buttis."
4. Turn on Instagram's AI label if asked.

## Base instruction
> Create a photorealistic, luxurious festive fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala
> Kari Creations. The model wears the women's 3-piece silk suit shown in the reference photos. Use the photos only
> for the outfit; do not copy the person, the room, the hanger or any tags. Reproduce it exactly:
> - **Fabric and colour:** soft, lightweight silk blend with a fine slub texture and a gentle sheen, in warm ivory /
>   off-white with a hint of cream (not stark white, not beige, not yellow).
> - **Kurta neckline and yoke:** a round neck framed by a wide, ornate hand-embroidered yoke that comes to a point
>   at the chest: silver zari and sequin scroll work with small mirror-like stones, around a central V-shaped inset
>   of deep rani pink / magenta silk filled with silver floral zari embroidery. Around the yoke, small raised
>   thread-embroidered flowers in pastel pink, mustard yellow and sage green with silver centres.
> - **Kurta body:** straight, gently A-line, calf length, with side slits. Small scattered floral buttis (red and
>   pink thread with a tiny silver centre) across the body and sleeves.
> - **Sleeves:** three-quarter sleeves ending in a scalloped, petal-shaped cuff panel edged with silver zari, filled
>   with large raised pink, orange and green thread flowers on one side and a pink criss-cross (jaal) embroidered
>   patch on the other.
> - **Pants:** matching ivory straight pants in the same fabric.
> - **Dupatta:** a sheer, light ivory dupatta scattered with small silver sequin buttis and a few red floral buttis
>   near the edge, finished with a thin scalloped zari border, draped elegantly over one shoulder.
> Model: a graceful Indian woman in her late 20s, soft glam festive makeup, a small bindi, statement gold-and-pink
> kundan jhumkas, thin gold bangles, hair in a sleek low bun with a few jasmine flowers. Real fabric texture,
> true-to-life colour, sharp focus on the embroidery. No logos, text or watermark.
