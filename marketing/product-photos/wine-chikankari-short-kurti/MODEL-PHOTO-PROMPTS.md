# Wine chikankari short kurti: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a wine (burgundy-plum) short kurti with a tone-on-tone chikankari embroidered yoke,
tiny sequin highlights and a beaded edge, a notched round neck, scattered pink flower bootis and
three-quarter sleeves. It's styled with white jeans.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the teal-wall photo. Crop off the blurred block at the top right
   first, or the AI may copy it.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real kurti (these are the details AI tools most often get wrong):
   - **Colour:** a deep wine/burgundy-plum, not bright red, maroon-brown or purple.
   - **Yoke:** a rectangular panel of dense floral chikankari in the same wine thread
     (tone-on-tone), with tiny sparkling sequins, edged with a dotted, beaded border.
   - **Neck:** round, with a narrow notch slit in the centre. No buttons, no collar.
   - **Body:** small six-petal flower bootis in a brighter magenta-pink, scattered evenly and
     spaced apart. They are not a dense print.
   - **Shape:** hip length, straight with side slits, plain three-quarter sleeves.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the kurti exactly like the reference photo: same wine
   colour, same tone-on-tone chikankari yoke with sequins and beaded edge, same notched round
   neck, same scattered pink flower bootis, same length and sleeves."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a teal wall with a wooden table, so these use other aesthetic
settings. Soft neutrals, greens and warm light flatter the wine colour.

**1 · Sunlit café, cane chairs (hero)**
> Full-length view of the model standing beside a small marble café table, one hand resting on
> the back of a cane chair, with a soft smile at the camera. Behind her are white walls, potted
> plants and a large arched window with linen curtains. The chikankari yoke and the scattered
> bootis are clearly visible. Bright, airy morning light, true wine colour.

**2 · Minimal beige limewash wall**
> Full-length front view against a warm beige limewash wall, with a tall ceramic vase of dried
> pampas grass and a cane stool beside her. She tucks her hair behind one ear and looks slightly
> off-camera with a calm half-smile. Soft, diffused daylight and a gentle window shadow on the
> wall, not on the kurti. Clean, Pinterest-style aesthetic.

**3 · Bookshop by the window**
> The model stands in a cosy old bookshop with tall wooden shelves, holding an open book and
> glancing up at the camera with a soft smile. Warm, golden window light falls on the yoke so the
> sequins catch a little sparkle. Captures her from head to toe.

**4 · Rooftop garden at golden hour**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking across a rooftop garden with potted palms, string
> lights and a city skyline softly blurred behind her, mid-step and laughing. Warm golden-hour
> light makes the wine colour glow. Full length visible.

**5 · Florist studio**
> The model stands at a wooden work table in a bright florist studio, arranging a bouquet of pale
> pink roses and eucalyptus in a ceramic vase and smiling down at it. Soft, natural daylight, with
> buckets of flowers in the background. Captures her from head to ankle.

**6 · Diwali evening, fairy lights (festive)**
> Full-length front view at dusk on a balcony strung with warm fairy lights, with lit clay diyas
> along the railing and a small marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya at chest
> height and smiles at the camera. The warm glow makes the sequins on the yoke sparkle, while
> the wine colour stays true.

**7 · Coffee date, seated**
> The model sits on a velvet sage-green armchair in a stylish café lounge, legs crossed, holding
> a cappuccino with latte art and laughing at someone off-camera. The kurti's yoke and bootis
> face the camera. Warm, soft interior light. Captures her from head to ankle.

**8 · Office-casual, modern workspace**
> The model stands at a light wooden desk in a bright modern workspace with a laptop, a notebook
> and a small plant, holding a coffee mug and looking confident. A smart-casual look with white
> jeans and nude flats. Soft daylight from large windows. Full length visible.

**9 · Garden swing**
> The model sits on a wooden garden swing hung from a tree, in a lush green garden with soft
> bokeh of white flowers, smiling dreamily with her hands on the ropes. Soft, dappled afternoon
> light. Captures her from head to ankle.

**10 · Detail close-up, yoke**
> A close-up from chin to mid-chest. The notched neckline and the full chikankari yoke are in
> crisp focus, showing the raised floral stitches, the shadow work, the tiny sequins and the
> beaded scallop edge, with a few pink bootis beside it. Soft side light, shallow depth of field.

**11 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact kurti on a soft ivory linen cloth, laid flat and smoothed so
> the yoke and the scattered bootis are fully visible. A pair of folded white jeans sits beside
> it, styled with a few pale pink roses, small gold earrings, a ceramic coffee cup and nude flats
> at the edges. Even, soft daylight. No wrinkles, no tags. Catalogue quality.

**12 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera down a sunny,
> tree-lined street with soft green bokeh, smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as clean,
> softly blurred greenery and sky with no objects, so text can be added later. Warm light, true
> wine colour, yoke clearly visible.
