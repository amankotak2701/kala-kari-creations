# Oatmeal linen coral-motif kurta set: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** an oatmeal-beige linen-cotton straight kurta with eight small brick-red
coral-branch motifs, a band collar and a V-slit framed by fine pintuck-stitch lines,
three-quarter balloon sleeves gathered into cuffs, and matching straight pants.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the garden-archway photo.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:** a natural oatmeal/sand beige (about #C1A98F), like undyed linen. Not white,
     cream, khaki or grey.
   - **Motifs:** exactly **eight** small brick-red coral/seaweed-branch sprigs, each about the
     size of a palm, in this layout:
     - two on the upper chest, one each side of the neck slit;
     - one at the centre of the chest, below the slit;
     - two at the waist, one each side;
     - one at the centre of the lower front;
     - two near the hem, one at each side slit.
     They are spaced far apart, with plain linen around them. Not a dense print, and none on the
     sleeves or pants.
   - **Neck:** a short band collar with a rounded front edge. A long, narrow V-slit is framed by
     a V-shaped panel of about six fine, parallel dusty-rose stitch lines (pintucks), reaching to
     mid-chest.
   - **Sleeves:** three-quarter balloon sleeves, gathered into a plain, wide cuff just below the
     elbow.
   - **Shape:** straight, just above the knee, with side slits and a plain hem. Matching
     straight pants.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photo: same
   oatmeal linen colour, same eight brick-red coral motifs in the same places, same band collar
   with the pintucked V panel, same gathered three-quarter sleeves with cuffs, same length. Do
   not add more motifs."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a garden with a carved red-stone arch and brick wall, so these use
other calm, earthy settings that suit natural linen.

**1 · Coastal Goa verandah (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing on the whitewashed verandah of an old Goan
> Portuguese house with blue window shutters, terracotta floor tiles and a cane chair. Her hands
> are relaxed at her sides with a bright smile, so the pintucked V panel, all eight coral motifs
> and the balloon sleeves are clearly visible. Soft, warm morning light. True oatmeal colour.

**2 · Wheat field at golden hour**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking slowly along a narrow path through a golden wheat
> field at sunset, mid-step, running her fingers over the wheat ears and smiling over her
> shoulder. The warm gold light flatters the oatmeal linen. Full length visible.

**3 · Pottery studio, artisan mood**
> The model sits at a pottery wheel in a sunlit, rustic studio with shelves of terracotta and
> cream ceramics, smiling down at a small clay bowl in her hands, her sleeves pushed slightly up
> at the cuffs. The red motifs on the chest face the camera. Soft, warm window light. Captures
> her from head to ankle.

**4 · Minimal limewash and dried flowers**
> Full-length front view against a warm white limewash wall with a cane stool and a large
> ceramic vase of dried pampas grass and bunny-tail grass. The model tucks her hair behind one
> ear with a gentle smile. Soft, diffused daylight with a gentle window-frame shadow on the
> wall, not on the kurta. Clean, Pinterest-style aesthetic.

**5 · Beach shack at sunrise**
> The model stands barefoot on soft sand next to a simple wooden beach shack with linen curtains
> moving in the breeze, holding her hair back from her face and laughing. A calm sea and a soft
> peach sunrise are behind her. Gentle, glowing light. Full length visible.

**6 · Kerala backwater, houseboat deck**
> The model sits on the edge of a wooden houseboat deck on a Kerala backwater, with coconut palms
> reflected in the still water behind her, holding a steel tumbler of filter coffee and looking
> out with a peaceful smile. Soft, misty morning light. Captures her from head to toe.

**7 · Organic café, brunch**
> The model sits at a reclaimed-wood table in a bright, plant-filled organic café, with a
> ceramic cup and a bowl of fruit in front of her, laughing at someone off-camera. The kurta's
> chest motifs and pintucked neckline face the camera. Warm, airy daylight. Captures her from
> head to ankle.

**8 · Diwali evening with diyas (festive)**
> Full-length front view at dusk in a courtyard lit with rows of clay diyas, warm fairy lights
> and a marigold-and-white-flower rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both hands at
> waist height, smiling softly. The warm candlelight makes the linen glow, and the brick-red
> motifs stay true.

**9 · Detail close-up, neckline**
> A close-up from chin to mid-chest. The band collar with its rounded corners, the narrow V-slit
> and the panel of six fine dusty-rose pintuck lines are in crisp focus, with the two upper-chest
> coral motifs at the edges of the frame. The linen slub is visible. Soft side light, shallow
> depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, motif and sleeve**
> A close-up of the model's arm and side. The gathered balloon sleeve with its wide cuff and one
> brick-red coral motif at the waist beside it are shown, so the fine branches of the motif and
> the linen weave are crisp. Soft, warm side light.

**11 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact kurta set on a soft white cotton cloth. The kurta is laid
> flat and smoothed, showing the pintucked neckline and all eight coral motifs in place, with the
> oatmeal pants folded neatly beside it. Styled with a few sprigs of dried flowers, a woven straw
> bag, small gold jhumkas and tan kolhapuris at the edges. Even, soft daylight. Catalogue
> quality.

**12 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking barefoot towards the camera along
> a sandy path lined with tall dune grass, smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as clean,
> soft, hazy sky with no objects, so text can be added later. Warm light, true oatmeal colour,
> motifs clearly visible.
