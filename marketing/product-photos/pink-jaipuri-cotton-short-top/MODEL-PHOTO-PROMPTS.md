# Pink Jaipuri cotton short top: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a bright, saturated candy-pink (about #F27AAE) Jaipuri print cotton short top/tunic. White silhouette
floral print in vertical rows, a V-neck edged with black and hot-pink hand running-stitch embroidery and tiny silver
sequins, a high empire yoke with soft gathers and side tie-strings, a flared peplum body and plain three-quarter
sleeves.

## Before you start
1. Upload all the photos in one Gemini chat, including the neckline close-up (it shows the true colour and the
   stitching best).
2. Paste the Base instruction, then one shot prompt per message. Ask for 4:5 (posts) or 9:16 (Reels/Stories).
3. If the pink is pale or washed out, reply: "Pink is too pale. Use the bright, saturated candy pink of the
   reference, about #F27AAE. Not pastel, not baby pink, not dusty rose." If it comes out too dark or red, ask for
   "a brighter candy pink like the reference neckline photo".
4. If anything else is off, reply: "Keep the top exactly like the reference photos: same candy pink, white floral
   stem print in vertical rows, V-neck with black and pink running-stitch embroidery and tiny sequins, gathered
   empire waist, flared body, plain three-quarter sleeves."
5. Turn on Instagram's AI label if asked.

## Base instruction
> Create a photorealistic professional fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala Kari
> Creations. The model wears the women's Jaipuri cotton short top shown in the reference photos. Use the photos only
> for the garment; do not copy the person, the necklace, the hanger, the price/QR tags or the "Liki" brand labels.
> Reproduce it exactly:
> - **Colour (most important):** a bright, saturated candy pink / bubblegum pink, about hex #F27AAE (in direct light
>   up to #FD82BC). Cheerful and vivid with strong colour, NOT pastel, NOT baby pink, NOT blush, NOT dusty rose, and
>   NOT dark rose, magenta or red. The pink background dominates; the print is white.
> - **Print:** soft mul-like printed cotton with a white Jaipuri-style silhouette print with slightly soft,
>   hand-printed edges: upright rose blooms on a straight stem with paired pointed leaves, alternating with smaller
>   flower-cluster sprigs, arranged in neat vertical columns all over, including the sleeves.
> - **Neckline:** a medium-depth V-neck with a narrow self-fabric bound edge, decorated all around with delicate hand
>   embroidery: a line of short black running stitches (small dashes) beside a line of hot-pink running stitches,
>   with tiny round silver sequins scattered along the stitch line. No collar, no buttons, no lace.
> - **Yoke and waist:** a short, fitted empire yoke ending just under the bust, with a seam and soft gathers below
>   it, and thin self-fabric tie-strings at the sides of the waist seam.
> - **Body:** flares into a loose, swingy A-line/peplum body, upper-thigh length, with a soft slightly uneven hem and
>   small side slits.
> - **Sleeves:** plain three-quarter sleeves, straight and slightly loose, in the same print, no border.
> Styling: paired with slim white cotton pants or light-blue straight jeans. Model: a graceful Indian woman in her
> mid-20s, fresh natural makeup, small oxidised silver jhumkas, a few thin silver bangles, hair open in soft waves.
> Real cotton texture and soft creases, true-to-life colour (no washed-out or overexposed light), sharp focus on the
> top. No logos, text or watermark.
