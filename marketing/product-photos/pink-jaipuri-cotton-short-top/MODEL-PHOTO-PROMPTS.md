# Pink Jaipuri cotton short top: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a vivid rose-pink (about #E36A96) Jaipuri print cotton short top/tunic. White silhouette floral print in
vertical rows, a V-neck edged with thin black-and-white twisted piping, a high empire yoke with soft gathers and
side tie-strings, a flared knee-skimming peplum body and plain three-quarter sleeves.

## Before you start
1. Upload all four photos in one Gemini chat (the hanger photo shows the true colour and neckline best).
2. Paste the Base instruction, then one shot prompt per message. Ask for 4:5 (posts) or 9:16 (Reels/Stories).
3. If the pink is light or pastel, reply: "Pink is too light. Use the vivid saturated rose pink of the reference,
   about #E36A96. Not baby pink, not pastel."
4. If anything else is off, reply: "Keep the top exactly like the reference photos: same rose pink, white floral
   stem print in vertical rows, V-neck with black-and-white twisted piping, gathered empire waist, flared body,
   plain three-quarter sleeves."
5. Turn on Instagram's AI label if asked.

## Base instruction
> Create a photorealistic professional fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala Kari
> Creations. The model wears the women's Jaipuri cotton short top shown in the reference photos. Use the photos only
> for the garment; do not copy the person, the hanger, the price/QR tags or the "Liki" brand labels. Reproduce it
> exactly:
> - **Colour (most important):** a vivid, saturated medium rose pink / flamingo pink, about hex #E36A96 (ranging
>   #D96895 to #EC719A in daylight). Clearly a bold pink, NOT pastel, NOT baby pink, NOT blush, NOT magenta or red.
>   The pink background dominates; the print is white.
> - **Print:** soft mul-like printed cotton with a white Jaipuri-style silhouette print with slightly soft,
>   hand-printed edges: upright rose blooms on a straight stem with paired pointed leaves, alternating with smaller
>   flower-cluster sprigs, arranged in neat vertical columns all over, including the sleeves.
> - **Neckline:** a medium-depth V-neck finished with a thin piping of black-and-white twisted cord (a fine black
>   line with tiny white dots) running all around the neck edge. No collar, no buttons.
> - **Yoke and waist:** a short, fitted empire yoke ending just under the bust, with a seam and soft gathers below
>   it, and thin self-fabric tie-strings at the sides of the waist seam.
> - **Body:** flares into a loose, swingy A-line/peplum body, upper-thigh length, with a soft slightly uneven hem
>   and small side slits.
> - **Sleeves:** plain three-quarter sleeves, straight and slightly loose, in the same print, no border.
> Styling: paired with slim white cotton pants or light-blue straight jeans. Model: a graceful Indian woman in her
> mid-20s, fresh natural makeup, small oxidised silver jhumkas, a few thin silver bangles, hair open in soft waves.
> Real cotton texture and soft creases, true-to-life colour (no washed-out or overexposed light), sharp focus on
> the top. No logos, text or watermark.
