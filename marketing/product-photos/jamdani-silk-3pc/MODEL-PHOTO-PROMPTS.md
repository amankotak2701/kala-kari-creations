# Jamdani work silk 3-piece dress: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a soft champagne-beige silk 3-piece: straight kurta with a woven Jamdani-work yoke panel (rani-pink
paisley panels, blue arch band, pink-cream lattice), Jamdani sleeve borders; matching beige straight pants with a
woven hem border; and a cream silk dupatta with gold zari borders, pink woven buttis and a pallu of rani-pink,
light-pink and cream panels with large blue paisleys.

## Before you start
1. Upload all the photos in one Gemini chat (the hanger photo shows the full kurta and dupatta best). Add: "Use the
   photos only for the outfit; do not copy the person, the room, the hanger or any tags."
2. Paste the Base instruction, then one shot prompt per message. Ask for 4:5 (posts) or 9:16 (Reels/Stories).
3. If something is off, reply: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photos: same champagne-beige silk,
   same Jamdani yoke panel with two rani-pink paisley panels and blue arch, same sleeve borders, and the cream
   dupatta with gold zari border and pink, light-pink and cream paisley pallu panels."
4. Turn on Instagram's AI label if asked.

## Base instruction
> Create a photorealistic, elegant festive fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala
> Kari Creations. The model wears the women's 3-piece Jamdani work silk suit shown in the reference photos. Use the
> photos only for the outfit; do not copy the person, the room, the hanger or any tags. Reproduce it exactly:
> - **Fabric and colour:** soft, fluid silk with a gentle sheen, in a light champagne beige / greige (warm, pale,
>   not white, not yellow).
> - **Neckline:** a soft sweetheart-style notched V neckline edged with a thin blue-grey piping.
> - **Yoke panel:** a large rectangular woven Jamdani-work panel on the chest, framed by a magenta-and-cream dotted
>   border: at the top, a lattice of small pink four-petal flowers on cream; below it, a powder-blue arch band; in the
>   centre, a cream arch with a tall blue-and-pink flame/tree motif; on each side, a deep rani-pink panel with a
>   large blue paisley (keri) filled with tiny pink and cream flowers.
> - **Kurta body:** straight, calf-length, plain champagne-beige silk with side slits.
> - **Sleeves:** three-quarter sleeves finished with a cream woven border with small blue and pink paisleys edged in
>   magenta.
> - **Pants:** matching champagne-beige straight pants with a narrow woven pink-and-cream border at the hem.
> - **Dupatta:** cream silk with wide gold zari woven borders along the long edges, small rani-pink woven buttis
>   scattered across it, and a rich pallu at the ends: alternating rectangular panels in rani pink, light rose pink
>   and cream, each with a large blue-grey paisley filled with small flowers, then a row of pink, blue and peach woven
>   flowers, a magenta stripe and a gold zari band. Draped over one shoulder so the pallu falls in front.
> Model: a graceful Indian woman in her late 20s, soft festive makeup, a small bindi, gold-and-pink kundan jhumkas,
> thin gold bangles, hair in a low bun with a few jasmine flowers. Real silk texture and sheen, true-to-life colour,
> sharp focus on the weaving. No logos, text or watermark.
