# Coral pink handwork chikankari 2-piece suit: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a coral-pink straight kurta and matching wide pants (palazzo), hand-embroidered all
over in tone-on-tone Lucknowi chikankari. It has a dense floral yoke around a notched neck,
butis on the body, vine borders down the side panels, and floral borders at the kurta hem, the
cuffs and the pant hems.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the courtyard photo.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real suit. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:** a warm coral pink / watermelon (about #BB2D37 in shade, lighter coral-rose in
     light). Not hot pink, not red, not peach.
   - **Embroidery:** all **tone-on-tone**, with the thread a shade lighter, a soft rose-coral. It
     shows as raised texture. **No white or contrast thread.**
   - **Neck:** a round neckline with a narrow V-notch, and below it a plain narrow placket with
     a fine pintuck line, reaching to the bust. No buttons.
   - **Yoke:** a dense floral yoke of chikankari on both sides of the placket, from the shoulders to
     below the bust. It ends in a pointed, shield-like shape at the centre front. It's filled
     with paisleys, flowers and leaves in shadow work, jaali and murri.
   - **Body:** large floral butis (a flower with leaves, about palm-sized) spaced in rows down the
     front.
   - **Side panels:** a vertical floral vine border runs down each side of the front, from under
     the arms to the hem, framing the centre.
   - **Hem:** a wide border of a leafy floral vine (flowers and long leaves) along the bottom.
     It joins the side vines at the corners.
   - **Sleeves:** full-length, with an embroidered cuff band and a few butis on the sleeve.
   - **Pants:** wide-leg palazzo in the same coral, with floral butis on the legs and a leafy
     floral vine border at each hem.
   - **Shape:** a straight, knee-to-calf-length kurta with side slits.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the suit exactly like the reference photo: warm coral
   pink with tone-on-tone chikankari only, a notched neck with a plain placket, a dense floral
   yoke ending in a point, floral butis, side vine borders, leafy vine borders at the kurta hem,
   cuffs and pant hems, and wide-leg pants with butis."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a sandstone pillared courtyard, so these use other aesthetic
settings. Soft whites, greens and warm light keep the coral true and show the tone-on-tone work.

**1 · White marble palace corridor (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a long white marble corridor with cusped
> arches and soft sunlight streaming in. Her hands are lightly clasped at her waist with a serene
> expression. Side light shows the raised yoke, the butis, the side vines, the hem borders and the
> pant borders. Warm, soft afternoon light, true coral colour.

**2 · Jasmine garden with a swing**
> The model sits on a wooden garden swing hung from a tree, in a garden of white jasmine and
> frangipani, smiling dreamily with her hands on the ropes. The jasmine in her braid echoes the
> flowers around her. Soft, dappled light. Captures her from head to ankle.

**3 · Mehendi/sangeet afternoon (festive)**
> The model sits on a floor cushion under a canopy of marigold and rose garlands at a mehendi
> afternoon, laughing as she holds out her hennaed hand. The yoke and the palazzo borders show.
> Warm, cheerful light. Captures her from head to ankle.

**4 · Cream limewash wall with a window**
> Full-length front view against a warm cream limewash wall beside an arched wooden window with
> soft light falling across her from the side. A tall ceramic vase of white tuberoses stands
> beside her. She looks slightly off-camera with a calm half-smile. Clean, editorial aesthetic.

**5 · Lotus pond steps, morning**
> The model sits on stone steps by a temple lotus pond in the early morning, with pink lotuses
> behind her, holding a lotus flower. The palazzo borders spread across the step. Soft, misty
> light. Captures her from head to toe.

**6 · Diwali evening with diyas**
> Full-length front view at dusk in a courtyard lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy lights,
> with a rose-and-marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both hands at waist
> height. The warm glow makes the coral rich and the embroidery texture visible.

**7 · Sunlit café with cane chairs**
> The model sits at a small marble table in a bright café with cane chairs and potted palms,
> holding a cup of rose tea and smiling. Bright, airy daylight. Captures her from head to ankle.

**8 · Walking in a haveli verandah (movement)**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking along a sunlit haveli verandah with carved wooden
> pillars, the wide palazzo pants swinging and her jasmine braid over her shoulder, glancing back
> with a smile. Golden-hour light. Full length visible.

**9 · Detail close-up, yoke**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The notched neckline, the plain pintucked placket and the dense
> tone-on-tone floral yoke ending in its pointed shape are in crisp focus, with the jasmine braid
> just in frame. Low, raking side light shows every raised stitch. Shallow depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, kurta hem and side vine**
> A close-up of the lower kurta. The leafy floral vine border at the hem, where it meets the
> vertical side vine at the corner, and two floral butis above are crisp tone-on-tone texture on
> the coral. Soft side light.

**11 · Detail close-up, palazzo hems and juttis**
> A low close-up of the model's ankles. The wide palazzo hems with their leafy vine borders and
> butis, and the pink embellished juttis, are in crisp focus. Soft daylight on a warm stone floor.

**12 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact 2-piece suit on a soft ivory linen cloth. The kurta is laid
> flat and smoothed, showing the yoke, butis, side vines and hem border, with the palazzo pants
> folded beside it so their butis and hem border show. Styled with a string of white mogra
> flowers, oxidised silver jhumkas, stacked bangles and pink juttis. Soft side light. Catalogue
> quality.

**13 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera through white
> marble arches, serene and smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as a clean, softly blurred
> white arch and wall with no objects, so text can be added later. Soft warm light, true coral,
> chikankari texture visible.
