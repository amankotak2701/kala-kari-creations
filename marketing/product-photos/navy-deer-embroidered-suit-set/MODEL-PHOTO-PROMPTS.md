## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the garden-path photo.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:** a deep indigo navy (about #20223E) with silver-white thread. Not royal blue,
     not black, not gold thread.
   - **Yoke:** a rectangular panel below a round neck with a short V-notch. It holds:
     - **two deer facing each other** across a slim central tree of life;
     - two curling peacock-tail/paisley vines above them, on each side of the notch;
     - three star-flowers in a row below them;
     - small dots and tiny flowers filling the gaps;
     - a leaf-vine (fishbone) border framing the panel.
   - **Borders:** a matching silver leaf-vine trim runs down both side seams/slits to the hem,
     around the elbow-length cuffs and around the pant hems.
   - **Dupatta:**
     - a light grey-white slubby handloom field with grey-blue mandala-flower butis, spaced
       apart;
     - long edges with a navy stripe and grey temple-triangle (kangura) borders;
     - a wide pallu at the end with navy and grey-white stepped diamond geometrics, finished
       with navy tassels.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photo: deep navy
   with silver-white embroidery, two deer facing a tree of life on the yoke, leaf-vine borders
   on the yoke, cuffs, side seams and pant hems, and the grey-white mandala dupatta with temple
   borders, a navy geometric pallu and tassels."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a palace garden with bougainvillea and hedges, so these use other
aesthetic settings. Warm stone, soft whites and greens make the navy and silver glow.

**1 · Sandstone haveli courtyard (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a sunlit golden-sandstone haveli courtyard
> with carved jharokha balconies and a small marble fountain. The dupatta is draped over her left
> shoulder and falls down the front, and her hands are relaxed. The deer yoke, the side-seam
> borders, the cuffs and the dupatta's mandala field and geometric pallu are all clearly visible.
> Warm late-afternoon light.

**2 · Dupatta held open (shows the full weave)**
> Full-length front view against a plain warm-white lime-plaster wall. The model holds the
> dupatta open behind her with both arms stretched out at shoulder height, so the whole
> grey-white mandala field, the temple-triangle edges and the navy geometric pallu with tassels
> are visible. The kurta's deer yoke shows in the centre. Soft, even daylight.

**3 · Lake palace terrace at sunset**
> The model sits on the edge of a white marble terrace overlooking a calm lake with distant
> palaces at sunset, turned slightly towards the camera with a soft smile. The dupatta is draped
> over one shoulder, its pallu and tassels spilling over the marble ledge. Warm golden backlight.
> Captures her from head to toe.

**4 · Temple corridor, morning prayer**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking along an old carved-stone temple corridor with brass
> bells and marigold garlands, carrying a small brass puja thali, the dupatta lightly over her
> head and one shoulder. Calm, soft morning light streams through the pillars. Full length
> visible.

**5 · Winter morning, misty garden**
> The model stands on a misty garden path lined with tall cypress trees on a soft winter
> morning, the dupatta wrapped around her shoulders like a light shawl, holding a kulhad of chai
> and smiling. Soft, diffused cool light, with the silver embroidery gently glowing. Full length
> visible.

**6 · Diwali evening with diyas (festive)**
> Full-length front view at dusk in a courtyard lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy
> lights, with a white-and-marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both hands
> at waist height. The silver embroidery catches the candlelight and the navy stays rich.

**7 · Library with tall windows**
> The model sits in a vintage leather armchair in an old library with tall wooden bookshelves
> and arched windows, an open book on her lap, glancing up with a soft smile. The dupatta falls
> over the arm of the chair, showing its pallu. Warm golden window light. Captures her from head
> to ankle.

**8 · Wind-blown dupatta on a terrace (movement)**
> The model stands on a rooftop terrace with a softly blurred old-city skyline at golden hour,
> the dupatta lifting and flowing out to one side in the breeze, her hair moving and a joyful
> laugh. Slight motion in the dupatta, but her face, the yoke and the weave stay sharp. Full
> length visible.

**9 · Detail close-up, deer yoke**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The full embroidered yoke is in crisp focus: the two deer
> facing the tree of life, the curling vines above, the row of star flowers below and the
> leaf-vine frame. The silver stitches and the fabric's soft sheen are visible, with the edge of
> the dupatta at one side. Soft side light, shallow depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, cuff, side border and dupatta pallu**
> A close-up of the model's hand resting at her side. It shows the herringbone silver cuff band,
> the leaf-vine border running down the side seam, and the dupatta's navy-and-grey stepped-diamond
> pallu with its navy tassels falling beside them. Soft daylight, true colours.

**11 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact 3-piece set on a warm off-white handloom cloth. The kurta is
> laid flat and smoothed in the centre, showing the deer yoke and side borders. The navy pants
> are folded to its left, and the dupatta is folded in soft pleats to its right with the mandala
> field, temple edges and tasselled pallu showing. Styled with oxidised silver jhumkas, a brass
> diya and a few white jasmine flowers. Even, soft daylight. Catalogue quality.

**12 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera through a
> carved sandstone archway, the dupatta over one shoulder, smiling. Keep the top third of the
> frame as a clean, softly blurred warm wall and sky with no objects, so text can be added
> later. Warm light, true navy, embroidery clearly visible.
