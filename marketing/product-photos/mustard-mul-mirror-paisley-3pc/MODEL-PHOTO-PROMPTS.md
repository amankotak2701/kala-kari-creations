# Mustard pure mul mirror-paisley 3-piece dress: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a mustard-yellow pure mul 3-piece: a straight kurta with a keyhole round neck, hand-embroidered paisleys
with mirrors and colourful thread flowers on the yoke and on two side-slit panels, sequin-dot seam lines and lace
at the cuffs and hem; matching wide palazzo pants; and a soft mustard mul dupatta with scattered pink embroidered
flowers.

## Before you start
1. Upload all the photos in one Gemini chat (the mannequin photo shows the full outfit and the palazzo best). Add:
   "Use the photos only for the outfit; do not copy the person, the mannequin, the room or any tags."
2. Paste the Base instruction, then one shot prompt per message. Ask for 4:5 (posts) or 9:16 (Reels/Stories).
3. If something is off, reply: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photos: same mustard mul, keyhole neck
   with orange piping, mirror paisleys with pink and green flowers on the yoke and both side-slit panels, lace at
   cuffs and hem, wide mustard palazzo, and mustard dupatta with pink flowers."
4. Turn on Instagram's AI label if asked.

## Base instruction
> Create a photorealistic, cheerful festive fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala
> Kari Creations. The model wears the women's 3-piece pure mul suit shown in the reference photos. Use the photos only
> for the outfit; do not copy the person, the mannequin, the room or any tags. Reproduce it exactly:
> - **Fabric and colour:** soft, feather-light pure mul cotton with a gentle sheen, in a bright mustard yellow (golden
>   yellow with a slight green-olive undertone, like haldi; not lemon, not orange).
> - **Neckline:** a round neck with a narrow keyhole slit at centre front, edged with orange thread piping.
> - **Yoke embroidery:** around the neckline, hand embroidery of large paisleys outlined in orange and white beads /
>   French knots, each with a round mirror (shisha) inside, surrounded by pink stars, small pink and lilac flowers,
>   white daisies and green leafy vines, forming a U-shaped yoke ending in a white flower at the centre.
> - **Kurta body:** straight, knee to mid-calf length, with thin vertical lines of tiny silver sequins/beads along the
>   princess seams. At the hem on both sides of the side slits, an embroidered panel with mirror paisleys, pink and
>   white flowers and green vines, edged with orange piping.
> - **Sleeves and hem:** three-quarter sleeves and the kurta hem finished with a narrow mustard crochet lace edge.
> - **Pants:** matching mustard wide-leg palazzo pants with soft gathers.
> - **Dupatta:** soft mustard mul dupatta with scattered pink-and-orange embroidered flowers and small mirrors, draped
>   over one shoulder or both arms.
> Model: a graceful Indian woman in her mid-20s, fresh glowing makeup, a small bindi, oxidised silver or gold
> jhumkas, glass bangles, hair in a long braid with a gajra, or open soft waves. Real fabric texture, true-to-life
> colour, sharp focus on the embroidery. No logos, text or watermark.
