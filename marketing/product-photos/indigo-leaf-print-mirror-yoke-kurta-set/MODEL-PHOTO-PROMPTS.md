# Indigo leaf-print kurta set with mirror yoke: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** an indigo-navy cotton A-line kurta, hand block-printed all over with sand-beige
leaves and feathers. It has a plain cobalt-indigo U-shaped yoke with fine pintucks and silver
mirror embellishments, and comes with matching printed straight pants.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the courtyard photo.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colours:**
     - **Body print:** a deep indigo-navy ground with a sand/beige print. Not black-and-white,
       not grey.
     - **Yoke:** a plain, slightly brighter **cobalt indigo**, smooth with no print.
   - **Print:** large, overlapping leaves, feathers and fern sprays in sand-beige. They are drawn
     with dotted and dashed outlines (a hand block-print / dabu look), with tiny dot clusters
     between them. It is dense, all-over and multi-directional.
   - **Yoke:**
     - **Shape:** a wide scoop round neck, and the yoke curves into a rounded U shape that ends
       at mid-chest.
     - **Pintucks:** six fine vertical pintucks in the centre, three on each side of the middle.
     - **Mirrors:** small rectangular silver mirrors set in thin stitched frames, in four
       vertical columns (two on each side). Four diamond-shaped silver mirrors run down the
       centre line.
   - **Sleeves:** three-quarter, printed, with a turned-up cuff band.
   - **Shape:** an A-line kurta to the knee/lower thigh, with matching printed straight pants.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photo: indigo-navy
   with the sand-beige dotted leaf-and-feather print, the plain cobalt U-shaped yoke with six
   centre pintucks, rectangular mirrors in four columns and four diamond mirrors down the centre,
   and matching printed pants."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a garden courtyard with bougainvillea and a fountain, so these use
other aesthetic settings. Warm whites, ochres and greens make the indigo glow.

**1 · Whitewashed blue-door lane (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a sunlit whitewashed old-city lane beside an
> old indigo-blue wooden door and a terracotta pot of tulsi. Her hands are relaxed at her sides,
> so the cobalt yoke with its mirrors and pintucks, the leaf print and the matching pants are
> clearly visible. Warm, soft morning light.

**2 · Indigo dye yard (the craft story)**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking between bamboo lines hung with long lengths of freshly
> dyed indigo fabric in an artisan's dye yard, mid-step, glancing back at the camera with a smile.
> Clay dye vats sit in the background. Dappled morning sunlight through the hanging cloth. Full
> length visible.

**3 · Café with cane chairs and plants**
> The model sits at a small marble table in a bright café with cane chairs, white walls and
> potted palms, holding a cup of coffee and laughing. The yoke's mirrors catch a little light, and
> the printed skirt falls over her knees. Bright, airy daylight. Captures her from head to ankle.

**4 · Ochre limewash wall**
> Full-length front view against a warm ochre-yellow limewash wall with a wooden bench and a
> brass pot of marigolds. The indigo and ochre contrast beautifully. She tucks her hair behind one
> ear with a gentle smile. Soft, warm daylight with a gentle shadow on the wall beside her.

**5 · Sunflower field at golden hour**
> The model walks along a path through a field of tall sunflowers at golden hour, one hand
> brushing the petals, and smiles over her shoulder. The indigo pops against the yellow. Warm
> backlight. Full length visible.

**6 · Temple steps by a lake, morning**
> The model sits on old stone temple steps by a calm lake in the early morning, holding a brass
> diya, with soft mist on the water behind her. The kurta's flared hem spreads across the step.
> Soft, cool morning light. Captures her from head to toe.

**7 · Diwali evening with diyas (festive)**
> Full-length front view at dusk on a terrace lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy lights,
> with a marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both hands at chest height,
> smiling. The silver mirrors on the yoke sparkle in the candlelight, and the indigo stays rich.

**8 · Bookshop corner**
> The model leans against tall wooden bookshelves in a cosy, sunlit old bookshop, reading an open
> book and glancing up with a soft smile. Warm golden window light. Captures her from head to
> ankle.

**9 · Detail close-up, mirror yoke**
> A close-up from chin to mid-chest. The cobalt yoke is in crisp focus: the scoop neckline, the
> six fine centre pintucks, the four columns of rectangular mirrors in their stitched frames and
> the four diamond mirrors down the centre, with the start of the leaf print at the U-shaped edge.
> The oxidised choker is just in frame. Soft side light makes the mirrors glint. Shallow depth of
> field.

**10 · Detail close-up, print and cuff**
> A close-up of the model's forearm resting at her waist. It shows the turned-up printed cuff and
> the sand-beige leaves and feathers with their dotted outlines and dot clusters on the indigo
> ground. The hand-block texture is visible. Soft daylight, true colours.

**11 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact kurta set on a natural off-white handloom cloth. The kurta is
> laid flat and smoothed, showing the full yoke with every mirror and pintuck and the all-over
> print, with the printed pants folded neatly beside it. Styled with a carved wooden printing
> block, an oxidised silver choker, a few marigolds and ivory juttis at the edges. Even, soft
> daylight. Catalogue quality.

**12 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera down a sunny
> whitewashed lane with an indigo door, smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as a clean,
> softly blurred white wall and sky with no objects, so text can be added later. Warm light, true
> indigo, yoke mirrors visible.
