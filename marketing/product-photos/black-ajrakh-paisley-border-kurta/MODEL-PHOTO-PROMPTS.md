# Black Ajrakh-style paisley border kurta: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a black cotton straight kurta, hand block-printed in an Ajrakh/Bagh style in madder
red and sand-beige. The print is placed in zones:
- small booti motifs on the upper body;
- a band of madder-red polka dots;
- a row of large paisleys;
- a wide geometric border at the hem.

The sleeves repeat the border, and the neckline is edged with red mirror-shaped motifs. It's
worn with plain black straight pants.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the hallway photo.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real kurta. AI tools most often get the **order of the print
   zones** wrong:
   - **Colours:** a black ground with **madder red** (a deep brick red, not bright red) and
     **sand-beige**. Natural-dye tones, matte.
   - **Print zones, top to bottom:**
     1. **Neckline:** a wide scoop round neck edged with a ring of red teardrop/almond shapes
        with pale centres, like mirrors.
     2. **Upper body to hip:** a dense, even all-over pattern of small vertical oval bootis (a
        beige leafy outline around a red flower centre) in staggered rows, with tiny beige dots
        between them.
     3. **Below the hip:** a band of evenly spaced madder-red polka dots with beige rings.
     4. **Knee level:** one row of large beige paisleys (kairi) standing upright, filled with
        fine dotted patterns, each on a small leafy base.
     5. **Border:** three narrow bands with red-and-beige chevrons and diamonds, separated by
        thin stripes.
     6. **Hem:** a wide band of fine beige zigzag (herringbone) lines on black.
   - **Sleeves:** three-quarter sleeves with a large paisley on the upper arm, then the same
     chevron-and-diamond bands, then the zigzag band, and a small red motif at the cuff edge.
   - **Shape:** straight, mid-calf length, with side slits. Plain black straight pants with no
     print.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the kurta exactly like the reference photo: black with
   madder red and sand-beige, a red mirror-shaped neckline, small oval bootis on the upper body,
   then a red polka-dot band, then a row of large paisleys, then three chevron and diamond bands,
   then a wide zigzag hem band, the same border on the sleeves, and plain black pants."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference shows a plain indoor hallway, so these add aesthetic settings. Warm, light and
earthy backgrounds make the black, red and beige print stand out.

**1 · Kutch mud-house courtyard (hero, the Ajrakh story)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a sunlit courtyard of a traditional Kutch
> bhunga mud house, with whitewashed walls decorated with mirror-work and clay relief and a
> wooden charpai to one side. Her hands are relaxed at her sides, so the neckline, every print
> zone and the plain black pants are clearly visible. Warm, soft late-afternoon light.

**2 · Block-printing workshop**
> The model stands at a long wooden printing table in an artisan's workshop, holding a carved
> wooden paisley block in both hands. Trays of madder-red and black dye and lengths of
> block-printed cloth drying behind her. Soft natural light from high windows. Full length
> visible.

**3 · Whitewashed wall with a carved wooden door**
> Full-length front view against a warm white lime-plaster wall beside an old carved teak door
> with brass studs and a terracotta pot of tulsi. She tucks her hair behind one ear with a calm
> smile. Soft, warm daylight. Clean, editorial aesthetic.

**4 · Café with cane chairs**
> The model sits at a small marble table in a bright café with cane chairs, white walls and
> potted palms, holding a cup of chai and laughing. The paisley row and the border bands fall
> across her lap, facing the camera. Bright, airy daylight. Captures her from head to ankle.

**5 · Mustard-field walk (colour contrast)**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking along a narrow path through a blooming yellow mustard
> field at golden hour, mid-step, smiling over her shoulder. The black and madder-red print pops
> against the yellow. Warm backlight. Full length visible.

**6 · Sandstone stepwell**
> The model sits on the golden sandstone steps of an ancient stepwell, the geometric steps
> repeating behind her, and smiles softly. The kurta's zigzag hem echoes the stepwell's patterns.
> Warm afternoon light. Captures her from head to toe.

**7 · Diwali evening with diyas (festive)**
> Full-length front view at dusk in a courtyard lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy lights,
> with a marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both hands at waist height.
> The candlelight warms the madder red and the beige, and the black stays rich.

**8 · Old library with arched windows**
> The model stands between tall wooden bookshelves in an old library with arched windows, holding
> an open book and glancing up with a soft smile. Warm golden window light. Captures her from head
> to ankle.

**9 · Detail close-up, neckline and bootis**
> A close-up from chin to mid-chest. The scoop neckline's ring of red almond shapes with pale
> centres and the dense rows of small oval bootis with tiny dots are in crisp focus, with a gold
> jhumka just in frame. The hand-block texture is visible. Soft side light.

**10 · Detail close-up, paisley row and border**
> A close-up crop from the hip to the hem. The madder-red polka-dot band, the row of large beige
> paisleys, the three chevron-and-diamond bands and the wide zigzag hem band are all in crisp
> focus, with the model's hand resting at her side. Soft daylight, true colours.

**11 · Detail close-up, sleeve**
> A close-up of the model's arm bent at her waist. It shows the sleeve's large paisley, the
> chevron-and-diamond bands, the zigzag band and the small red cuff motif, with a thin gold
> bangle. Soft, warm side light.

**12 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact kurta on a natural off-white handloom cloth, laid flat and
> smoothed so every print zone, from the neckline to the zigzag hem, and both sleeves are fully
> visible. The plain black pants are folded beside it. Styled with carved wooden printing blocks,
> gold jhumkas, a few marigolds and tan juttis at the edges. Even, soft daylight. Catalogue
> quality.

**13 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera through a
> whitewashed archway in a sunny village lane, smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as a
> clean, softly blurred white wall and sky with no objects, so text can be added later. Warm
> light, true colours, print zones clearly visible.
