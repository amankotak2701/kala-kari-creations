# Olive cotton tunic with smocked yoke and white paisley embroidery: photoshoot prompts

Use in Gemini: upload the product photo as the reference, paste the **base description**, then **one** shot
prompt. Use a new chat for each image. Choose 4:5 for posts or 9:16 for reels/stories.

## Base description (paste before every shot)

> Create a photorealistic, high-end editorial fashion photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala
> Kari Creations. The model wears the exact tunic from the reference photo. Reproduce every detail exactly.
>
> **Fabric and colour:** soft, breathable, matte cotton in a deep olive / khaki-brown green (earthy, warm,
> like dried moss). It is not bright green, army green or brown.
>
> **Shape:** a straight short tunic ending at mid-thigh, with side slits, soft princess seam lines on the
> front and three-quarter straight sleeves.
>
> **Neckline and placket:** a short band (mandarin) collar with a small notch at the centre, and a short
> button placket with five round white shell-style buttons reaching to the bottom of the yoke.
>
> **Smocked yoke:** a rectangular yoke from the shoulders to the bust. On both sides of the placket it is
> worked in Canadian (lattice) smocking, a raised, puffy diamond-and-triangle honeycomb texture in the same
> olive fabric.
> - Tiny scattered silver sequins and small seed beads dot the smocking.
> - The yoke's two vertical sides are edged with a line of white hand running stitch (kantha dashes).
> - A white embroidered border sits along the bottom of the yoke: a row of small eight-petal flowers between
>   two lines of fine white zigzag / lattice stitching.
>
> **Sleeve embroidery:** on the outer side of each sleeve, from the shoulder down to the elbow, there is one
> large white thread-embroidered paisley (kairi) motif. It is made of fine lacy chain-stitch patterns:
> concentric scalloped rings, small flowers and dotted bands, curling into a tip near the elbow. Small white
> embroidery also sits near the cuff.
>
> **Pants:** plain ivory / off-white straight cotton pants, ankle length.
>
> Do not change the colours, flatten the smocking, or move or enlarge the embroidery. No prints, mirrors or
> extra borders. No text, no logos, no watermark. The model is a young Indian woman with a warm, natural
> smile, soft makeup, a small bindi, shoulder-length soft waves, oxidised silver jhumkas and tan leather
> juttis. 85mm lens, soft warm natural light, true-to-life colour, the smocking texture and white embroidery
> crisp. Make exactly one single photo, not a collage.
