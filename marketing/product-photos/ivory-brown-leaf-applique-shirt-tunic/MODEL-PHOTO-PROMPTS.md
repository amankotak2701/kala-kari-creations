Use in Gemini: upload the product photo as the reference, paste the **base description**, then **one** shot
prompt. Use a new chat for each image. Choose 4:5 for posts or 9:16 for reels/stories.

## Base description (paste before every shot)

> Create a photorealistic, high-end editorial fashion photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala
> Kari Creations. The model wears the exact tunic from the reference photo. Reproduce every detail exactly.
>
> **Fabric and colour:** soft, breathable, matte handloom cotton in a natural ivory / off-white with a fine
> slub and tiny natural flecks in the weave (khadi-like texture). It is warm and unbleached, not bright white.
>
> **Shape:** a relaxed, straight, shirt-style tunic ending at the hip, with a straight hem and small side
> slits.
> - **Collar and placket:** a short band (mandarin) collar and a full front button placket running from the
>   collar to the hem, with small round ivory 4-hole buttons evenly spaced.
> - **Sleeves:** three-quarter length, softly gathered into a narrow plain cuff band at the forearm.
>
> **Appliqué work:** two abstract leaf motifs in hand-cut cotton appliqué, with the edges neatly
> hand-stitched down. They are flat fabric shapes, not a print.
> 1. **Upper chest**, on the left side as you face the model: a large fan of four or five rounded, petal-like
>    leaves in deep chocolate brown. They radiate outwards from one point at the button placket, at
>    mid-chest. The leaves are decorated with small scattered ivory hand-stitched dashes (kantha-style running
>    stitch), and fine ivory lines radiate from the base where the leaves meet.
> 2. **Lower front**, on the right side as you face the model, just beside the placket: three long, wavy
>    leaves in a lighter mocha / taupe brown. They rise from near the hem and curve outwards towards the side
>    seam, each covered with small darker-brown hand-stitched dashes.
>
> **Pants:** wide, straight, beige / sand linen-cotton pants, ankle length.
>
> Do not change the colours or move, resize or add appliqué shapes. No prints, mirrors or extra embroidery.
> No text, no logos, no watermark. The model is a young Indian woman with a warm, natural smile, soft
> makeup, shoulder-length soft waves, chunky natural wooden bangles and tan leather flat sandals. 85mm lens,
> soft warm natural light, true-to-life colour, the appliqué stitches crisp and visible. Make exactly one
> single photo, not a collage.
