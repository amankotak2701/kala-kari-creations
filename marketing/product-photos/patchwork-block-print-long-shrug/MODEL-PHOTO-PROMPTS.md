# Patchwork block-print long shrug: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a long, open-front cotton shrug pieced from hand block-printed patchwork (Ajrakh,
Bagru and indigo dabu prints). It has a patchwork-square yoke, a gathered flared body of vertical
print panels, three-quarter sleeves, an indigo tie at the yoke and indigo hem and cuff bands.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photos:** upload **all three** in the same chat.
   - The photo of the model walking outside the Kutch mud house (mirror-work wall, shrug open over
     a black dress) is the main reference.
   - The hanger photo shows every patch and panel clearly.
   - The photo of the shrug worn in the shop shows how it falls on the body. Crop out the shop
     sign first.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real shrug (patchwork is where AI tools most often go wrong):
   - **Yoke:** squares of different prints, including a mustard-yellow floral, a cream with red
     vines, an indigo-and-white leaf dabu, a maroon floral and an olive geometric. They are
     squares, not stripes.
   - **Body:** gathered, flared **vertical** panels of different prints, not a single repeated print.
   - **Front:** open, with a thin indigo tie at the bottom of the yoke.
   - **Sleeves:** dark maroon with a small red diamond Ajrakh print, ending in a cream geometric
     band and an indigo band.
   - **Hem:** indigo and cream printed bands; the shrug is mid-calf length.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the shrug exactly like the reference photos: same
   patchwork squares on the yoke, same vertical print panels, same indigo tie, cuffs and hem
   bands. Do not simplify or repeat the prints."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your Kutch mud-house photo already covers the village hero, so these add new aesthetic settings.

**1 · Rann of Kutch, white desert at golden hour**
> Full-length front view of the model walking across the white salt desert of the Rann of Kutch
> at golden hour, with endless flat white ground and a soft pink-orange sky. A breeze lifts one
> side of the open shrug out behind her, showing the vertical print panels, while the black dress
> stays still. Low, warm side light, long soft shadow. Calm, cinematic mood.

**2 · Indigo dye yard, fabrics drying**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking between tall bamboo lines hung with long lengths of
> freshly dyed indigo and madder-red block-printed fabric in an artisan's dye yard. She looks over
> her shoulder at the camera with a soft smile. Dappled morning sunlight through the hanging
> cloth; earthy ground, a few clay dye vats in the background. Full length visible.

**3 · Terracotta pottery studio**
> The model sits on a low wooden stool in a rustic pottery studio surrounded by shelves of
> terracotta pots and clay vases, one elbow resting on her knee, looking thoughtfully off-camera.
> The shrug falls open and spreads on either side of the stool so the panels are visible, with
> the yoke patchwork facing the camera. Warm window light, earthy terracotta tones. Captures her
> from head to toe.

**4 · Minimal boho, limewash wall**
> Full-length front view of the model leaning lightly against a textured, warm beige limewash wall,
> next to a cane armchair and a tall ceramic vase of dried pampas grass and palm leaves. One hand
> holds the edge of the shrug slightly open to show the yoke and the tie. Soft, diffused daylight
> with a gentle shadow of a window frame on the wall beside her, not on the shrug. Clean, calm,
> Pinterest-style aesthetic.

**5 · Udaipur ghat at sunset**
> The model sits on old stone steps of a lakeside ghat in Udaipur at sunset, with the lake and
> soft silhouettes of palaces and temples behind her. She's turned slightly towards the camera
> with a relaxed smile, and the shrug's long panels spill down the steps beside her. Warm golden
> backlight with a soft glow on her face. Captures her from head to toe.

**6 · Indo-western street style**
> Full-length view of the model walking past an old exposed-brick wall with a hanging
> bougainvillea in a heritage city lane. She wears the shrug open over a plain white tee, blue
> straight-leg jeans and white sneakers instead of the black dress, with a tan leather sling bag.
> Mid-stride and laughing, the shrug swinging. Bright, natural daylight. Fun, modern energy.

**7 · Diwali rooftop, fairy lights (festive)**
> Full-length front view at dusk on a rooftop strung with warm fairy lights, with rows of lit clay
> diyas along the parapet and a marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya at chest
> height and smiles softly at the camera. The shrug is open over the black dress and glows with
> warm candlelight, showing true colours. Deep blue evening sky behind.

**8 · Art gallery, editorial**
> The model stands in a minimal white art gallery in front of a large abstract canvas in earthy
> rust and indigo tones, hands loosely in the shrug's sides, gazing slightly past the camera with
> a calm, confident expression. Clean white walls, a polished concrete floor, soft gallery
> lighting. High-fashion editorial mood. Full length visible.

**9 · Twirl in a sunlit courtyard (movement)**
> The model spins in a sunlit whitewashed courtyard with a single neem tree, the open shrug
> flaring out in a wide circle around her so every vertical print panel is visible, with her arms
> slightly out and a joyful laugh. Slight motion in the fabric, but her face and the prints stay
> sharp. Bright, warm afternoon light. Full length.

**10 · Detail close-up, yoke and tie**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The model gently holds the indigo tie between her fingers at
> the bottom of the yoke. The patchwork squares (mustard floral, cream vine, indigo leaf dabu,
> maroon floral) and the start of the gathered panels below are in crisp focus, and the silver
> choker is just in frame. Soft side light shows the cotton texture. Shallow depth of field.

**11 · Clean product shot on a hanger (no model)**
> This exact shrug hanging open on a natural wooden hanger against a warm off-white limewash
> wall, with a small trailing plant at one side and soft daylight from a window. It is centred,
> smooth and straight so every yoke square, panel, cuff and hem band is visible. No wrinkles, no
> tags. Catalogue quality.

**12 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera down a sandy
> village path lined with low whitewashed mud walls, the shrug open and swinging over the black
> dress. Keep the top third of the frame as clean, softly blurred warm sky with no objects, so
> text can be added later. Golden-hour light, true colours.
