# Navy handwork chikankari kurti: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a dusky navy cotton straight kurti, hand-embroidered in tone-on-tone Lucknowi
chikankari. It has:
- a densely embroidered jaali yoke with a long placket of fabric-covered round buttons;
- a front panel of floral sprays and tiny bootis, framed by embroidered vine lines;
- full sleeves with embroidered cuffs.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the studio photo. Crop out the white umbrella at the top left
   first, so it isn't copied.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real kurti. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:** a dusky navy (about #293654), a soft, slightly muted indigo-navy. Not royal blue,
     not black, not purple.
   - **Embroidery:** all **tone-on-tone**, in navy thread on navy fabric, so it shows as raised
     texture, not contrast. No white or silver thread.
   - **Neck and placket:**
     - a round neck with a short V-notch and fine piping;
     - below the notch, a narrow placket with about **12 small round fabric-covered (potli)
       buttons** in navy, set close together, reaching to just under the bust.
   - **Yoke:** a dense rectangular yoke from the shoulders to under the bust, covered in fine
     **jaali and murri** work: a small, close-set floral-lattice texture, and a plain band below
     it at the empire line.
   - **Front panel:** below the yoke, a long centre panel is outlined on each side by a vertical
     embroidered vine line (**bakhiya/keel-kangan**). Inside it:
     - large **star-like floral sprays** with long pointed petals and leaves, in a staggered
       column;
     - **tiny scattered teardrop bootis** (phanda) between the sprays.
   - **Sleeves:** full length, with an embroidered cuff band of fine horizontal rows and scattered
     bootis on the sleeve.
   - **Shape:** straight, calf length, with side slits.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the kurti exactly like the reference photo: dusky navy
   with tone-on-tone navy chikankari only, a notched neck with a placket of about 12 navy potli
   buttons, a dense jaali yoke, a front panel framed by vine lines with star floral sprays and
   tiny bootis, full sleeves with embroidered cuffs, and calf length."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference is a plain studio shot, so these add aesthetic settings. Soft side light is key
for tone-on-tone chikankari: it makes the raised stitches visible.

**1 · Lucknow heritage arches (hero, the chikankari story)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a sunlit Nawabi-era arcade with cusped
> cream-and-ochre arches and fine stucco work, like old Lucknow. Her hands are lightly clasped
> at her waist with a serene smile. Warm side light falls across the kurti so the jaali yoke,
> the potli buttons, the vine-framed panel and the floral sprays stand out in relief.

**2 · Window light, white linen room**
> Full-length view of the model standing beside a tall window with sheer white linen curtains
> in a minimal room with a wooden floor and a ceramic vase of white tuberoses. Soft, directional
> window light rakes across the kurti and shows every raised stitch. She looks out of the window
> with a calm half-smile.

**3 · Garden with white flowers, morning**
> The model walks slowly along a stone path in a garden of white jasmine and frangipani in soft
> morning light, mid-step, gently touching a flower and smiling. The dusky navy looks rich
> against the white blooms. Full length visible.

**4 · Café with cane chairs**
> The model sits at a small marble table in a bright café with cane chairs and potted palms,
> holding a cup of chai and laughing. The yoke and the potli buttons face the camera. Soft, airy
> daylight. Captures her from head to ankle.

**5 · Old haveli courtyard with a jharokha**
> The model sits on a cushioned stone ledge inside a carved jharokha window, knees drawn slightly
> up, looking out with a calm expression. Soft golden light through a jaali screen casts gentle
> patterns on the wall, not on the kurti. Captures her from head to toe.

**6 · Lakeside at sunset**
> Three-quarter view of the model standing on old stone ghat steps by a calm lake at sunset,
> turned towards the camera with a soft smile. Warm golden light rims her silhouette while the
> embroidery stays visible. Full length visible.

**7 · Diwali evening with diyas (festive)**
> Full-length front view at dusk in a courtyard lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy lights,
> with a white-and-marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both hands at waist
> height. The warm candlelight shows the chikankari texture, and the navy stays true.

**8 · Library with tall shelves**
> The model stands between tall wooden bookshelves in an old library with arched windows, holding
> an open book and glancing up with a soft smile. Warm golden window light. Captures her from head
> to ankle.

**9 · Detail close-up, yoke and potli buttons**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The piped notched neckline, the row of about twelve navy potli
> buttons and the dense jaali and murri floral lattice on both sides of the yoke are in crisp
> focus, with an oxidised jhumka just in frame. Low, raking side light shows every raised stitch.
> Shallow depth of field.

**10 · Macro, floral spray and bootis**
> An extreme close-up of the front panel. One star-like floral spray with its long pointed petals,
> its raised centre and slender leaves, the tiny teardrop bootis around it and the vine-line border
> at one side are shown as tone-on-tone navy texture on the cotton. Raking side light, very shallow
> depth of field.

**11 · Detail close-up, cuff**
> A close-up of the model's hands lightly clasped at her waist, showing the full-sleeve cuff band
> of fine horizontal stitch rows and a few scattered bootis on the sleeve. Soft side light, true
> colour.

**12 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact kurti on a soft ivory linen cloth, laid flat and smoothed so
> the notched neck, the potli buttons, the jaali yoke, the vine-framed front panel with its
> floral sprays and the sleeves are fully visible. Styled with white tuberoses, oxidised silver
> jhumkas and a small embroidery hoop with navy thread at the edges. Soft side light to show the
> texture. Catalogue quality.

**13 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera through cream
> heritage arches, serene and smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as a clean, softly blurred
> cream arch and wall with no objects, so text can be added later. Soft warm side light, true
> navy, embroidery texture visible.
