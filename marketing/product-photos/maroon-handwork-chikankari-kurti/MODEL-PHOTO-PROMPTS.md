# Maroon handwork chikankari kurti: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a deep maroon straight long kurti, hand-embroidered in white Lucknowi chikankari.
It has:
- a dense floral yoke around a notched neck, with a placket of tiny fabric buttons;
- scattered leaf bootis all over the body and sleeves;
- embroidered cuffs and side seams;
- a wide geometric chikankari border at the hem.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the studio photo.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real kurti. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:** a deep maroon / oxblood red (about #60151F) with **pure white** thread. Not
     bright red, not wine-purple, not cream or silver thread.
   - **Neck:** round, with a narrow V-notch slit, and below it a narrow placket with a row of
     tiny maroon fabric (potli) buttons, set close together, reaching to just under the bust.
   - **Yoke:** densely filled with large white flowers in raised **tepchi and murri** work (many
     pointed petals around a knotted centre), with fern-like leaf sprays. It covers both sides of
     the placket from shoulder to mid-chest and curves down in a soft U shape below the placket.
   - **Body:** evenly spaced **leaf bootis** (a small curled leaf or paisley sprig, about the
     size of a thumb) in staggered rows on the front and on the sleeves. Plenty of plain maroon
     shows between them.
   - **Side seams and cuffs:** a narrow white embroidered line runs down each side seam, and
     each cuff has a narrow white embroidered band.
   - **Hem border:** a wide border, from top to bottom:
     1. a row of slanted (diagonal) dash stitches;
     2. a row of white four-petal flowers (butterfly shapes);
     3. a thin line;
     4. two more rows of four-petal flowers.
   - **Shape:** straight, ankle-to-calf length, full-length sleeves, side slits.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the kurti exactly like the reference photo: deep maroon
   with pure white chikankari, a notched neck with a tiny-button placket, a dense floral yoke,
   evenly spaced leaf bootis, white lines on the side seams and cuffs, and the wide hem border of
   slanted stitches and rows of four-petal flowers."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference is a plain studio shot, so these add aesthetic settings. Warm creams, soft greens
and candlelight make the maroon and the white chikankari glow.

**1 · Lucknow Nawabi arches (hero, the chikankari story)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a sunlit Nawabi-era arcade with cusped cream
> arches and fine stucco work, like old Lucknow. Her hands are lightly clasped at her waist with
> a serene expression. The floral yoke, the button placket, the leaf bootis, the side-seam lines
> and the full hem border are clearly visible. Warm, soft afternoon light.

**2 · Diwali evening with diyas (festive hero)**
> Full-length front view at dusk in a courtyard lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy
> lights, with a marigold-and-white-flower rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both
> hands at waist height, smiling softly. The warm glow makes the maroon rich and the white
> chikankari bright.

**3 · White jasmine garden, morning**
> The model walks slowly along a stone path in a garden of white jasmine and frangipani in soft
> morning light, gently touching a flower, mid-step. The white blooms echo the chikankari. Full
> length visible.

**4 · Haveli doorway with marigold toran**
> The model stands in a carved wooden haveli doorway hung with a marigold-and-mango-leaf toran,
> one hand resting on the door frame, smiling softly. Warm evening light. Full length visible.

**5 · Cream limewash wall with dried flowers**
> Full-length front view against a warm cream limewash wall, with a tall ceramic vase of dried
> pampas grass and a wooden stool. She looks slightly off-camera with a calm half-smile, hands
> clasped at her waist. Soft, diffused daylight. Clean, editorial aesthetic.

**6 · Temple steps by a lake**
> The model sits on old stone ghat steps by a calm lake in the early morning, holding a brass
> diya, with soft mist on the water. The hem border spreads across the step. Soft morning light.
> Captures her from head to toe.

**7 · Wedding-guest mood, mehendi evening**
> The model sits on a floor cushion under a canopy of marigold strings and fairy lights at a
> mehendi evening, laughing as she holds out her hennaed hand. The yoke and bootis face the
> camera. Warm, festive light. Captures her from head to ankle.

**8 · Old library with arched windows**
> The model stands between tall wooden bookshelves in an old library with arched windows, holding
> an open book and glancing up with a soft smile. Warm golden window light. Captures her from head
> to ankle.

**9 · Detail close-up, yoke and button placket**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The notched neckline, the row of tiny maroon potli buttons and
> the dense white floral yoke in raised tepchi and murri work are in crisp focus, with the
> oxidised choker just in frame. Soft side light shows the raised stitches. Shallow depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, hem border**
> A close-up of the lower kurti. The row of slanted dash stitches and the three rows of white
> four-petal flowers in the hem border, with a few leaf bootis above and a side-seam line at one
> edge, are crisp and detailed. Soft daylight, true colours.

**11 · Detail close-up, sleeve and cuff**
> A close-up of the model's hands clasped at her waist, showing the full sleeve with its leaf
> bootis, the white embroidered cuff band and the oxidised cuff bracelet. Soft side light.

**12 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact kurti on a soft ivory linen cloth, laid flat and smoothed so
> the yoke, the button placket, the leaf bootis, the side-seam lines, the sleeves and the full hem
> border are visible. Styled with white jasmine, an oxidised silver choker and a small embroidery
> hoop with white thread at the edges. Even, soft daylight. Catalogue quality.

**13 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera through cream
> heritage arches, serene and smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as a clean, softly blurred
> cream arch and wall with no objects, so text can be added later. Warm light, true maroon, crisp
> white chikankari.
