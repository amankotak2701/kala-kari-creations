# Royal blue leheriya and bandhani Jaipuri cotton 3-piece set: photoshoot prompts

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the photo of the model holding the dupatta open against the white
   curtain.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:** a rich **royal / cobalt blue** (about #365180 in shade, brighter cobalt in light)
     with **white** print. It isn't navy, black or sky blue.
   - **Kurta print:** white **leheriya**: thin, slightly wavy diagonal stripes running from the
     upper left down to the lower right, evenly spaced, over the body and sleeves.
   - **Neck:** a V-neck. Each side of the V is framed by a wide placket panel of fine **vertical
     white pinstripes**, set at an angle to the body stripes. The two panels meet at a point at
     the chest, where a small **blue fabric bow** sits.
   - **Buttons:** a short row of **small round white buttons** runs down the outer edge of each
     placket panel, five on the left side and two or three on the right.
   - **Cuffs:** three-quarter sleeves with turned-up **pinstriped cuffs**.
   - **Shape:** a straight kurta, just below the knee, with side slits.
   - **Pants:** blue, straight, covered in small white **bandhani dots** (tiny squares with a
     dark centre). A wider hem border has rows of **bandhani rings**.
   - **Dupatta:** soft blue mulmul in alternating vertical bands:
     - wide bands of **bandhani mandala** (rosettes made of tiny white dots, with dotted
       borders);
     - **leheriya** diagonal stripes between them.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the set exactly like the reference photo: royal blue with
   white print, a leheriya diagonal-stripe kurta, pinstriped V-neck plackets with small white
   buttons and a fabric bow, pinstriped cuffs, bandhani-dot pants with a ring border, and a dupatta
   of bandhani mandala bands and leheriya stripes."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a white curtain backdrop with a jute rug, so these use other aesthetic
settings. Leheriya and bandhani are Rajasthani crafts, and warm sandstone, whitewash and marigolds
make the blue pop.

**1 · Jaisalmer golden sandstone lane (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing in a narrow golden sandstone lane in Jaisalmer with
> carved jharokha balconies above. The dupatta is draped over one shoulder and her hands are relaxed,
> so the leheriya kurta, the pinstriped V-neck with its buttons and bow, the bandhani pants and the
> dupatta's mandala bands are clearly visible. Warm late-afternoon light.

**2 · Dupatta held open (your reference pose)**
> Full-length front view against a warm white lime-plaster wall. The model holds the dupatta open out
> to one side with her arm stretched, so its alternating bandhani mandala and leheriya bands show.
> Soft, even daylight.

**3 · Whitewashed courtyard with marigolds**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking through a sunlit whitewashed courtyard with brass urlis of
> floating marigolds and a carved blue wooden door, mid-step, glancing back with a smile. Bright,
> warm light. Full length visible.

**4 · Thar desert dunes at golden hour**
> Full-length front view of the model walking across golden sand dunes at sunset, the dupatta lifting
> in the breeze behind her. The royal blue glows against the golden sand. Warm backlight.

**5 · Bandhani tie-dye workshop (the craft story)**
> The model stands in a bright Rajasthani tie-dye workshop with lengths of colourful bandhani and
> leheriya fabric hanging behind her, holding a length of freshly tied fabric and smiling. Soft
> natural light from high windows. Full length visible.

**6 · Café with cane chairs**
> The model sits at a small marble table in a bright café with cane chairs and potted palms, holding
> a cup of chai and laughing. The V-neck plackets, the buttons and the bow face the camera. Airy
> daylight. Captures her from head to ankle.

**7 · Haveli doorway with marigold toran**
> The model stands in a carved wooden haveli doorway hung with a marigold-and-mango-leaf toran, one
> hand resting on the frame, smiling softly. Warm afternoon light. Full length visible.

**8 · Diwali evening with diyas (festive)**
> Full-length front view at dusk in a courtyard lit with rows of clay diyas and warm fairy lights,
> with a marigold rangoli at her feet. She holds a lit brass diya in both hands at waist height. The
> warm glow makes the white print crisp and the blue rich.

**9 · Detail close-up, V-neck plackets**
> A close-up from chin to waist. The V-neck, the two pinstriped placket panels at an angle to the
> leheriya stripes, the small white buttons and the blue fabric bow at the point are in crisp focus.
> Soft side light shows the cotton texture. Shallow depth of field.

**10 · Detail close-up, pants and dupatta**
> A close-up of the model's lower legs and hand. The bandhani-dot pants with their ring border and a
> fold of the dupatta with its mandala rosettes and leheriya stripes are crisp. Soft daylight, true
> colours.

**11 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact 3-piece set on a natural off-white handloom cloth. The kurta is
> laid flat showing the leheriya stripes, the V-neck plackets, the buttons, the bow and the cuffs,
> with the bandhani pants folded to one side showing the ring border, and the dupatta folded in soft
> pleats to the other showing its mandala and stripe bands. Styled with marigolds and thin gold
> bangles. Even, soft daylight. Catalogue quality.

**12 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera down a golden
> sandstone lane, smiling. Keep the top third of the frame as a clean, softly blurred sandstone wall
> and sky with no objects, so text can be added later. Warm light, true royal blue, prints clearly
> visible.
