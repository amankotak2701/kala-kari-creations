# Black contrast-topstitch shirt kurta set: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a jet-black cotton shirt-style long kurta with a pointed collar, a full button
placket with pearl buttons, and off-white double topstitching that outlines a bib yoke, panel
lines and cuffs. It comes with black straight pants that have topstitched hems.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the office photo.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:** true jet black with off-white/cream thread. Not charcoal, navy or brown, and not
     bright white thread.
   - **Topstitching:** always **double** parallel lines, thin and neat, about 3 mm apart, never a
     single thick stripe or piping. It appears on:
     - the collar edge;
     - both sides of the placket;
     - two horizontal lines across the chest that form a bib yoke;
     - vertical panel lines that run down to the hem;
     - the cuffs and sleeve seam;
     - the pant hems.
   - **Buttons:** small round off-white pearl buttons, evenly spaced, from the collar all the way
     down to the hem.
   - **Shape:** a fitted bodice that flares into an A-line skirt below the waist, a mid-calf
     length, side slits, three-quarter sleeves ending just below the elbow.
   - **Pants:** black, straight, ankle-cropped, with two rows of double off-white topstitching
     at the hem.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photo: jet black
   with thin off-white double topstitching on the collar, placket, bib yoke, panel lines, cuffs
   and pant hems, pearl buttons from collar to hem, same A-line length. No piping, no prints."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

warm or textured backgrounds make the black and the white stitch lines stand out.

> bib yoke and every topstitch line down to the hem are clearly visible against the white. Soft,
> even gallery light.
