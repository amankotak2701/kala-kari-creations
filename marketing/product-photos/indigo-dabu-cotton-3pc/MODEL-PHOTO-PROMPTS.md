# Indigo Dabu cotton 3-piece dress: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a pure Dabu-print cotton 3-piece in deep indigo with white floral buttas and fine white pinstripes: a
straight kurta with an off-white placket panel carrying indigo appliqué geometry (triangle, circle, inverted
triangle) edged with small metal-bead danglers; matching indigo printed pants; and an off-white textured cotton
dupatta with indigo leaf buttas and a wide indigo printed end panel.

## Before you start
1. Upload all the photos in one Gemini chat (the hanger photo shows the full kurta and placket best). Add: "Use the
   photos only for the outfit; do not copy the person, the room, the hanger or any tags."
2. Paste the Base instruction, then one shot prompt per message. Ask for 4:5 (posts) or 9:16 (Reels/Stories).
3. If something is off, reply: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photos: same deep indigo cotton with white
   floral buttas and white pinstripes, off-white placket with indigo triangle, circle and inverted triangle and bead
   danglers, matching printed pants, and the off-white dupatta with indigo leaf buttas and indigo printed end panel."
4. Turn on Instagram's AI label if asked.

## Base instruction
> Create a photorealistic fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala Kari Creations. The
> model wears the women's 3-piece Dabu-print cotton suit shown in the reference photos. Use the photos only for the
> outfit; do not copy the person, the room, the hanger or any tags. Reproduce it exactly:
> - **Fabric and print:** soft, breathable pure cotton in a deep indigo / navy blue, printed all over with small white
>   upright floral buttas (a little flower stem with leaves) arranged in neat vertical rows, separated by fine white
>   dotted pinstripes running top to bottom. Slightly soft, hand-printed edges like Dabu / indigo block print.
> - **Neckline:** a simple round neck.
> - **Front placket panel:** a long rectangular off-white textured cotton panel from the neckline to mid-chest, with
>   indigo appliqué shapes stacked down the centre: a triangle at the top, a circle in the middle and an inverted
>   triangle at the bottom, each with tiny mirrors/sequins. The panel edges are trimmed with a row of small dangling
>   metal and coloured beads.
> - **Kurta body:** straight, calf-length, with side slits.
> - **Sleeves:** three-quarter sleeves in the same print.
> - **Pants:** matching indigo straight pants in the same white floral and pinstripe print.
> - **Dupatta:** off-white / natural cream textured cotton (kota-like, with fine woven stripes and slubs), with large
>   indigo block-printed leaf buttas, and at each end a wide indigo panel printed with white florals and stripes,
>   edged with a row of indigo temple-triangle motifs. Draped over one shoulder or across both arms.
> Model: a graceful Indian woman in her mid-20s, natural makeup, a small black bindi, oxidised silver jhumkas and a
> few silver bangles, hair in a low bun or a long side braid. Real cotton texture, true-to-life colour, sharp focus
> on the outfit. No logos, text or watermark.

## Shot 1: Hero, full length
> Full-length front view in a sunlit courtyard with a whitewashed lime-plaster wall, a blue wooden door, terracotta
> pots and a neem tree, soft golden-hour light. Dupatta over one shoulder, a warm smile. Tan kolhapuri sandals.

## Shot 2: Artisan / craft setting
> Three-quarter view in a rustic block-printing workshop with wooden printing blocks, indigo-dyed fabric drying on
> lines and a wooden table, touching a carved wooden block. Authentic handcrafted mood. Soft daylight.

## Shot 3: Office / everyday
> Full-length walking shot in a bright modern-ethnic café or co-working space with cane chairs, plants and a white
> wall, holding a jute tote and a coffee. Smart everyday look.

## Shot 4: Close-up, placket detail
> Close-up from the shoulders to the waist, dupatta moved aside. Sharp detail on the off-white placket with the indigo
> triangle, circle and inverted triangle, the tiny mirrors and the bead danglers, plus the floral pinstripe print.
> Soft blurred cream background.

## Shot 5: Dupatta spread
> Full-length front view against a warm white wall, holding the dupatta open with both arms so the cream textured
> fabric, indigo leaf buttas and the indigo printed end panel are clearly visible.

## Shot 6 (optional): Seated with chai
> Seated on a charpai or cane chair on a sunlit verandah with a kulhad of chai, dupatta draped over her lap. Calm,
> earthy, homely mood.
