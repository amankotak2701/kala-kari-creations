# Ivory cotton tunic with black lotus lace appliqué: photoshoot prompts

Use in Gemini: upload the product photo as the reference, paste the **base description**, then **one** shot
prompt. Use a new chat for each image. Choose 4:5 for posts or 9:16 for reels/stories.

## Base description (paste before every shot)

> Create a photorealistic, high-end editorial fashion photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala
> Kari Creations. The model wears the exact tunic from the reference photo. Reproduce every detail exactly.
>
> **Fabric and colour:** soft, breathable, matte handloom cotton with a fine slub texture, in a natural
> ivory/off-white (warm, unbleached, not bright white).
>
> **Shape:** a straight short tunic ending at the upper thigh, with small side slits and three-quarter
> sleeves that are slightly wide at the cuff.
>
> **Neckline:** a deep V-neck framed by a wide band of the same ivory fabric. The band is trimmed with three
> parallel black lines that follow the V: a black zigzag (ric-rac) trim on the outer edge, a thin straight
> black line in the middle, and a second black zigzag trim on the inner edge. Three small round shiny silver
> bead latkans hang at the point of the V.
>
> **Sleeves:** each sleeve hem is finished with a single black zigzag (ric-rac) trim.
>
> **Lotus work:** across the lower front, a garden of black lotus motifs rises from the hem.
> - About nine tall, thin black stems in thick black chain stitch, standing straight up from the hem at
>   different heights.
> - At the top of the stems are lotus buds and open lotus flowers. The tallest stem, near the centre, carries
>   one large open lotus at waist height.
> - Pairs of pointed leaves sprout from the stems.
> - Every flower, bud and leaf is black cutwork lace / net appliqué: a solid black outline filled with an
>   open black mesh, so the ivory fabric shows through the net. This is not a print.
>
> **Pants:** plain black straight-fit pants, ankle length.
>
> Do not change the colours or the motif layout, and do not add extra embroidery, prints, mirrors or
> borders. No text, no logos, no watermark. The model is a young Indian woman with a warm, natural smile,
> soft makeup, a small bindi, shoulder-length soft waves, oxidised silver jhumkas and tan leather juttis.
> 85mm lens, soft natural light, true-to-life colour, the lace appliqué crisp and detailed. Make exactly one
> single photo, not a collage.

2. **Minimal white wall:** Full-length front view against a warm white lime-plaster wall, with a tall ceramic

   Dappled afternoon light. Head to ankle.

7. **Close-up, neckline:** A close-up from chin to waist showing the V-neck band with the black zigzag trims

8. **Close-up, lotus lace appliqué:** A close-up of the lower front of the tunic showing the large open lotus,

10. **Reel/story cover (9:16):** Full-length front view of the model walking towards the camera through a
