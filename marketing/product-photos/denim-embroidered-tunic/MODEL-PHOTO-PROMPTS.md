# Denim embroidered tunic: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** an indigo denim short kurti/tunic with a mandarin collar, three fabric knot buttons, an offset
L-shaped panel seam, a large white paisley-and-leaf embroidery down the front, and embroidered sleeve borders.

## Before you start
1. Upload photos 1–4 of the denim tunic in the same Gemini chat (the close-up shows the collar, buttons and
   embroidery best).
2. Paste the Base instruction, then one shot prompt per message. Ask for 4:5 (posts) or 9:16 (Reels/Stories).
3. If something is off, reply: "Keep the tunic exactly like the reference photos: same indigo denim, mandarin
   collar, three knot buttons, L-shaped seam, white paisley embroidery and embroidered cuffs."
4. Turn on Instagram's AI label if asked.

## Base instruction
> Create a photorealistic professional fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala
> Kari Creations. The model wears the women's denim tunic shown in the reference photos. Reproduce it exactly:
> - **Fabric:** soft, lightweight mid-indigo denim with a natural faded wash.
> - **Neck:** a mandarin (band) collar with a short front placket closed by three round fabric knot buttons.
> - **Front seam:** below the placket, a stitched seam turns left and runs straight down, making an offset
>   L-shaped panel on the front.
> - **Embroidery:** next to that seam, a large vertical motif of white and pale-blue thread embroidery with a
>   thin brown outline: a paisley at the top, curling scrolls and leaves in the middle and three rounded petals
>   near the hem, with a fine mesh (jaali) fill inside the shapes.
> - **Sleeves:** three-quarter sleeves ending in a border of white looped scroll embroidery with brown accents.
> - **Fit and length:** straight, gently A-line, hip length, worn untucked.
> Styling: paired with white or off-white straight cotton pants. Model: a graceful Indian woman in her mid-20s,
> natural makeup, small oxidised silver jhumkas, hair in soft waves. Real fabric texture, true-to-life colour,
> sharp focus on the tunic. No logos, text or watermark.

## Shot 1: Hero, full length
> Full-length front view, standing relaxed with one hand in her pocket. A sunlit whitewashed courtyard with an
> arched wooden door, terracotta pots and a bougainvillea vine. Soft golden-hour light and shallow depth of field.

## Shot 2: Café, casual chic
> Three-quarter view sitting at a marble café table with a cold coffee, by a large window with cane chairs and
> green plants. A relaxed smile. Show the collar, buttons and embroidery clearly. Warm natural light.

## Shot 3: Walking, street style
> Full-length walking shot on a quiet tree-lined street with pastel heritage buildings, holding a jute tote bag
> and wearing white sneakers. Candid, breezy, modern-ethnic look.

## Shot 4: Close-up, detail
> Close-up from the collar to the waist, one hand lightly touching the knot buttons. Sharp detail on the
> mandarin collar, knot buttons, L-shaped seam and the white mesh-filled paisley embroidery. A soft blurred
> beige wall behind.

## Shot 5: Sleeve and embroidery detail
> Mid-shot with the model's arm raised to tuck hair behind her ear, showing the embroidered sleeve border and the
> full front motif. Minimal studio backdrop in warm sand colour, soft diffused light.
