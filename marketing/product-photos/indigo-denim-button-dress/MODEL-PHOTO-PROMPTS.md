# Indigo denim button-front midi dress: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a deep indigo soft denim midi dress (long kurta-dress) with a scoop neck, full-length button front,
curved princess panel seams, side pockets, elbow-length sleeves with button tabs, and back princess seams.

## Before you start
1. Upload all three photos (front close-up, front full, back) in one Gemini chat. Add: "Ignore the hanger and tags."
2. Paste the Base instruction, then one shot prompt per message. Ask for 4:5 (posts) or 9:16 (Reels/Stories).
3. If something is off, reply: "Keep the dress exactly like the reference photos: same deep indigo denim, scoop
   neck, buttons all the way down, curved panel seams, side pockets, sleeve button tabs, midi length."
4. Turn on Instagram's AI label if asked.

## Base instruction
> Create a photorealistic professional fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala Kari
> Creations. The model wears the women's dress shown in the reference photos. Ignore the hanger and tags.
> Reproduce it exactly:
> - **Fabric and colour:** soft, lightweight denim in a deep indigo blue with a subtle natural wash. Plain, no print.
> - **Neckline:** a rounded scoop neck with a clean finish.
> - **Front:** a centre placket buttoned all the way from the neckline to the hem with small grey-silver buttons.
> - **Seams:** curved princess panel seams over the bust and down the body, with tonal topstitching.
> - **Pockets:** two curved in-seam side pockets at the hips.
> - **Sleeves:** elbow-length sleeves with a turned-up cuff held by a small button tab on the outer side.
> - **Back:** plain back with vertical princess seams.
> - **Fit and length:** fitted through the bodice, then a straight, slightly flared skirt to mid-calf (midi).
> Model: a graceful Indian woman in her mid-20s, natural makeup, small silver hoops or studs, a slim watch, hair in
> a sleek low ponytail or soft waves. Real fabric texture, true-to-life colour, sharp focus on the dress. No logos,
> text or watermark.

## Shot 1: Hero, full length
> Full-length front view, one hand in a side pocket, a confident smile. A sunlit modern courtyard with a white
> wall, a tall olive tree in a terracotta pot and a wooden bench. Soft golden-hour light, shallow depth of field.
> White sneakers or tan flats.

## Shot 2: Office chic
> Three-quarter view standing by a large window in a bright minimal workspace with a wooden desk, a laptop and a
> potted plant, holding a coffee mug. Smart, polished work look with a tan leather tote.

## Shot 3: Walking, city street
> Full-length walking shot on a quiet street with pastel heritage buildings and trees, the skirt moving with the
> step, a straw sling bag on her shoulder. Candid and breezy.

## Shot 4: Back view
> The model stands with her back to the camera, looking over her shoulder, against a warm beige arched wall with
> a potted palm. Show the back princess seams, sleeve tabs and full midi length. Soft daylight.

## Shot 5: Close-up, details
> Close-up from the shoulders to the hips, one hand resting in the side pocket. Sharp detail on the scoop neck,
> the buttons, the curved panel seams with topstitching, the pocket and the sleeve button tab. Soft blurred cream
> background.

## Shot 6 (optional): Weekend café
> Seated at an outdoor café table with cane chairs and a cold coffee, legs crossed, sunglasses on the table.
> Relaxed weekend vibe, warm afternoon light.
