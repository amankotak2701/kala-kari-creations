# Black lace-and-zigzag co-ord set: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a black cotton-linen short kurta with an asymmetric frame of cream crochet lace and
black-zigzag woven tape, a band-collar henley placket with pearl buttons, and lace-trimmed
three-quarter sleeves. It comes with matching straight pants that have lace stripes at the
outer leg.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the white-dune photo.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set. This trim layout is what AI tools most often get
   wrong:
   - **Colour:** a deep soft black (slightly cool, matte) with cream/ivory trims. Not bright
     white, not gold.
   - **Two kinds of trim:**
     - **lace:** narrow cream crochet lace with tiny holes and a scalloped edge;
     - **zigzag tape:** a wider cream woven tape with a bold black zigzag (ric-rac) pattern
       through the middle.
   - **The frame on the kurta front is asymmetric:**
     - On the left side as you face the model, three parallel bands (lace, zigzag tape, lace)
       run straight down from the shoulder to the hip.
     - Then they turn the corner and run across the full front at hip level as the same three
       bands.
     - On the right side as you face the model, a single lace strip runs from the shoulder down
       to meet the horizontal band, closing the frame.
   - **Sleeves:** a lace strip runs along each sleeve to the cuff, and each cuff has a zigzag-tape
     band edged with lace.
   - **Pants:** on the outer side of each leg, from just below the knee to the hem, two parallel
     cream lace stripes turn in at the top in a stepped L shape.
   - **Neck:** a short band collar with a V-notch and a short henley placket with three small
     white pearl buttons.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photo: black with
   cream crochet lace and black-zigzag tape in the same asymmetric frame, three bands down the
   left front and across the hip and a single lace strip on the right, lace and zigzag cuffs,
   lace stripes on the pant legs, henley placket with three pearl buttons."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

> - On the left side as you face the model, three parallel bands run straight down from the

> - At hip level the three bands turn a sharp right-angled corner and run horizontally across
>   the entire front of the kurta, in the same order: inner lace on top, zigzag tape in the
>   middle, outer lace below.
> - On the right side as you face the model, a single lace strip runs from the shoulder seam
>   straight down the outer chest to meet the horizontal band, so the inner lace forms a closed
>   rectangle.
> - There is a plain black area inside the frame, with the placket, and plain black below the
>   horizontal band down to the hem.

> to the cuff. Each cuff is finished with a band of zigzag tape edged above with lace.

> leg, from just below the knee to the hem, two parallel cream lace stripes run straight down.

> Do not change the trim placement, make it symmetric, add more lace or swap the zigzag tape for

> watermark.
>
> **Make exactly ONE single photo**: one image, one scene, one pose. Not a collage, grid,
> contact sheet or before/after, and no captions or labels on the image.

## Shot prompts

Your reference already shows a white sand dune at dawn, so these use other light, earthy,
boho-minimal settings. Pale backgrounds make the black and the cream trims pop.

**1 · Santorini-style white terrace (hero)**
> Full-length front view of the model standing on a whitewashed terrace with a blue-domed
> rooftop and the sea far behind her. One hand rests on her hip and the other is relaxed, with a
> soft, confident expression. The full asymmetric trim frame, the pearl buttons, the cuff bands
> and the pant lace stripes are clearly visible against the white walls. Bright, soft daylight.

**2 · Desert camp at golden hour**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking barefoot past a cream canvas bell tent with woven rugs,
> lanterns and floor cushions in a desert camp at golden hour, mid-step and glancing at the
> camera. Warm, low light glows on the sand. Full length visible.

**3 · Boho café with terracotta and cane**
> The model sits on a cane chair at a round wooden table in a sunlit boho café with terracotta
> walls, macramé hangings and potted cacti, holding a cup of coffee and smiling. The kurta's
> trim frame faces the camera. Warm, airy light. Captures her from head to ankle.

**4 · White adobe wall with palm shadows**
> Full-length front view against a smooth white adobe wall, with soft shadows of palm leaves
> falling on the wall beside her, not on the outfit, and a large clay pot with a cactus at one
> side. She tucks her hair behind one ear with a calm half-smile. Clean, warm daylight.
> Minimal, editorial aesthetic.

**5 · Beach at sunset, linen canopy**
> The model stands on a quiet beach beside a wooden canopy draped with sheer white linen that
> moves in the breeze, holding the fabric lightly with one hand. A calm sea and a soft pink-gold
> sunset are behind her. Gentle backlight, true black and cream. Full length visible.

**6 · Jaisalmer fort lane**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking through a narrow golden sandstone lane in Jaisalmer
> fort with carved jharokha balconies above, mid-stride with a soft smile. The warm stone makes
> the black and cream outfit stand out. Afternoon light. Full length visible.

**7 · Minimal studio with pampas and stools**
> Full-length front view in a warm-cream studio with a curved plaster arch, a wooden stool and a
> tall vase of pampas grass. The model sits on the stool with one leg extended, showing the pant
> lace stripes, and looks at the camera. Soft, even studio light. High-fashion look.

**8 · Rooftop at dusk with lanterns**
> The model leans on a white parapet on a city rooftop at dusk with Moroccan lanterns and string
> lights, holding a mocktail and laughing. Warm glowing light, a softly blurred skyline behind
> her. Captures her from head to ankle.

**9 · Detail close-up, neckline and frame**
> A close-up from chin to hip. The band collar with its V-notch, the henley placket with three
> pearl buttons, the three vertical bands (lace, zigzag tape, lace) on the left, the single lace
> strip on the right and the corner where the bands turn across the hip are in crisp focus. The
> crochet holes and the black zigzag are sharp. Soft side light.

**10 · Detail close-up, cuff and pant stripes**
> A close-up of the model's hand resting on her thigh. It shows the sleeve's lace strip and the
> cuff's zigzag-tape band, and below them the pant leg's two lace stripes with their stepped L
> turn. Soft daylight, true colours, crisp trim texture.

**11 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact co-ord set on a warm sand-coloured linen cloth. The kurta is
> laid flat and smoothed, showing the full asymmetric trim frame, the cuff bands and the pearl
> buttons, with the pants folded beside it so the lace leg stripes show. Styled with smooth white
> and black pebbles, a piece of driftwood, a small clay pot and tan leather slides at the edges.
> Even, soft daylight. Catalogue quality.

**12 · Reel/Story cover (9:16, space for text)**
> A vertical 9:16 full-length front view of the model walking barefoot towards the camera along
> a white sandy path between low whitewashed walls, calm and confident. Keep the top third of the
> frame as clean, soft pale sky with no objects, so text can be added later. Soft warm light,
> rich black, crisp cream trims.
