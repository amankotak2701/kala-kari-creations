# Denim floral embroidered top: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a mid-blue washed denim button-front top (short tunic) with a band collar, white floral embroidery on
the yoke, paisley borders along the placket and hem, scattered white flower buttis, a gathered A-line body,
balloon sleeves with elasticated cuffs, and a plain back with a box pleat.

## Before you start
1. Upload all four photos in one Gemini chat. Add: "Ignore the hanger, tags and the hand holding the sleeve."
2. Paste the Base instruction, then one shot prompt per message. Ask for 4:5 (posts) or 9:16 (Reels/Stories).
3. If something is off, reply: "Keep the top exactly like the reference photos: same washed blue denim, band
   collar, white floral yoke embroidery, paisley borders on the placket and hem, scattered flower motifs, gathered
   body, balloon sleeves with elastic cuffs."
4. Turn on Instagram's AI label if asked.

## Base instruction
> Create a photorealistic professional fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala Kari
> Creations. The model wears the women's denim top shown in the reference photos. Ignore the hanger, tags and the
> hand holding the sleeve. Reproduce the top exactly:
> - **Fabric and colour:** soft lightweight denim in a mid-blue, slightly faded wash.
> - **Collar and front:** a band (mandarin) collar and a centre placket with small white round buttons.
> - **Yoke:** a straight front yoke covered with white thread embroidery: daisies, small stars, leafy sprigs and
>   dots, on both sides of the placket.
> - **Placket border:** a narrow vertical border of white paisley-chain embroidery running down both sides of the
>   button placket.
> - **Body:** soft gathers below the yoke, flaring into a relaxed A-line, hip-length body. Scattered small white
>   embroidered flower buttis across the front and the sleeves.
> - **Hem:** two rows of white wavy paisley-vine embroidery near the hem.
> - **Sleeves:** full balloon sleeves gathered into elasticated cuffs, with a white flower motif near each cuff.
> - **Back:** plain, with a back yoke seam and a centre box pleat.
> Styling: paired with white straight-leg cotton pants. Model: a graceful Indian woman in her mid-20s, natural
> makeup, small oxidised silver jhumkas, hair in soft loose waves. Real fabric texture, true-to-life colour, sharp
> focus on the top. No logos, text or watermark.

## Shot 1: Hero, full length
> Full-length front view, hands lightly in her pant pockets, a soft smile. A sunlit whitewashed courtyard with an
> arched blue wooden door, terracotta pots and a bougainvillea vine. Golden-hour light, shallow depth of field.

## Shot 2: Café, boho chic
> Three-quarter view seated at a rustic wooden café table with a cold coffee, cane chairs, hanging plants and warm
> fairy lights. The yoke embroidery and balloon sleeves clearly visible.

## Shot 3: Walking, breezy street style
> Full-length walking shot along a quiet tree-lined lane with pastel heritage houses, a straw tote on her
> shoulder, white sneakers. The gathered body and sleeves moving naturally.

## Shot 4: Close-up, yoke embroidery
> Close-up from the collar to the waist, one hand touching the placket. Sharp detail on the band collar, the white
> floral yoke embroidery, the paisley placket borders and the buttons. Soft blurred cream background.

## Shot 5: Back view
> The model stands with her back to the camera, looking over her shoulder, against a warm peach wall with a potted
> palm. Show the back yoke, the box pleat and the balloon sleeves with flower motifs. Soft daylight.

## Shot 6 (optional): Sleeve detail
> Mid-shot with one arm raised to adjust her hair, showing the balloon sleeve, the elastic cuff with its flower
> motif and the hem borders. Minimal sand-coloured studio backdrop, soft diffused light.
