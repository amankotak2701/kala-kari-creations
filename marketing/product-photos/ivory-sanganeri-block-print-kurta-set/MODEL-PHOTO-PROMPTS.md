# Ivory Sanganeri block-print kurta set: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a warm-ivory cotton A-line kurta, hand block-printed in the Sanganeri style:
- Mughal flower bouquets and cypress-tree motifs in magenta-pink and olive on the front;
- printed side panels;
- a hand-stitched pink-and-cream notched neckline;
- three-quarter sleeves with pink stitched cuffs.

It comes with ivory straight pants.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photos:** upload **both** photos in the same chat. The full-length photo shows the
   side panels and length; the half-length photo shows the neckline and motifs up close. Crop off
   the blurred block at the top right of the half-length photo first, or the AI may copy it.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real kurta. These are the details AI tools most often get wrong:
   - **Colour:** a warm ivory base (about #E8DCD0), like unbleached cotton. Not bright white,
     not beige-brown.
   - **Two motifs on the front, never mixed up:**
     - The **bouquet**: one large magenta-pink flower with a darker spiral centre, two small pink
       buds and olive leaves on a short stem.
     - The **cypress tree**: a tall olive-khaki fern-like spray with small pink five-petal flowers
       along both sides and a pink bud at the tip.
     - They alternate in a staggered (half-drop) layout, well spaced on the ivory. Not an all-over
       print.
   - **Side panels:** a long panel down each side, from underarm to hem, densely printed with
     small olive-khaki leaf sprigs and tiny dusty-pink buds in neat rows. The front centre stays
     open ivory.
   - **Neckline:** a round neck with a deep V notch, edged with a narrow cream binding with tiny
     olive slanted stitches and an outer line of fine magenta-pink hand stitching.
   - **Sleeves:** three-quarter length, each with one cypress motif near the cuff and one bouquet
     on the upper arm. The cuff hem has a narrow cream band edged with a fine magenta-pink
     sawtooth stitch line.
   - **Shape:** an A-line kurta reaching just below the knee with a plain straight hem. Ivory
     straight pants.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the kurta exactly like the reference photos: same warm
   ivory, same magenta-and-olive bouquet and cypress motifs in a spaced staggered layout, same
   printed side panels, same pink-stitched notched neckline and cuffs. Do not add more motifs or
   change the colours."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

these use other aesthetic settings. Soft greens, pinks and warm light suit the ivory and magenta.
