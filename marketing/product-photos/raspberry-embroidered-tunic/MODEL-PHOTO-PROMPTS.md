# Raspberry embroidered cotton tunic: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a raspberry-red cotton short kurti with a deep V-neck framed by white floral borders with small
mirrors, a pintucked inset, a square embroidered patch with three seashell latkans, an embroidered band collar at
the back, a matching square patch on the back, and white printed borders on the sleeves and side slits.

## Before you start
1. Upload all 4 photos (front, front close-up, back, back close-up) in the same Gemini chat. The hanger has
   another brand's name on it, so add: "Ignore the hanger and its text."
2. Paste the Base instruction, then one shot prompt per message. Ask for 4:5 (posts) or 9:16 (Reels/Stories).
3. If something is off, reply: "Keep the tunic exactly like the reference photos: same raspberry red, V-neck
   borders with mirrors, pintucked inset, square patch with three seashell latkans, back collar border and back
   patch, white sleeve and slit borders."
4. Turn on Instagram's AI label if asked.

## Base instruction
> Create a photorealistic professional fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala
> Kari Creations. The model wears the women's tunic shown in the reference photos. Ignore the hanger and its text.
> Reproduce the tunic exactly:
> - **Fabric and colour:** soft matte cotton in a rich raspberry red (deep pinkish red).
> - **Neckline:** a deep V-neck edged on both sides with a wide hand-embroidered border: white four-petal
>   flowers, small diamond mirrors (shisha) and red and orange thread accents.
> - **Inset:** inside the V, a red panel with fine horizontal pintucks and a column of tiny white stitches and
>   mirror dots down the centre.
> - **Front patch:** at the point of the V, a square embroidered patch with a white four-petal flower medallion,
>   red and gold thread accents and a scalloped white frame. Three white seashell latkans on short threads with
>   small mother-of-pearl discs hang below it.
> - **Back:** a narrow band collar with the same white floral and mirror embroidered border, and a matching
>   smaller square embroidered patch in the upper middle of the back.
> - **Shoulders:** a few fine vertical pintucks on each side of the front.
> - **Sleeves:** three-quarter sleeves with a small slit edged in a white printed floral-and-scroll border, and a
>   tiny seashell charm at the slit.
> - **Sides and length:** hip length with side slits edged in the same white border. Relaxed straight fit.
> Styling: paired with off-white or white cotton palazzo pants. Model: a graceful Indian woman in her mid-20s,
> natural makeup, a small bindi, oxidised silver jhumkas and a few thin bangles, hair in a soft low bun.
> Real fabric texture, true-to-life colour, sharp focus on the tunic. No logos, text or watermark.
