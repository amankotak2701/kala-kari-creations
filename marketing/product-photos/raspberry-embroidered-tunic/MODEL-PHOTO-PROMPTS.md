# Raspberry embroidered cotton tunic: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a raspberry-red cotton short kurti/tunic with a deep V-neck framed by white floral embroidered
borders, a pintucked inset, a square embroidered patch with three dangling charms (latkans) and bordered sleeves.

## Before you start
1. Upload the photo of the red tunic in Gemini.
2. Paste the Base instruction, then one shot prompt per message. Ask for 4:5 (posts) or 9:16 (Reels/Stories).
3. If something is off, reply: "Keep the tunic exactly like the reference photo: same raspberry red, V-neck with
   white floral borders, square patch with three charms, pintucks and bordered sleeves."
4. Turn on Instagram's AI label if asked.

## Base instruction
> Create a photorealistic professional fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala
> Kari Creations. The model wears the women's tunic shown in the reference photo. Reproduce it exactly:
> - **Fabric and colour:** soft matte cotton in a rich raspberry red (deep pinkish red).
> - **Neckline:** a deep V-neck edged on both sides with a wide white embroidered border of small four-petal
>   flowers.
> - **Inset:** inside the V, a red inset panel with fine vertical pintucks and tiny white stitch details.
> - **Patch:** at the point of the V, a square embroidered patch with a white floral medallion on red, with three
>   small silver-and-shell drop charms (latkans) hanging below it.
> - **Shoulders:** a few fine pintucks on each shoulder.
> - **Sleeves:** three-quarter sleeves with a small slit and a white embroidered floral border at the hem.
> - **Sides and length:** hip length with side slits edged in the same white border. Relaxed straight fit.
> Styling: paired with off-white or white cotton palazzo pants. Model: a graceful Indian woman in her mid-20s,
> natural makeup, a small bindi, oxidised silver jhumkas and a stack of thin bangles, hair in a soft low bun.
> Real fabric texture, true-to-life colour, sharp focus on the tunic. No logos, text or watermark.
