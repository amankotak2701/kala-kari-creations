# Grey chambray cotton midi dress: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a women's soft grey-blue chambray (yarn-dyed cotton) A-line midi dress with a V-neck, short
cap-style sleeves, princess seams at the front, and a gathered back waist with a smocked band and a button
keyhole at the back neck.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photos:** upload **all three** photos in the same chat.
   - The front photo, close up, shows the V-neck and seams. Treat it as the main reference.
   - The back photo shows the smocked waist band and the keyhole button.
   - The full-length photo shows the length and the A-line flare.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate message (or a new
   chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real dress: a plain grey-blue chambray with no print, a V-neck, short
   sleeves, a fitted bodice that flares into an A-line skirt, and a midi length. If anything is off, reply
   with: "Keep the dress exactly like the reference photos: same grey-blue chambray, same V-neck, same short
   sleeves, same seams, same length. No print, no embroidery."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Base instruction (paste before every shot prompt)

> Create a photorealistic professional fashion catalogue photo for the Indian handcrafted clothing brand Kala
> Kari Creations. The model wears the women's dress shown in the reference photos. Reproduce it exactly:
> - **Fabric:** soft, breathable chambray cotton in a muted grey-blue with a fine heathered (slub) texture.
>   Plain, with no print or embroidery. Natural, lightly pressed look.
> - **Neckline:** a clean V-neck at the front with a narrow facing.
> - **Sleeves:** short, slightly dropped cap-style sleeves with a turned hem, ending above the elbow, and a
>   visible seam running from the shoulder towards the neck (yoke seam).
> - **Bodice:** gently fitted with vertical princess seams/darts at the front.
> - **Back:** a round back neck with a small keyhole opening and one button at the top, and an elasticated,
>   smocked waistband across the back with soft gathers above and below it.
> - **Skirt and length:** flares into a relaxed A-line skirt that falls to mid-calf (midi), with a soft,
>   fluid drape.
>
> Model: a graceful Indian woman in her mid-20s with a natural, warm look, soft makeup, small silver or oxidised
> jewellery, and hair styled softly. Real fabric folds, true-to-life colour, sharp focus on the dress, natural
> skin texture. No logos, no text, no watermark on the image.

> The model stands with her back to the camera, looking over her shoulder with a soft smile, in front of a

## Shot 5: Close-up, fabric and neckline

> Close-up from the shoulders to the waist. The model's hands rest lightly at the waist. Sharp detail on the

## Shot 6 (optional): Styled with a stole for Diwali / office
