# Mustard folk-embroidery co-ord set: photoshoot prompts

**Product:** a long mustard cotton kurta with a full button placket and cream thread embroidery
of a Rajasthani folk horse and a lady with a peacock, with matching mustard pants.

Use these prompts in an AI image tool that accepts a reference photo, such as Gemini or ChatGPT.

## Before you start

1. **Reference photo:** upload the full-length haveli photo of the model in this set (front view,
   hand at her jhumka). It shows the collar, buttons, both embroideries, cuffs and pants clearly,
   so it's the best reference. For the flat-lay (Shot 5) you can also add your original product
   photo.
2. Paste the **Base instruction**, then **one** shot prompt. Send each shot as a separate
   message (or a new chat) so every shot comes back as its own image file.
3. Check the result against the real set:
   - a mandarin collar and buttons from neck to hem,
   - the horse embroidery on the upper chest and the lady-with-peacock embroidery on the lower
     front, on opposite sides,
   - embroidered cuffs,
   - matching mustard pants.

   If anything is off, reply with: "Keep the outfit exactly like the reference photo: same
   embroidery placement, same buttons, same colour."
4. Ask for 4:5 for Instagram/Shopify posts, or 9:16 for Reels/Stories.

**Colour note:** the set is a solid mustard-ochre (the haveli photo confirms there's no ombré).
If a result comes back too yellow or too brown, reply: "Match the exact mustard-ochre colour of
the reference photo."

Instagram may ask you to label posts as AI-generated. Turn the label on if it does.

## Shot prompts

**1 · Hero, full-length front**
> Full-length front view, showing the model from head to toe. She stands relaxed with a warm
> smile, one hand lightly touching her jhumka, so both embroidered motifs and the full button
> placket are clearly visible. She wears tan leather juttis. Setting: a sandstone Rajasthani
> haveli courtyard with a carved jharokha window behind her and a few potted plants. Warm
> late-afternoon sunlight that brings out the mustard.

**2 · Walking, three-quarter view**
> Three-quarter view of the model walking slowly past an old blue-washed wall in a Jodhpur-style
> lane, mid-step, so the kurta's front slit opens slightly and the wide-leg pants swing. The
> mustard looks rich against the blue wall. She glances at the camera with a soft smile.
> Golden-hour light. Full length visible.

**3 · Seated lifestyle**
> The model sits sideways on a jute charpai in a sunlit courtyard, holding a brass glass of chai
> and laughing naturally. The peacock embroidery on the lower front falls across her lap and
> faces the camera. Around her: a terracotta matka, a woven basket and bougainvillea in the
> background. Warm, cheerful morning light. Captures her from head to ankle.

**4 · Embroidery close-up**
> A close-up crop from shoulder to mid-thigh, with the model standing at a slight angle and one
> hand resting by her side to show the embroidered cuff. The horse motif on the chest and the
> top of the lady-and-peacock motif are both in frame, so the cream thread stitches, the wooden
> buttons and the cotton texture are crisp and detailed. Soft window light from the side.
> Shallow depth of field.

**5 · Styled flat-lay (no model)**
> A top-down flat-lay of this exact co-ord set on an off-white handloom cotton cloth. The kurta
> is laid flat and smoothed with its buttons centred and both embroideries fully visible, and the
> mustard pants are folded neatly beside it. Styled with oxidised silver jhumkas, a few marigold
> flowers, a small brass bowl and a pair of tan juttis at the edge. Even, soft daylight. No
> wrinkles, no tags. Catalogue quality.
