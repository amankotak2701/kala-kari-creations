# "What we cooked / How it looked" reel: images to make

## How the reel works

The screen is split in two:
- **Top half, "WHAT WE COOKED":** the kurta neatly folded on a white dinner plate, like a dish, on a dining
  table.
- **Bottom half, "HOW IT LOOKED":** a model wearing that same outfit.

It shows **5 products**, one after another (about 1.7 s each). It ends on a festive card and then the logo
card with the website.

## What to send me

**5 products × 2 images = 10 images.** For each product:
1. one **plate** image (prompt A);
2. one **look** image (prompt B).

Make both images **square (1:1)**. I crop them slightly to fit each half of the screen.

**Pick 5 products in clearly different colours**, as the reference does (red, sage, plum, peach, black), so
each plate looks like a new "dish". Products with strong embroidery on the front look best folded.

Use Gemini, uploading your product photo as the reference. Paste prompt A or B, then add a short description
of your product's colour and work. You can copy that description from the product's MODEL-PHOTO-PROMPTS.md
file.

## Prompt A: "What we cooked" (plate)

> Create a photorealistic top-down (flat-lay, camera directly overhead) food-styling photo, but instead of food,
> the "dish" is the exact kurta from the reference photo. Reproduce the kurta's colour, fabric and embroidery
> exactly.
>
> The kurta is neatly folded into a rectangle, like a shirt in a shop, with the neckline and the main
> embroidered yoke facing up and centred. It lies on a large plain white round ceramic dinner plate in the
> middle of the frame. The folded kurta is slightly wider than the plate's inner circle and overlaps its rim
> a little at the top and bottom.
>
> Setting: a dining table covered with a soft, plain, warm-beige linen tablecloth. Around the plate:
> - to the right, a folded cream cloth napkin with a thin edge piping in the kurta's main colour, and a small
>   embroidered leaf in the same colour in its lower corner, with a vintage silver knife and fork on top;
> - at the top, a silver spoon lying horizontally above the plate;
> - at the top left, a small white scalloped bowl of salt with a tiny silver spoon, its rim edged in the
>   kurta's colour;
> - at the top right, a small white salt shaker.
>
> Soft, even, natural daylight from the top, with gentle shadows. Calm, minimal, warm-neutral colours, so the
> kurta is the hero. No people, no hands, no text, no logos, no watermark. Square 1:1.

## Prompt B: "How it looked" (model)

> Create a photorealistic fashion photo for the Indian clothing brand Kala Kari Creations. The model wears the
> exact outfit from the reference photo (kurta, pants and dupatta if any). Reproduce its colour and work
> exactly.
>
> A young Indian woman stands full length in the centre of the frame, from head to feet, with space around
> her. She has a calm, elegant expression, hair in a low bun or soft braid, and small earrings. She stands
> relaxed, one hand loosely at her side or lightly touching a piece of furniture.
>
> Setting: a warm, sunlit Indian haveli interior or courtyard in soft terracotta-pink and peach tones, with an
> old wooden door, a carved arch or stone steps, a few potted plants, and a terracotta or patterned floor.
> Soft, warm natural window light with gentle shadows. Editorial, minimal and calm. The model fills about
> two-thirds of the frame's height. No text, no logos, no watermark. Square 1:1.

(Change the setting slightly for each product, for example a wooden door, a carved arch, stone steps or a
window with plants, so the five looks aren't identical, just as in the reference.)
