garment, so they stay exactly as they are. You only need to make **3 short video clips and 1 photo**. I then

## How to make them

- **Video clips A, B, C:** use a text-to-video or image-to-video AI tool, for example Veo in the Gemini app,
  Kling or Runway.
  - Make each clip **vertical 9:16**, at least **3 seconds** long. I cut the best second from each.
  - Generate 2–3 tries of each and send me the best ones.
  - If your tool accepts a reference image, upload your ivory kurta photo, so the print matches.
- **Photo D:** use Gemini image generation with your ivory kurta photo uploaded as the reference.
- Turn on Instagram's AI label when you post.

Add this **style line to every prompt**, so the clips match the reference's look:

> Cinematic, moody tailoring-studio footage. Muted teal-grey and cream colour grade, soft window light,
> shallow depth of field, 50mm macro lens, slow and steady camera, realistic, no text, no logos, no
> watermark. Vertical 9:16.
