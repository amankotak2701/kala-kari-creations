# Meta ad creatives: 9 ads (5 images, 4 videos)

Each ad comes in 3 sizes. When you create the ad, upload all three and Meta will use the right one
for each placement.

| File suffix | Size | Use it for |
|---|---|---|
| `-1x1` | 1080×1080 | Facebook right column, Marketplace, Search results, feed |
| `-4x5` | 1080×1350 | Facebook & Instagram feed (best for mobile feed) |
| `-9x16` | 1080×1920 | Facebook & Instagram Stories and Reels, WhatsApp Status |

In Ads Manager, open the ad and go to **Media → Select images/videos**. Add the 4:5 file first,
then use **Edit → Customise by placement** and pick the 1:1 file for the right column and
Marketplace, and the 9:16 file for Stories and Reels.

All videos are 10–12 seconds long with soft original background music (no copyright issues). They are
H.264 MP4 and well under Meta's and WhatsApp's size limits. Images are high-quality JPG.

Every creative shows **KALAKARICREATIONS.COM** in the maroon band at the bottom, with the Call / WhatsApp
number under it. The video end cards repeat the website in large text. Meta's own **Shop Now** button
still appears under the ad and links straight to the page you set.

**Campaign settings to use:** Objective: *Sales* or *Traffic* → Website: `https://kalakaricreations.com`
(product pages below) · Call to action: **Shop Now**.

### Tips
- Run ads 1, 2 and 7 as a **carousel** or in one ad set to test which one gets the cheapest clicks.
- The pants (₹500) are your best hook for new customers. Retarget people who viewed them with the
  tunic and co-ord ads.
- Turn on **Advantage+ creative** enhancements only for brightness and contrast. Leave "add music"
  and "text improvements" off so your prices and copy stay exactly as written.
- Re-render after a price change: edit the `ADS` dict in `make_ads.py` and run
  `python3 make_ads.py <images> <fonts> <cutouts> out [ad_id ...]`.
