Caring for your
handcrafted piece

✦

Hand embroidery, hand-block prints and natural dyes are delicate. A little care keeps them beautiful for years.

First wash

Dry clean, or a gentle hand wash in cold water.

Mild detergent

Use a mild liquid detergent. No bleach, no harsh soaps.

Wash separately

Natural dyes may release a little colour in the first few washes.

Never wring

Gently press out water. Twisting can pull embroidery threads.

Dry in shade

Direct sun fades natural colours. Dry flat or on a hanger indoors.

Iron inside out

Medium heat, on the reverse side, especially over embroidery.

Small variations in colour, print or stitch are the signature of the maker, not a defect. That's what makes your piece one of a kind.
